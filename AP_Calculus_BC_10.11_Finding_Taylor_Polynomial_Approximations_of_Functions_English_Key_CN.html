Core lesson

The Taylor Polynomial Formula

泰勒多项式公式

Pn(x)=Σk=0n f(k)(a)/k! · (x−a)k

The center is x=a. A Maclaurin polynomial uses a=0.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1003

Write the k=0 term as f(a). This avoids treating 0! or a zeroth derivative as mysterious exceptions.

Core lesson

Coefficients Encode Derivatives

系数记录导数值

The coefficient of (x−a)k is f(k)(a)/k!.

f(k)(a)=k! · [coefficient of (x−a)k]

从系数还原导数时要乘 k!。

Derivative Matching at the Center

在中心匹配导数

At x=a, the polynomial satisfies

Pn(k)(a)=f(k)(a), 0≤k≤n.

The graph matches value, slope, and higher-order behavior through degree n.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1005

Higher degree does not mean global accuracy. The matching statement is local at the center.

The First-Degree Case

一次情形就是切线近似

P1(x)=f(a)+f′(a)(x−a)

This is the tangent line at x=a. Higher-degree Taylor polynomials add curvature and further derivative information.

Taylor Example: ln x at 1

以 1 为中心近似 ln x

For f(x)=ln x centered at a=1,

P3(x)=(x−1)−(x−1)²/2+(x−1)³/3.

ln(1.1)≈0.1−0.1²/2+0.1³/3=0.095333…

Building from a Derivative Table

由导数表构造多项式

If f(2)=−1, f′(2)=3, f″(2)=4, f‴(2)=−6, then

P3(x)=−1+3(x−2)+2(x−2)²−(x−2)³.

Core lesson

Recovering a Derivative

从多项式还原导数

Let P4(x)=3−2x²+5x⁴ be centered at 0.

The coefficient of x⁴ is f(4)(0)/4!=5.

f(4)(0)=5·4!=120.

Core lesson

Distance from the Center

离中心越远通常误差越大

The approximation usually works best near x=a.

The factor (x−a)k grows as the evaluation point moves away from the center.

代入前先计算 x−a。

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1011

Do not promise monotonic improvement everywhere. State this as a local approximation principle.

Evaluation After Construction

构造后代入求值

Keep the polynomial in powers of x−a, then substitute the target value.

If the target is a+h, every power becomes a power of the small number h.

Common Errors

常见错误

  • Using xk when the center is not zero
  • Forgetting the factorial denominator
  • Reading a coefficient directly as a derivative value
  • Calling Pn(x) the exact function

Quick Check

课堂检测

Given f(1)=2, f′(1)=−1, f″(1)=6, find P2(1.2).

Answer: P2(x)=2−(x−1)+3(x−1)², so P2(1.2)=2−0.2+3(0.04)=1.92.
