Directions: Differentiate the proposed function and compare both sides on its domain.
要求：对候选函数求导并代回；按要求检查初始条件。

Fast check: A solution must satisfy the equation identically, not at only one point.

1. Verify that y=3e2x solves y′=2y.3 points
Derivative and substitution / 求导与代入：
Answer: y′=6e2x=2(3e2x)=2y, so it is a solution for all real x.
2. Does y=x²+1 solve y′=2x with y(0)=1?3 points
Equation and condition checks / 方程与初值：
Answer: Yes. y′=2x for all x, and y(0)=1.
3. Find k so y=ke−3x+8sin(2x) solves y″+4y=26e−3x.4 points
Second derivative and coefficients / 二阶导与系数：
Answer: Substitution gives y″+4y=13ke−3x. Thus 13k=26 and k=2.
