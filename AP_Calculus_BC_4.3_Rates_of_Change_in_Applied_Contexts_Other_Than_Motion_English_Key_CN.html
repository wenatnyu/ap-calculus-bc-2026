An Exponential Rate Model

指数速率模型

R(t)=cekt million gallons/year

R(0)=2 gives c=2. Doubling every 9 years gives e9k=2.

k=ln 29≈0.077

R′ would measure how the consumption rate changes, in million gallons/year².

Net Population in a Building

f(t) = entering rate; g(t) = exiting rate.

N′(t)=f(t)−g(t)

N″(t)=f′(t)−g′(t)

The rate of change of the number is decreasing when f′(t)−g′(t)<0.正确选项是 D。

AP Check

AP 选择题检查

Q(t) is measured in liters/minute. What does Q′(6)=4 mean?

  • A.There are 4 liters.
  • B.The flow rate is 4 L/min.
  • C.The flow rate is increasing at 4 L/min².
  • D.The volume is increasing at 4 L/min².
C. Q is already a rate, so Q′ is the change in that rate.原函数已经是流速。

Exit Ticket

离堂检测

A model gives B(t)=50(1−e−0.7t²), where B is measured in mph and t in hours. Find and interpret B′(2).

Answer: B′(t)=70te−0.7t², so B′(2)=140e−2.8≈8.513 mph/hour. At hour 2, speed is increasing at about 8.513 mph per hour.第2小时时，速率约每小时增加8.513 mph。
