Definition / 定义

2. A series has SN=5-2/(N+1). Determine convergence and the sum.3 points
Partial-sum limit / 部分和极限
Answer: SN→5, so the series converges and its sum is 5.
3. Evaluate Σn=1∞2/[n(n+1)] using partial sums.4 points
Telescoping / 裂项相消
Answer: 2/[n(n+1)]=2/n-2/(n+1), so SN=2-2/(N+1)→2. The sum is 2.
