From Small Chords to an Integral

从小弦长到积分

Over a short time, distance is approximately √[(Δx)²+(Δy)²]. Passing to a limit gives the integral of speed.

Core lesson

Parametric Arc-Length Formula

参数弧长公式

L=∫ab√([x′(t)]²+[y′(t)]²) dt.

Assume the parametrization is smooth or piecewise smooth on [a,b].

Core lesson

Why This Is Speed

为什么积分的是速率

For position r(t)=⟨x(t),y(t)⟩, velocity is r′(t) and speed is ||r′(t)||=√(x′²+y′²).

A Straight-Line Check

用直线检验公式

For x=3t, y=4t, 0≤t≤2, speed is 5, so L=∫₀²5dt=10.

结果应与两端点距离一致。

Repeated Tracing Counts

重复经过会重复计长

The integral measures distance traveled over the parameter interval. If the curve is traced twice, its geometric path length is counted twice.

Quick check · Reveal

Reveal: Exact Length

揭晓：精确弧长

Try it. Find the length of x=2t, y=t² for 0≤t≤1.

Answer. L=∫₀¹√(4+4t²)dt=2∫₀¹√(1+t²)dt=√2+ln(1+√2).

Exit ticket

Reveal: Tracing

揭晓：重复描迹

Try it. For x=cos t, y=sin t on 0≤t≤4π, what does the arc-length integral equal?

Answer. ∫₀4π1dt=4π. The unit circle is traced twice, so the distance traveled is twice its circumference.
