Directions: Work without a calculator. Show one clear reason for each answer.
要求：不使用计算器；每题写出一个清楚的理由。

Fast check: look for four habits: compare one-sided limits, simplify before substituting, state both squeeze bounds, and use the indicated side.

1. Given limx→2⁻ f(x)=3, limx→2⁺ f(x)=−1, and f(2)=7, find limx→2 f(x).2 points
Conclusion / 结论：
Answer: DNE. The one-sided limits are different, 3≠−1. The value f(2)=7 does not decide the limit. 左右极限不相等，所以双侧极限不存在；点值不影响结论。
2. Evaluate limx→4 x²−16x−4.3 points
Algebra / 代数过程：
Answer: 8. For x≠4, (x²−16)/(x−4)=(x−4)(x+4)/(x−4)=x+4; therefore the nearby values approach 8. 先因式分解并约去造成 0/0 的因子，再代入。
3. Near x=0, suppose −x²≤g(x)≤x². Find limx→0g(x) and name the theorem.2 points
Outside limits / 上下界极限：
Answer: 0, by the Squeeze Theorem. Both outside functions approach 0: limx→0(−x²)=0=limx→0x². 上下界极限同为 0，因此中间函数也趋近 0。
4. Evaluate the one-sided limit limx→3⁻ |x−3|x−3. Explain the sign.3 points
Sign and value / 符号与结果：
Answer: −1. To the left of 3, x−3<0, so |x−3|=−(x−3) and the quotient equals −1. 从左侧靠近时 x−3 为负，因此商恒为 −1。
