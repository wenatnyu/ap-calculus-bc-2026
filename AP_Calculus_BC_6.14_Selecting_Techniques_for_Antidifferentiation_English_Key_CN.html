Choose u-Substitution

何时换元

composite function plus inner derivative

example: ∫2x/(x²+5)dx

=ln(x²+5)+C

Look for a complete inner derivative up to a constant factor.

Choose Integration by Parts

何时分部积分

product where one factor simplifies when differentiated

example: ∫xeˣdx=eˣ(x−1)+C

Also consider parts for ln x or inverse-trig functions standing alone.

Topic 6.14 · Core lesson

Choose Long Division

何时长除

rational function with numerator degree ≥ denominator degree

(x²+4)/(x+1)=x−1+5/(x+1)

Divide first, then integrate the polynomial and remainder.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 607

Resulting integral is x squared over 2 minus x plus 5 ln absolute x plus 1.

Choose Completing the Square

何时配方

quadratic denominator resists factoring over the reals

x²+4x+8=(x+2)²+4

integral of reciprocal = (1/2)tan⁻¹((x+2)/2)+C

Look for inverse-tangent or inverse-sine templates.

Mixed Strategy

组合方法

∫(2x+7)/(x²+6x+13)dx

split numerator as (2x+6)+1

use u-sub for one part and completing the square for the other

One integral may require more than one tool.

Exit Ticket

离堂检测

Choose a method and evaluate ∫(x²+4)/(x+1)dx.

Answer: Use long division: (x²+4)/(x+1)=x−1+5/(x+1). The integral is x²/2−x+5ln|x+1|+C.分子次数不低于分母，先做长除法。
