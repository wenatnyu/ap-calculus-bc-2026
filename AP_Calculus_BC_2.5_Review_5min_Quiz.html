Directions: Rewrite roots and reciprocals as powers before differentiating. Use exact values.
要求：根式、分式先改写成幂，再求导；保留精确值。

Fast check: For a constant exponent n, d(xⁿ)/dx=nxⁿ⁻¹. Multiply by the old exponent; subtract 1 from it.

1. Differentiate each function: (a) y=x⁷; (b) y=x⁻⁴; (c) y=x2/3.4 points
Power rule / 幂法则：
Answer: (a) y′=7x⁶; (b) y′=−4x⁻⁵=−4/x⁵; (c) y′=(2/3)x−1/3=2/(3∛x) where the derivative is defined.
2. Let f(x)=√x. Find f′(16).3 points
Rewrite → differentiate → evaluate / 改写、求导、代入：
Answer: f(x)=x1/2, so f′(x)=(1/2)x−1/2=1/(2√x). Thus f′(16)=1/8.
3. Write an equation of the tangent line to y=x³ at x=−2.3 points
Point + derivative + line / 点、导数、切线：
Answer: y(−2)=−8 and y′=3x², so the slope is 12. The tangent line is y+8=12(x+2).
