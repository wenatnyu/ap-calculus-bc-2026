1. State the difference between absolute convergence and conditional convergence.3 points
Definitions / 定义
Answer: Σan converges absolutely if Σ|an| converges. It converges conditionally if Σan converges but Σ|an| diverges.
2. Classify Σn=1∞(−1)n+1/n.3 points
Test and classification / 判别与分类
Answer: Conditionally convergent. The alternating harmonic series converges by the Alternating Series Test, while Σ1/n diverges.
3. Classify Σn=1∞(−1)n/n² and justify.4 points
Comparison / 比较
Answer: Absolutely convergent because Σ| (−1)n/n² |=Σ1/n² is a convergent p-series with p=2.
