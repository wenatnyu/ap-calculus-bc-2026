Verifying Solutions for Differential Equations

验证微分方程的解

The Verification Workflow

验证流程

1

Copy the candidate and its domain.

2

Compute every derivative required.

3

Substitute into the differential equation.

4

Check each initial or boundary condition.

The equality must hold for every x in the stated interval.必须在整个指定区间恒成立。

A Family That Works

一族解

y=Ce2x   and   y′=2Ce2x

y′=2Ce2x=2y

Every constant C gives a solution of y′=2y.通解中的常数对应不同解曲线。

Core lesson

One Point Is Not Enough

只在一点成立不够

Candidate

y=x²

y′=2x

Equation

y′=2y

Would require 2x=2x² for all x.

The equality holds only at x=0,1, so y=x² is not a solution on an interval.个别点相等不能验证函数解。

Check the Initial Condition

检查初始条件

y=Ce−x,   y′=−y

If the curve passes through (0,3), then 3=Ce⁰, so C=3.

y=3e−x

微分方程筛出解族，初始条件选出一条曲线。

Recover a Particular Solution

由导数找特解

y′=4x+2,   y(−1)=3

y=2x²+2x+C

3=C

y=2x²+2x+3. Differentiate once to verify and substitute x=−1 to check the point.求出后再做两步回查。

Core lesson

Higher-Order Equations

高阶微分方程

y″−5y′+4y=0

A candidate must be differentiated twice. For y=eˣ:

y′=eˣ,   y″=eˣ

eˣ−5eˣ+4eˣ=0

方程出现几阶导数，就计算到几阶。

Determine an Unknown Parameter

由方程确定参数

y=ke−3x+8sin(2x)

y″+4y=26e−3x

y″+4y=13ke−3x

13k=26, so k=2.同类函数项的系数必须相等。

Core lesson

Equilibrium Solutions

平衡解

y′=(y−4)³ sin(πx/2)

For the horizontal function y=4, the left side is 0 and the right side is also 0.

y=4 is an equilibrium solution.常数解使变化率始终为零。

Domain Still Matters

定义域仍然重要

Equation

y′=1/x

Candidate

y=ln|x|+C

The candidate works on any interval that does not cross x=0.

A formula can solve an equation on separate intervals without being defined between them.解必须连同适用区间一起理解。

Equation Check vs. Condition Check

方程检查与条件检查

QuestionHow to check

Does it solve the IVP?Also test the initial condition.
Is the formula valid there?Check its domain and denominators.

Verify a First-Order IVP

验证一阶初值问题

Does y=e−x solve y′+y=0 with y(0)=1?

Answer: Yes. y′=−e−x, so y′+y=0, and y(0)=1.方程与初值都满足。

Verify a Second-Order IVP

验证二阶初值问题

Verify y=2cos x+sin x for y″+y=0, y(0)=2, y′(0)=1.

Answer: y′=−2sin x+cos x and y″=−2cos x−sin x=−y. Both initial conditions also hold.二阶方程需要两个给定条件时，逐个检查。
