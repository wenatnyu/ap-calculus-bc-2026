Directions: Separate variables, integrate, simplify, and check equilibrium solutions.
要求：分离变量、两边积分、化简，并检查平衡解。

Fast check: Record any y-factor that is divided out and keep absolute values in logarithms.

1. Is y′=x+y directly separable? Explain briefly.3 points
Decision and reason / 判断与理由：
Answer: No. The sum x+y cannot be written directly as an x-only factor times a y-only factor.
2. Find the general solution of y′=2xy.3 points
Separation and solution / 分离与通解：
Answer: dy/y=2x dx, so ln|y|=x²+C and y=Cex², including y=0 when C=0.
3. Find an implicit general solution of y′=3x²/y.4 points
Separation and integration / 分离与积分：
Answer: y dy=3x²dx, so y²/2=x³+C, equivalently y²=2x³+C.
