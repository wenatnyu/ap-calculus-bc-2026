AP Calculus BC · Topic 5.3

Determining Intervals Where a Function Is Increasing or Decreasing

判断函数递增与递减区间

f′(x)>0 ⟹ f increases

f′(x)<0 ⟹ f decreases

The sign of the derivative describes the direction of the original function.导数的正负决定原函数的增减方向。

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 501

把导数读成 slope：positive slope means the graph rises as x increases。

Graph interpretation

Read the Graph of f′

看导数图像在 x 轴上方还是下方

Above the x-axis

f′>0, so f increases.导数图在轴上方。

Below the x-axis

f′<0, so f decreases.导数图在轴下方。

Do not ask whether the graph of f′ itself is rising. For monotonicity of f, ask whether f′ is positive or negative.判断 f 增减只看 f′ 的高度符号。

Height of f′ → direction of f06

用手势区分“f′ 在上方”和“f′ 自己上升”。后者描述 f″，不是本节问题。

Domain before sign

A Domain Break Splits Intervals

函数未定义处不能跨过去写一个区间

f(x)=1/x

f′(x)=−1/x²<0 for x≠0

The function is decreasing on (−∞,0) and on (0,∞).

Do not write “decreasing on (−∞,∞).” The function is not defined at 0.定义域断点必须把区间分开。

Two intervals, not one.07

虽然两侧导数都为负，不能把穿过 0 的输入直接比较为一个递减区间。

Is the Population Increasing at t=3?

变化率为负，原数量在减少

R(t)=2t cos(t²)

R(3)=6cos(9)≈−5.467

Because R(3)<0, the population is decreasing at t=3.R 已经是变化率，不需要再求 R′。

Official test prep · Question 14

Where Is g Decreasing?

把已知函数与导数代入 g′

Suppose f(x)=4−x and g′(x)=f(x)f′(x)(x−2).

g′(x)=(4−x)(−1)(x−2)=(x−4)(x−2)

  • A.(−∞,2] and [2,∞)
  • B.(−∞,2] only
  • C.[2,4] only
  • D.[2,∞) only
  • E.[4,∞) only
C. The product (x−4)(x−2) is negative for 2<x<4. In the answer-choice convention, g is decreasing on [2,4].导数在内部为负，端点为零。
Official answer: C14

官方 test-prep 第 14 题。严格导数负区间是 (2,4)；选项以函数递减区间的闭端点形式给出 [2,4]。

State Every Monotonicity Interval

f(x)=x⁴−4x²

Find all intervals where f is increasing and decreasing.
