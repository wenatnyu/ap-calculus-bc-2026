5-Minute Review: Limits at Infinity

五分钟复习：无穷远处的极限与水平渐近线

5MINUTES · 10 POINTS

Directions: Evaluate each limit. Use leading-term reasoning and state a horizontal asymptote when requested.
要求：比较最高次项并写清结论；题目要求时写出水平渐近线。

Fast check: students should divide by the highest power or compare degrees. A horizontal asymptote describes end behavior, not a barrier the graph cannot cross.

1. Evaluate limx→∞5x²−32x²+7.3 points
Reasoning / 过程：
Answer: 5/2. The numerator and denominator have the same degree, so the limit is the ratio of leading coefficients, 5/2. 同次有理式看最高次项系数之比。
2. Evaluate limx→−∞3x³+x−x³+4.3 points
Leading terms / 最高次项：
Answer: −3. The leading-term quotient is 3x³/(−x³)=−3. The direction x→−∞ does not change this coefficient ratio. 正负无穷都由同一最高次项系数比决定。
3. Let f(x)=2x²+1x²−9. State its horizontal asymptote and justify it with both end-behavior limits.4 points
Limits and asymptote / 极限与渐近线：
Answer: y=2. limx→∞f(x)=2 and limx→−∞f(x)=2; therefore y=2 is a horizontal asymptote. 函数两端都趋近 2，所以水平渐近线是 y=2。

Scoring / 评分

  • Q1: 2 points for degree/leading-term reasoning; 1 for 5/2.
  • Q2: 2 points for the leading-term quotient; 1 for −3.
  • Q3: 1 point for each limit and 2 for the asymptote conclusion.
