Connecting Differentiability and Continuity

连接可导性与连续性

Differentiability is the stronger condition: every differentiable function is continuous at that point, but not every continuous function is differentiable.可导一定连续；连续不一定可导。

differentiable at c
⟹ continuous at c
converse: false

Zoom-in test

What “Differentiable at c” Means

导数极限存在，且放大后局部像一条直线

f′(c)=limh→0f(c+h)−f(c)h

This two-sided limit must exist as a finite real number.

Graphically: near c, the graph has one well-defined, nonvertical tangent direction.左右看过去是同一个有限斜率。

Being defined at c is not enough.

Differentiability is a local property.03

官方 notes 用“放大后像直线”描述可导。补充 AP 语境中的导数值为有限实数，因此竖直切线不算可导。

Logic matters

The One-Way Theorem

可导推出连续，但不能把箭头反过来

f differentiable at c ⟹ f continuous at c

Valid: If f′(c) exists, then limx→cf(x)=f(c).可导可直接推出连续。

Invalid converse: Continuity alone does not guarantee a tangent slope.连续图像仍可能有折角、尖点或竖直切线。

Contrapositive: if f is not continuous at c, then f is not differentiable there.

Differentiability is sufficient for continuity.04

同时练正命题与逆否命题。不要说“不连续函数都没有任何导数”；只讨论该不连续点。

Official notes classification

Where a Derivative Fails

导数不存在的四类常见图像

Discontinuity

A hole, jump, or asymptote breaks continuity first.

不连续必不可导。

Corner

Finite left and right slopes are unequal.

左右斜率不同。

Cusp

Slopes become unbounded with opposite signs.

两侧趋向相反无穷。

Vertical tangent

Slope magnitude becomes unbounded.

切线竖直，斜率非有限数。

First classify continuity. Then, at continuous points, check whether one finite tangent slope exists.先查连续，再查左右斜率。

A graph may fail in more than one way at different inputs.05

与官方 notes 的三类一致：discontinuity、corner/cusp、vertical tangent。这里把 corner 与 cusp 拆开便于术语掌握。

The standard counterexample

Continuous but Not Differentiable

绝对值函数在顶点连续但不可导

h(x)=|x−3|

limx→3h(x)=0=h(3): continuous.

h′−(3)=−1, but h′+(3)=1: not differentiable.

One point, two tangent directions.一个点出现两个不同斜率。

This single example disproves the converse.06

图像水平平移不改变 V 形。要求学生同时写出连续性证据和不可导证据。

Join two formulas

Piecewise Functions: Continuity First

分段函数先让连接点函数值相等

f(x)={ x+1, x<1
ax+b, x≥1 }

left value at 1: 2

right value at 1: a+b

continuity condition: a+b=2

Continuity matches heights at the join.第一步只匹配纵坐标。

This condition alone does not match the slopes.

Height first; slope second.09

把拼接点 x=1 代入左右两段。右段含等号，所以 f(1)=a+b；左极限为 2。

Differentiability needs a second condition

Then Match the Slopes

可导还要求连接点左右导数相等

left derivative: 1

right derivative: a

a=1

a+b=2 ⟹ b=1

Result: a=1, b=1.高度相同 + 斜率相同。

Matching slopes without matching values is not enough: two parallel pieces can still have a jump.

Differentiable join = continuous join + equal one-sided derivatives10

先使用上一页的连续条件，再比较导数。要求学生按这两个方程的来源解释。

Continuous · Infinite slope magnitude

A Vertical Tangent Is Not a Finite Derivative

竖直切线处函数可连续，但普通导数不存在

f(x)=∛x=x1/3

f′(0)=limh→0∛hh=limh→01∛(h²)

The difference quotient grows without bound instead of approaching a finite number.

f is continuous at 0.

f′(0) is not a finite real number, so f is not differentiable at 0 in the usual AP sense.连续不等于有有限斜率。

A vertical line has undefined slope.11

学生可能说“斜率是无穷”。更准确：差商不收敛到有限实数，因此普通导数不存在。

Fast exam procedure

A Two-Pass Graph Scan

图像题固定流程：先连续，再可导

Pass 1

Mark discontinuities

Holes, jumps, asymptotes, and mismatched function values.

Each marked input is automatically not differentiable.

先圈出所有间断点。
Pass 2

Scan remaining points

Look for corners, cusps, and vertical tangents.

These are continuous but not differentiable.

再找连续图像中的非光滑点。

Do not call every sharp-looking bend a discontinuity. A corner can remain fully connected.“尖”不等于“断”。

This procedure matches the official practice layout.13

让学生在官方 packet practice 的图上用两种颜色：红色标不连续，蓝色标连续但不可导。

Official test prep 6

Absolute Value Check

h(x)=|x−4| 在 x=4 的性质

I. h is continuous at 4.
II. h is differentiable at 4.
III. h has an absolute minimum at 4.

  • A.I only
  • B.II only
  • C.III only
  • D.I and III only
D: I and III only. The V-shaped graph is continuous and reaches its absolute minimum 0 at x=4, but the left slope −1 and right slope 1 do not match.连续且有最小值，但折角处不可导。
A local or absolute extremum need not be differentiable.14

官方答案 D。借此预防“极值点导数一定等于 0”的错误；该结论需要可导这一前提。

Exit ticket · Classify and justify

Where Is It Not Differentiable?

判断并说明原因

f(x)=|x²−1|

Classify continuity and differentiability at x=−1 and x=1.

Continuous at both; not differentiable at either. At each zero of x²−1, the absolute value creates a corner. At both x=−1 and x=1, the left-hand slope is −2 and the right-hand slope is 2.绝对值在符号改变处形成折角。
Continuity first · then compare the one-sided slopes
Practice aligned to the official 2.4 packet15

两点从左到右的斜率均为 −2、2；函数连续，但左右导数不相等。
