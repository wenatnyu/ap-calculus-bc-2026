Fast check: Look for substitution, integration by parts, and linear partial fractions.

1. Evaluate ∫ 2x/(x²+5) dx.3 points
Method and antiderivative / 方法与原函数：
Answer: Let u=x²+5. The result is ln(x²+5)+C.
2. Evaluate ∫ xeˣ dx.3 points
Set up integration by parts / 分部积分：
Answer: Using u=x and dv=eˣdx gives xeˣ−eˣ+C=eˣ(x−1)+C.
3. Evaluate ∫ 5/(x²−4) dx.4 points
Decomposition and antiderivative / 分解与原函数：
Answer: 5/(x²−4)=5/[4(x−2)]−5/[4(x+2)], so the integral is (5/4)ln|(x−2)/(x+2)|+C.
