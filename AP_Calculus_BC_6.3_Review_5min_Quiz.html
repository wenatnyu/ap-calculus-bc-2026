1. For f(x)=x² on [0,4] with n=4, find Δx and L₄.3 points
Setup / 列式：
Answer: Δx=1 and L₄=(0²+1²+2²+3²)(1)=14.
2. For the same function and partition, find R₄ and state whether it over- or underestimates.3 points
Work / 过程：
Answer: R₄=(1²+2²+3²+4²)=30; it overestimates because f is increasing.
3. Use a trapezoidal sum for x=0,2,5 with f(x)=4,7,3.4 points

Answer: T=2(4+7)/2+3(7+3)/2=26.
