1. Write the volume formula when cross-sectional area perpendicular to the x-axis is A(x) for a≤x≤b.3 points
Volume element / 体积微元
Answer: V=∫abA(x)dx.
2. The base lies between y=x and y=x² on [0,1]. Square cross sections are perpendicular to the x-axis. Find V.3 points
Square area / 正方形面积
Answer: V=∫₀¹(x−x²)²dx=1/30.
3. The base width is w(x)=1−x² on [0,1]. Rectangular cross sections have height twice the base. Find V.4 points
Rectangle ratio / 矩形比例
Answer: V=∫₀¹2(1−x²)²dx=16/15 cubic units.
