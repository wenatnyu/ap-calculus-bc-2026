1. State the arc-length formula for a smooth graph y=f(x) on [a,b].3 points
Formula and conditions
Answer: L=∫ab√(1+[f′(x)]²) dx, assuming the graph is smooth (or piecewise smooth) on the interval.
2. A particle has velocity v(t)=t−2 for 0≤t≤4. Find its displacement and total distance.3 points
Show both integrals
Answer: Displacement: ∫₀⁴(t−2)dt=0. Total distance: ∫₀⁴|t−2|dt=4.
3. Find the exact length of y=(2/3)x3/2 on 0≤x≤3.4 points
Derivative, setup, evaluation
Answer: y′=√x, so L=∫₀³√(1+x)dx=(2/3)[(1+x)3/2]₀³=14/3.
