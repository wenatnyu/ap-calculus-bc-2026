要求：5 分钟，不用计算器。保留精确值并写出积分。

Fast check: Checks area with 1/x, one-step integration by parts, and a convergent improper p-integral.

1. Find the area under y=1/x from x=1 to x=e².3 points
Area integral / 写定积分；ln(e²)=2。
Answer:

∫₁e²(1/x)dx=[ln x]₁e²=2.

2. Evaluate ∫₀¹xe2xdx.3 points
Integration by parts / u=x，dv=e^(2x)dx。
Answer:

u=x, dv=e2xdx, v=(1/2)e2x. Thus [xe2x/2−e2x/4]₀¹=(e²+1)/4.

3. The region under y=1/x for x≥2 is revolved about the x-axis. Set up and evaluate the volume.4 points
Improper disk volume / 圆盘半径平方；∞ 要改写成极限。
Answer:

V=limb→∞π∫₂ᵇx−2dx=lim π[−1/x]₂ᵇ=π/2.
