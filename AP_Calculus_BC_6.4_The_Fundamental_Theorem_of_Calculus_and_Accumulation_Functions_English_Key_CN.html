Why the Variable Is t

为什么积分变量写 t

G(x)=∫ₐˣ f(t)dt

x controls the endpoint; t runs inside the integral

The names t and u are placeholders inside a definite integral.

AP-style quick check

Quick Check

课堂即时检测

If F(x)=∫₁ˣ²√(1+t³)dt, what is F′(x)?

  1. A√(1+x³)
  2. B2x√(1+x⁶)
  3. Cx²√(1+x⁶)
  4. D2x√(1+x³)
Answer: B. Evaluate the integrand at x² and multiply by 2x.上限是 x²，必须再乘 2x。
