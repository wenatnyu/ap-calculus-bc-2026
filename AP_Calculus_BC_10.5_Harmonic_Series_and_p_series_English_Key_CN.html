Harmonic Series and p-series

调和级数与 p 级数

Σ1/np converges if p>1 and diverges if p≤1.

The harmonic series is the boundary case p=1. / 调和级数是临界情形 p=1。

The Harmonic Series

调和级数

Σn=1∞1/n diverges.

Its terms approach zero, but its partial sums grow without bound.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1003

The p-Series Rule

p 级数判别规则

Σn=1∞1/np converges exactly when p>1.

It diverges when p≤1.

The Boundary p=1

临界值 p=1

p=1 is the harmonic series, so it diverges.

Do not include equality in the convergent case.

A Convergent p-Series

收敛的 p 级数

Σ1/n3/2 converges because p=3/2>1.

No exact sum is supplied by the p-series test.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1006

The test classifies convergence; it does not usually evaluate the sum.

A Divergent p-Series

发散的 p 级数

Σ1/√n=Σ1/n1/2 diverges because p=1/2≤1.

Move Powers Correctly

正确改写指数

Σ1/∛(n2)=Σ1/n2/3.

Since 2/3<1, the series diverges.

Core lesson

Constants Do Not Change Behavior

常数倍不改变收敛性

For nonzero constant c, Σc/np has the same convergence behavior as Σ1/np.

The constant can change the sum, not the classification.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1009

If c is negative, classify absolute magnitudes first; convergence behavior still matches.

Index Shifts

指标平移

Σn=1∞1/(n+4)p

A finite shift does not change the p-series convergence threshold.

Finite Changes

有限项改变

Starting at n=5 instead of n=1 removes four terms.

The numerical sum changes, but convergence or divergence does not.
