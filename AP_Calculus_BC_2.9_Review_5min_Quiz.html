Directions: Use (fg)′=f′g+fg′. Keep both terms before simplifying.
要求：使用乘积法则，先完整写出两项再化简。

Fast check: The derivative of a product is not the product of the derivatives.

1. Differentiate h(x)=x2sin x.3 points
Two product-rule terms / 乘积法则两项：
Answer: h′(x)=2x sin x+x2cos x.
2. At x=2, suppose f=3, f′=−1, g=4, g′=5. If p(x)=2f(x)g(x), find p′(2).3 points
Substitute all four table values / 代入四个表格值：
Answer: p′(2)=2[f′g+fg′]=2[(−1)(4)+(3)(5)]=22.
3. Find the tangent line to y=xex at x=0.4 points
Point and slope / 点与斜率：
Answer: y(0)=0 and y′=ex+xex, so y′(0)=1. The line is y=x.
