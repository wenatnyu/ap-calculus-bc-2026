Fast check: An instant’s numerical value is not a constant over the whole motion.

1. A sphere’s radius is 2 cm and grows at 3 cm/s. Find dV/dt.3 points
V, derivative, value:
Answer: V=(4/3)πr³, so dV/dt=4πr²(dr/dt)=4π(4)(3)=48π cm³/s.
2. A 10-foot ladder has x=6 ft, y=8 ft, and dx/dt=2 ft/s. How fast is the top moving vertically?3 points
Signed vertical rate:
Answer: dy/dt=−3/2 ft/s; the top moves downward at 3/2 ft/s.
3. A rope passes through a pulley 5 ft above a boat. When rope length r=13 ft, it is hauled in at 2 ft/s. How fast is the boat approaching the dock?4 points
Triangle, signed rate, approaching speed:
Answer: Since x=12 and r′=−2, xx′=rr′ gives x′=−13/6 ft/s. The approaching speed is 13/6 ft/s.
