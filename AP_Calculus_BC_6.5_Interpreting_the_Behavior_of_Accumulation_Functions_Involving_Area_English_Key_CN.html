Increasing and Decreasing

递增与递减

f(x)>0

g′(x)>0, so g is increasing.

f(x)<0

g′(x)<0, so g is decreasing.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 604

The vertical position of f, not whether f itself rises, controls monotonicity of g.

Topic 6.5 · Core lesson

Concavity of g

累积函数的凹凸性

f increasing

g′=f is increasing, so g is concave up. Where f′ exists, f′≥0.

f decreasing

g′=f is decreasing, so g is concave down. Where f′ exists, f′≤0.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 606

Here the trend of f, not its sign, controls concavity of g. Do not replace increasing with the stronger pointwise claim f prime is positive.

Inflection Points of g

累积函数的拐点

g″=f′

g changes concavity when f changes from increasing to decreasing or vice versa

A local extremum of f is a candidate for an inflection point of g; verify the change.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 607

Do not require f to cross the x-axis for g to have an inflection point.

Quick Check

课堂即时检测

Let g(x)=2+∫₀ˣf(t)dt. If f changes from positive to negative at x=3, what happens to g?

  1. Alocal minimum
  2. Blocal maximum
  3. Cinflection only
  4. Dno conclusion
Answer: B. Since g′=f changes + to −, g has a local maximum at x=3.f 就是 g′，由正变负对应局部最大值。

Exit ticket · One clean solution

Exit Ticket

离堂检测

If f is negative and increasing on (1,4), describe g(x)=∫₀ˣf(t)dt on (1,4).

Answer: g is decreasing because g′=f<0, and concave up because g′=f is increasing (where f′ exists, g″=f′≥0).函数符号判断单调性；函数趋势判断凹凸性。
