Official solution walkthrough

Part (a): Compute f(0) and f(5)

（a）计算 f(0)、f(5)

The lower semicircle has signed area −(1/2)π(2²)=−2π.

f(0)=f(4)−∫₀⁴f′(x)dx=3+2π

From 4 to 5, the triangular area is 1/2.

f(5)=3+1/2=7/2

向左求值时要正确处理积分方向。

Official solution walkthrough

Concavity Lives in the Trend of f′

f′ 的增减决定 f 的凹凸

  • f′ decreasing ⇒ f concave down
  • f′ increasing ⇒ f concave up
  • A change between these trends can create an inflection point.

拐点看 f′ 的增减变化，不是只看 f′ 是否过零。

Part (b): Inflection Points

（b）找拐点

f′ decreases on (0,2), increases on (2,6), then decreases on (6,7).

Therefore f changes concavity at x=2 and x=6.

答案要给 x 坐标并引用 f′ 的单调变化。

Part (c): Differentiate the New Function

（c）先求新函数的导数

g(x)=f(x)−x ⇒ g′(x)=f′(x)−1

Here g′<0 on the interior of the decreasing interval. Comparing f′≤1 also captures its endpoints in the AP answer.

区间内部看 g′<0；用 f′≤1 读出 AP 答案的端点。

Official solution walkthrough

Read the Horizontal Level y=1

在 f′ 图像上读 y=1

For 0≤x≤4, f′≤0≤1.

On the segment f′=x−4, the level 1 occurs at x=5.

f′>1 for 5<x<7, and f′(7)=1.

一个孤立的等号点不会增加递减区间。

Part (c): Decreasing Interval

（c）递减区间结论

g′(x)=f′(x)−1≤0 on 0≤x≤5

Answer: g is decreasing on [0,5] (endpoint notation variations are acceptable).

Part (d): Locate the Absolute Minimum

（d）定位绝对最小值

g′<0 on (0,5) and g′>0 on (5,7).

So g decreases, then increases; its absolute minimum occurs at x=5.

负变正说明 x=5 为最小点；区间内只有这一处关键转折。

Nine-Point Map and Common Errors

9 分结构与常见错误

  • (a) area/integral evidence + f(0) + f(5): 3
  • (b) both x-values + justification from trends of f′: 2
  • (c) derivative/inequality analysis + interval with reason: 2
  • (d) considers critical point + value with global justification: 2

高频错误：把图当成 f、把几何面积当积分、把 f′ 过零当拐点、只给最小点不求最小值。

Quick check · Reveal

Quick Check

课堂快检

Using the same graph of f′:

  1. Find f(6).
  2. Is x=4 an inflection point of f? Explain.

用面积求 f(6)，再判断 x=4 是否为拐点。

Answer:
  1. f(6)=f(4)+ area of the triangle from 4 to 6 =3+2=5.
  2. No. Although f′(4)=0, f′ is increasing on both sides of 4, so f remains concave up.

f′ 过零对应 f 的极值候选，不自动对应拐点。

Exit ticket

Exit Ticket

离堂题

For h(x)=f(x)−2x, determine where h is decreasing on [0,7]. Give the derivative test.

对 h=f−2x 求导，并与 f′ 图像的高度 2 比较。

Answer:

h′(x)=f′(x)−2≤0 wherever f′(x)≤2. The graph never exceeds 2, so h is decreasing on the entire interval [0,7] (with h′=0 only at x=6).

导数可在孤立点等于 0，函数仍可在整个区间递减。
