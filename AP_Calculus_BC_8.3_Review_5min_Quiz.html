1. A particle has v(t)=t²−4 on [0,3]. Find its displacement.3 points
Signed integral / 有向积分
Answer: ∫₀³(t²−4)dt=−3.
2. For v(t)=t−1 on [0,3], find total distance traveled.3 points
Split at sign change / 按变号点分段
Answer: ∫₀¹(1−t)dt+∫₁³(t−1)dt=5/2.
3. If a(t)=6t and v(0)=2, find v(2) and include units if t is in seconds and position in meters.4 points
Initial value / 初值
Answer: v(2)=2+∫₀²6t dt=14 m/s.
