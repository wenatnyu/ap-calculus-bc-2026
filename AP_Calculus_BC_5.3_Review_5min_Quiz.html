Directions: Check the original domain before naming critical numbers.
要求：判断临界数前先检查原函数定义域。

Fast check: Critical numbers are domain inputs where the derivative is zero or undefined.

1. State the Extreme Value Theorem, including its hypothesis and conclusion.3 points
Hypothesis and conclusion / 条件与结论：
Answer: If f is continuous on a closed interval [a,b], then it attains both an absolute maximum and an absolute minimum on that interval.
2. Find all critical numbers and corresponding points of f(x)=x³−3x.3 points
Derivative and coordinates / 导数与坐标：
Answer: f′(x)=3(x−1)(x+1), so the critical numbers are −1,1. The points are (−1,2) and (1,−2).
3. The derivative of g(x)=1/(x−2) is undefined at x=2. Is 2 a critical number of g? Explain.4 points
Domain check / 定义域检查：
Answer: No. The original function is also undefined at x=2. A critical number must belong to the domain of the original function.
