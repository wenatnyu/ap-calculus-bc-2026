The first move is diagnostic

Substitute Before You Manipulate

先代入判断，再决定是否需要变形

1. Substitute the approaching value.
2. Read the form you get.
3. Use only the procedure that fixes that form.

Why this saves time

A complicated-looking expression may already have a finite value. In that case, extra algebra adds risk without adding information.

Appearance does not choose the procedure. The substitution result does.不要看外形猜方法；看代入后的结果。

Every example today begins with the same first line: “Substitute.”03

让学生把 “substitute first” 写在页边。选方法的证据必须来自代入后的形式，而不是“看到根号就有理化”。

Four diagnoses

The Substitution Result Chooses the Next Move

代入结果决定下一步

finite real valueEvaluate and stop.direct substitution
0 / 0The form is indeterminate; rewrite the expression.factor, rationalize, or combine
nonzero / 0Check signs and one-sided behavior.infinite limit or DNE
outside domainInspect the nearby real domain before deciding.one-sided or DNE

0/0 is not an answer. It tells you the current expression hides more information.0/0 不是答案，而是“需要继续变形”的信号。

“Indeterminate” means the form alone does not determine the limit.04

快速口答：3/4 → stop；0/0 → rewrite；5/0 → check signs；√(negative nearby) → check domain。不要把所有分母为 0 的情况都称为 0/0。

A repeatable routine

Use This Decision Sequence

按同一顺序判断，减少试错

  1. 01Substitute. If the result is a finite real number, you are done.直接代入得到有限实数，就结束。
  2. 02If you get 0/0, look for a shared algebraic cause: a common factor, a radical difference, or nested denominators.0/0 后判断：公因式、根式差，还是复合分式。
  3. 03Rewrite on a punctured neighborhood, then substitute again.在去心邻域内改写成等价式，再次代入。
  4. 04If the denominator approaches 0 but the numerator does not, compare the two sides.若是非零/0，要检查左右符号。
The sequence matters more than memorizing a list of tricks.05

强调 punctured neighborhood：约分后的式子可以在 x≠a 时与原式相等，而极限只关心 a 附近。这里不要求正式定义，只要求学生理解“点上可不同，附近相同”。

Procedure 1 · Direct substitution

A Radical Does Not Automatically Mean Rationalize

有根号不代表一定要有理化

limx→1√(x+4)
= √(1+4)
= √5

Diagnosis

Substitution gives the finite real number √5. No denominator becomes zero and no ambiguity remains.

Procedure: direct substitution.代入后已经得到有限实数，不需要额外变形。

Packet practice 2 · The expression looks harder than the procedure is.06

先遮住答案，让学生只说代入结果。追问：为什么不需要乘共轭式？因为没有 0/0。

Procedure 2 · Factor and cancel

A Shared Factor Calls for Cancellation

出现公因式时，因式分解并约分

limx→5x²−5xx−5
= limx→5x(x−5)x−5
= limx→5x = 5

Why cancellation is legal

For every nearby input with x≠5, the two expressions agree. A limit can ignore the missing point itself.

Cancel factors, never separate terms.只能约因子，不能跨加减号约项。

Packet practice 8 · Factor first; cancel second.07

学生若把 x² 与 x 相约，要求先写完整因式分解。提醒原函数在 x=5 可能无定义，但极限仍可为 5。

Procedure 3 · Rationalize

A Difference of Radicals Calls for the Conjugate

根式作差得到 0/0 时，乘共轭式

limx→0√(x+7)−√7x
= limx→01√(x+7)+√7
= 12√7

The useful identity

(A−B)(A+B)=A²−B²

The conjugate turns the radical difference into (x+7)−7=x, which cancels the denominator.

Signal: a radical difference plus 0/0.“根式差 + 0/0” 才是有理化信号。

Packet practice 1 · Multiply by a form of 1.08

板书中把共轭式写成分子分母相同，强调乘的是 1。约分后再代入，答案 1/(2√7)。

Procedure 4 · Simplify a complex fraction

Nested Denominators Call for One Fraction

复合分式先通分，再约去造成 0/0 的因子

limx→01x+3−13x
= limx→0−x3(x+3) · 1x
= −19

Two clean moves

1. Combine the numerator using the common denominator 3(x+3).

2. Rewrite division by x as multiplication by 1/x.

Do not substitute again until the entire complex fraction is simplified.整式化简完成后再代入。

Packet practice 3 · Keep the fraction bars at different visual levels.09

学生常漏掉“除以 x”等于“乘 1/x”。要求把大分数线画长，先明确大分子和大分母。

Do not over-process

A Rational Expression May Still Need Only Substitution

分式不等于“必须因式分解”

limx→−3x−2x²−3x+2
= −59+9+2
= −14

Diagnosis

The denominator approaches 20, not 0. The quotient law applies immediately.

Procedure: direct substitution.分母极限非零，可以直接代入。

A longer method can be correct, but it is not the best procedure.

Packet practice 4 · Choose the shortest justified route.10

让学生先预测“是否需要因式分解”，再计算分母值 20。目标是把“外形”与“诊断”分开。

Common errors

Four Errors to Catch Early

四个常见错误

  1. Calling 0/0 the limit. It is an indeterminate form, not a final value.0/0 是继续计算的信号，不是极限值。
  2. Cancelling terms. Cancellation works only with common factors.只能约公因子，不能约加减中的“项”。
  3. Rationalizing every radical. First check whether substitution already works.先代入；不是所有根式都要有理化。
  4. Stopping after the rewrite. Substitute again and state the limit clearly.变形后还要再次代入并写出结论。
A correct procedure includes a diagnosis, a rewrite, and a final evaluation.11

可以让四名学生各读一条英文错误描述，再让全班给出中文解释。把第 2 条配一个错误示例，如 (x²−5x)/(x−5) 不能直接把分子中的 x 与分母约掉。

Quick sort · Say the method first

Choose the Procedure Before Computing

先说方法，再计算

  • B.limx→5x²−5xx−5
  • C.limx→7√(x+9)−4x−7
  • D.limx→01x+3−13x

Guided practice · Diagnose before solving

Guided Practice: Two Different 0/0 Forms

同样是 0/0，结构不同，方法不同

A · Radical difference

limx→0√(x+11)−√11x

Name the procedure and evaluate.

B · Complex fraction

limx→01(x+2)²−14x

Name the procedure and evaluate.

A. Rationalize: 1√(x+11)+√11→12√11.
B. Combine fractions: 4−(x+2)²4(x+2)²x=−x+44(x+2)²→−1/4.A 用共轭式；B 先通分，再约去 x。

AP-style check · Parameter b

AP Check: Rationalize with a Parameter

含参数时，同样先找共轭式

limx→bb−x√x−√b
  • A.−2√b
  • B.−√b
  • C.2b
  • D.√b
  • E.2√b
A. Since b−x=−(x−b)=−(√x−√b)(√x+√b), the quotient equals −(√x+√b) for nearby x≠b. Its limit is −2√b.先把 b−x 写成 −(x−b)，负号不能丢。
Packet test prep 14 · Track the negative sign before cancelling.14

正确选项 A。最常见错误是把 b−x 当成 x−b，从而选 E。让学生先圈出负号，再写平方差。

Exit ticket · One complete explanation

Exit Ticket: Name the Procedure and Finish

说出方法，并完成计算

limx→113−13xx−1

Write one sentence: “I chose ______ because direct substitution gave ______.”

写一句理由：我选择 ______，因为直接代入得到 ______。
Procedure: combine the complex fraction. Substitution gives 0/0.
1/3−1/(3x)x−1=(x−1)/(3x)x−1=13x→1/3
先通分得到公因子 x−1，再约分并代入。
