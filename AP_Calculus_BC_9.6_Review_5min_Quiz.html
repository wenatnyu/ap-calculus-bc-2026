Fast check: Check componentwise integration and the two distinct uses of initial data.

1. A particle has v(t)=⟨2t,−1⟩ and r(0)=⟨3,4⟩. Find r(t).3 points
Integrate velocity / 积分速度
Answer: r(t)=⟨t²+3,4−t⟩.
2. A particle has a(t)=⟨6t,2⟩ and v(0)=⟨1,−1⟩. Find v(t).3 points
Recover velocity / 恢复速度
Answer: v(t)=⟨3t²+1,2t−1⟩.
3. A particle has a(t)=⟨2,0⟩, v(0)=⟨0,3⟩, and r(0)=⟨1,−2⟩. Find r(2).4 points
Use both initial conditions / 使用两个初始条件
Answer: v(t)=⟨2t,3⟩ and r(t)=⟨t²+1,3t−2⟩, so r(2)=⟨5,4⟩.

Scoring / 评分

  • Q1: integrate both components and use r(0).
  • Q2: integrate both components and use v(0).
  • Q3: integrate twice, applying the correct initial condition at each stage.
  • Accept equivalent vector notation.
