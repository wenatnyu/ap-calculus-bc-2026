AP Calculus BC · Topic 2.8

The Product Rule

乘积法则

If h(x)=f(x)g(x),

h′(x)=f′(x)g(x)
       +f(x)g′(x)

When two changing factors are multiplied, both changes contribute.两个因子都在变化，因此导数必须包含两项。

Differentiate one · Keep the other · Add

Core formula

The Product Rule

每次只求导一个因子

h=fg

h′=f′g+fg′

  1. Differentiate f; keep g.
  2. Add.
  3. Keep f; differentiate g.

The order of the two added terms may switch; the mathematics is unchanged.两项顺序可交换。

Not: (fg)′=f′g′.乘积的导数不等于导数的乘积。

Two changing factors create two contributions.03

把公式分成左项和右项，用两种颜色标记“求导的因子”。最后明确 f′g+fg′ 与 fg′+f′g 等价。

Quick proof by counterexample

Why f′g′ Fails

用简单例子排除错误公式

Multiply first

(x2·x3)′

=(x5)′=5x4

Wrong shortcut

(x2)′(x3)′

=(2x)(3x2)=6x3

5x4≠6x3. The shortcut fails even for basic powers.一个反例就能说明错误公式不成立。

The correct rule must agree with simplifying first.04

用这个反例让学生自己发现 f′g′ 不可能正确。正确乘积法则给 2x·x³+x²·3x²=5x⁴。

Official notes · Example 1

Polynomial Times Sine

一次写全乘积法则的两项

f(x)=8x sin x

f′(x)=8 sin x+8x cos x

First contribution: (8x)′sin x=8sin x前因子求导，后因子不动。

Second contribution: 8x(sin x)′=8xcos x前因子不动，后因子求导。

Write both terms before simplifying.05

官方 notes 第 1 题。学生若写成 8cos x，追问“x 的变化贡献在哪里”。

A Product with Reciprocals

分式因子先改写为负指数

h(x)=(1/x+1)(2x2−5)

h′(x)=−1x2(2x2−5)+(1/x+1)(4x)

Derivative of the first factor: (x−1+1)′=−x−2.

Derivative of the second factor: (2x2−5)′=4x.

Equivalent simplified forms are acceptable on the original domain.07

Extend the structure

Constants and More Factors

常数倍保留；三个因子产生三项

Constant multiple

[c·f·g]′=c(f′g+fg′)

The outside constant multiplies the whole result.常数乘整个导数。

Three factors

(fgh)′=f′gh+fg′h+fgh′

Differentiate one factor in each term.每项只对一个因子求导。

For three factors, there are three contributions; for two factors, there are two.项数对应可变化的因子数。

Keep every unchanged factor in each contribution.10

这页用于结构迁移，不要求展开复杂三因子。可让学生用颜色标出每一项中唯一带撇的因子。

Tangent Line from a Product

原函数求点，导函数求斜率

g(x)=−2xex,   x=0

g(0)=0

g′(x)=−2ex−2xex

g′(0)=−2

Equation

y=−2x

Point (0,0), slope −2.不要把导数值当成 y 坐标。

Tangent: y−y₀=m(x−x₀)11

官方练习 15。点来自 g(0)，斜率来自 g′(0)。答案 y=−2x。

AP-Style Tangent Line

函数信息题：不需要知道 f 的公式

f(2)=7,   f′(2)=−2

g(x)=x2f(x)

g(2)=4·7=28

g′(2)=2(2)(7)+4(−2)=20

Tangent line

y−28=20(x−2)

Use f(2) for the point and both f(2),f′(2) for the slope.答案为官方选项 B。

Values are enough; no formula for f is needed.12

Exit ticket · Derivative and tangent

One Product, Two Results

求导并写切线

h(x)=(x2+1)ex

Find h′(x), then write the tangent line at x=0.

Answer: h′=2xex+(x2+1)ex=ex(x+1)2. Since h(0)=1 and h′(0)=1, the tangent is y=x+1.点为 (0,1)，斜率为 1。
