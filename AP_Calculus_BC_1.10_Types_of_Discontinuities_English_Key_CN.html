Continuous or Broken?

Continuous near x=c

Official notes · Three categories

Three Types of Discontinuities

三类间断点

1 · Hole

A finite two-sided limit exists, but the point is missing or has the wrong value.

Removable

极限存在且有限；补一个点即可修复。

2 · Vertical asymptote

Outputs become unbounded on at least one side of x=c.

Nonremovable

至少一侧函数值无界。

3 · Jump

Both one-sided limits are finite, but they approach different values.

Nonremovable

左右有限极限不相等。
Hole · Vertical asymptote · Jump04

让学生给三类各说一个关键词：finite-but-wrong, unbounded, unequal sides。

Official notes 1 · Rational function

Factor Before Classifying

有理函数：先因式分解，再看哪些因子约掉

f(x)=x²−8x+12x²+3x−10

=(x−6)(x−2)(x+5)(x−2)

x=2: canceled factor

Hole at x=2.约掉的因子产生空洞。

x=−5: factor remains below

Vertical asymptote at x=−5.分母保留的零因子产生竖直渐近线。

Official notes 106

强调约分只对 x≠2 的附近式成立，所以 x=2 仍不在原定义域；这正是空洞。

Official notes 2 · A fourth power

Factor a Difference of Powers

高次式也要分解到能判断每个零点

g(x)=x+1x⁴−1

x⁴−1=(x−1)(x+1)(x²+1)

g(x)=1(x−1)(x²+1), x≠−1

Hole at x=−1. The factor x+1 cancels.

Vertical asymptote at x=1. The factor x−1 remains.

x²+1 has no real zeros.x²+1 不产生实数范围内的间断。

Official notes 207

先写 x⁴−1=(x²−1)(x²+1)，再继续分解 x²−1。要求学生检查实数零点。

Official notes 3 · Trigonometric denominators

Trig Vertical Asymptotes

三角函数：令隐藏的分母为零

h(x)=tan(2x)=sin(2x)cos(2x)

cos(2x)=0

2x=π2+kπ

On 0≤x≤2π

x=π4, 3π4, 5π4, 7π4

All four are vertical asymptotes.在指定区间内共有四条竖直渐近线。

Official notes 4 · Recognize a continuous family

Some Functions Have No Breaks

不要强行寻找不存在的间断点

f(x)=x²−1

A polynomial is continuous for every real number.

No discontinuities.多项式在所有实数上连续。

There is no denominator, even root, logarithm, or piece boundary to restrict the domain.

“Continuous function” is a valid final classification.

Official notes 409

让学生对比有理函数与多项式。题目说 identify each discontinuity 时，答案可以是 none。

Official test prep 13

Which Statement Is True?

根据官方图像逐条核对

  1. f is undefined at x=1.
  2. f is defined but not continuous at x=2.
  3. f is defined and continuous at x=3.
  • AOnly I
  • BOnly II
  • CI and II
  • DI and III
  • ENone

At x=1, a filled point exists, so I is false.

At x=2, the curve meets at a filled point, so II is false.

x=3 is a vertical asymptote, so III is false.

E · None of the statements are true.三句话都不成立。
Official answer: E14

官方第13题答案 E。提醒 x=1 虽不连续但有定义；x=2 连续；x=3 无定义且为渐近线。
