Directions: Verify f′(c)=0, then use f″(c) when the test is conclusive.
要求：先确认一阶导数为零，再用二阶导数分类。

Fast check: A zero second derivative makes the test inconclusive; it does not mean “neither.”

1. Suppose f′(2)=0 and f″(2)=−7. What can you conclude?3 points
Conclusion / 结论：
Answer: f has a local maximum at x=2, because f″(2)<0.
2. For g(x)=x⁴, explain why the Second Derivative Test is inconclusive at x=0.3 points
Derivative values / 导数值：
Answer: g′(0)=0 and g″(0)=0. The test is inconclusive, although another method shows a local minimum.
3. Find and classify every critical point of h(x)=x³−3x².4 points
Points and classification / 点与分类：
Answer: h′=3x(x−2), so x=0,2. Since h″=6x−6, (0,0) is a local maximum and (2,−4) is a local minimum.

  • Q2: 1 first derivative, 1 second derivative, 1 conclusion.
  • Q3: 1 candidates, 1 second derivative, 2 complete points.
