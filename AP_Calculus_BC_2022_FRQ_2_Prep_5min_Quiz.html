Fast check: Checks parametric slope, speed, acceleration, and integration of velocity.

1. A particle has x′(t)=2t and y′(t)=t². Find the tangent slope and speed at t=2.3 points
Slope is y′/x′; speed is the magnitude of velocity. / 斜率是 y′/x′；速率是速度向量的模。
Answer:

dy/dx=4/4=1; speed=√(4²+4²)=4√2.

2. Differentiate x′(t)=√(1+t²) and y′(t)=ln(2+t²) to give the acceleration vector.3 points
Apply the chain rule to both components. / 两个分量都使用链式法则。
Answer: a(t)=⟨t/√(1+t²), 2t/(2+t²)⟩.
3. A particle has (x(2),y(2))=(1,5), x′(t)=3, and y′(t)=4. Find y(4) and the total distance from t=2 to t=4.4 points
Position uses initial value plus displacement; distance integrates speed. / 坐标 = 初值 + 位移；路程 = 速率积分。
Answer:

y(4)=5+∫₂⁴4dt=13. Speed is 5, so distance =∫₂⁴5dt=10.
