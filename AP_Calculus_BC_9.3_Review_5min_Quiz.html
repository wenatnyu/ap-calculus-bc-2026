Fast check: Checks that students apply the d/dx operator rather than replacing it with a second t-derivative.

1. State the formula for d²y/dx² when x=x(t) and y=y(t).3 points
Formula and condition
Answer: d²y/dx²=[d/dt(dy/dx)]/(dx/dt), with dx/dt≠0 where the formula is used.
2. For x=2t, y=t², compute d²y/dx².3 points
Two derivative steps
Answer: dy/dx=t, so d²y/dx²=(1)/2=1/2.
3. For x=t²+1, y=t³, state where the curve is concave up and concave down in terms of t.4 points
Expression and sign chart
Answer: d²y/dx²=3/(4t) for t≠0. It is concave down for t<0 and concave up for t>0.
