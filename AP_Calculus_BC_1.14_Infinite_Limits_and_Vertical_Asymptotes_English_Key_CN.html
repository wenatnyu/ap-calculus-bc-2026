Meaning · Unbounded behavior

What an Infinite Limit Means

无穷极限描述无界趋势，不是一个实数答案

limx→c+f(x)=+∞

As x approaches c from the right, f(x) grows beyond every positive bound.

从右侧靠近 c 时，函数值可任意大。

limx→c−f(x)=−∞

As x approaches c from the left, f(x) decreases without bound.

从左侧靠近 c 时，函数值无下界。

∞ is not a real number. The notation describes behavior, so use it only when values become unbounded.∞ 描述趋势，不是可直接代入运算的实数。

Finite input target · Unbounded output03

用“beyond any bound”解释，不要把无穷说成“一个很大的数”。

Definition · One infinite side is enough

Definition of a Vertical Asymptote

只要至少一侧无界，就有竖直渐近线

The line x=c is a vertical asymptote if at least one one-sided limit as x→c is +∞ or −∞.

若至少一个单侧极限为 ±∞，则 x=c 是竖直渐近线。

+∞ / +∞

VA and two-sided limit +∞.

−∞ / −∞

VA and two-sided limit −∞.

−∞ / +∞

VA, but two-sided limit DNE.

finite / +∞

VA; two-sided limit DNE.

Asymptote criterion and two-sided-limit criterion are different.04

让学生比较：VA 只需一侧无穷；双侧 +∞ 或 -∞ 则要求两侧同号一致。

Algebra · Hole versus vertical asymptote

Factor Before Naming an Asymptote

分母为零不一定就是竖直渐近线

Canceled denominator factor

A finite nearby rule may remain. This gives a hole.

被约掉的分母因子通常产生空洞。

Surviving denominator factor

If the numerator stays nonzero, the quotient becomes unbounded. This gives a vertical asymptote.

未约掉的分母因子可能产生竖直渐近线。

original denominator zero → factor → cancel? → hole or VA

Never decide from the denominator alone.必须先看分子是否含同一因子。

Official notes example · Classify first

Worked Example: Hole and Vertical Asymptote

先约分分类，再判断渐近线两侧符号

f(x)=x²+2x−8x²+x−12

=(x+4)(x−2)(x+4)(x−3)=x−2x−3

Original restrictions: x≠−4,3.

Hole: x=−4, height 6/7.

Vertical asymptote: x=3.

x→3−: −∞
x→3+: +∞

Near 3, the numerator approaches 1>0; only the denominator changes sign.在 3 附近分子为正，分母左右变号。

Official packet Notes 1-306

官方 notes 用同一函数找 VA 和两侧极限。空洞高度是 (-6)/(-7)=6/7；不要误算符号。

A Reliable Sign-Analysis Routine

无穷极限的符号判断流程

01

Simplify

Factor and cancel removable factors.

先排除空洞。
02

Separate sides

Write c−ε and c+ε.

左右分开。
03

Track signs

Find the sign of every factor.

逐因子判正负。
04

Combine

positive/small positive → +∞, etc.

合并得到 ±∞。

Magnitude comes from the denominator approaching zero; sign comes from all factors.分母趋零决定无界，所有因子共同决定正负。

Multiplicity · Same sign on both sides

Even Multiplicity Keeps the Denominator Sign

偶次因子在渐近线两侧不变号

h(x)=x−3(x−1)²

As x→1: numerator → −2; denominator → 0+ from both sides.

lim x→1− h(x)=−∞

lim x→1+ h(x)=−∞

lim x→1 h(x)=−∞

Vertical asymptote: x=1. The two-sided infinite limit exists in the extended sense because both one-sided signs match.两侧都为负无穷，可写双侧极限为负无穷。

Odd multiplicity often flips sign; even multiplicity does not.09

把平方因子和一次因子对比。这里“exists”指可写一致的无穷极限，不表示结果是实数。

Error analysis · Read the notation

Three Common Errors

三个常见错误

“Denominator zero means VA.”

Not always.

A common factor may cancel and leave a hole.

先约分分类。

“Both sides are infinity.”

Incomplete.

Check whether each side is +∞ or −∞.

必须写正负号。

“DNE means no VA.”

False.

Opposite one-sided infinities still give a vertical asymptote.

双侧极限不存在也可能有 VA。
Direction, sign, and classification are separate decisions.14

让学生各写一个反例。最佳反例：1/(x-c) 两侧异号，双侧 DNE，但 x=c 是 VA。

Exit ticket · No guessing

Signs at a Vertical Asymptote

写出两侧极限、双侧结论和渐近线

q(x)=x+1(x+2)(x−3)

Analyze x→−2− and x→−2+.

Left: −∞. Right: +∞. Two-sided limit: DNE. Vertical asymptote: x=−2. Near −2, x+1<0 and x−3<0; the factor x+2 changes sign.左右异号无穷，双侧极限不存在，但 x=−2 是竖直渐近线。
