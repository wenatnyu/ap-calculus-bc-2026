Read the Model Before Pressing Buttons

先读模型，再按计算器

A(t)=450√(sin(0.62t)), where t is hours after 5 A.M. and A(t) is vehicles/hour.

  • Input: time
  • Output: an instantaneous arrival rate
  • Accumulation over time: integrate the rate

A(t) 不是车辆总数，而是“每小时到达多少辆”。

Part (a): Rate Accumulates to Amount

（a）速率积分得到总量

From 6 A.M. to 10 A.M., the time bounds are t=1 and t=5.

Total arrivals = ∫₁⁵ A(t) dt

Units: (vehicles/hour)(hours) = vehicles.

上下限必须由钟表时间换成题目中的 t。

Part (b): Average Value of a Rate

（b）速率的平均值

Average of A on [1,5] = 1/(5−1) ∫₁⁵ A(t) dt

The denominator is interval length, not an extra unit conversion.

平均值 = 函数在区间上的积分 ÷ 区间长度。

Part (c): Is the Rate Increasing?

（c）到达率在增加吗？

A rate A is increasing at t=1 when A′(1)>0.

A′(t)=139.5 cos(0.62t) / √(sin(0.62t))

A′(1)=148.947272…>0

Conclusion: the arrival rate is increasing at 6 A.M.

判断 A 的增减，要看 A′ 的符号。

Official solution walkthrough

Part (d): Build the Queue Function

（d）建立排队函数

A queue grows when arrivals exceed service capacity 400.

N(t)=∫ₐᵗ[A(x)−400]dx

By FTC, N′(t)=A(t)−400.

  • A>400 ⇒ N′>0: queue grows
  • A<400 ⇒ N′<0: queue shrinks

净变化率 = 到达率 − 处理能力。

Official solution walkthrough

Common Errors to Eliminate

常见错误

  • Using 6 and 10 as t-bounds instead of 1 and 5.
  • Calling the integral an average without dividing by 4.
  • Using degree mode.
  • Testing only one root of A(t)=400.
  • Rounding roots too early.
  • Giving 71 with no absolute-maximum justification.

先写数学结构，再使用计算器。

Quick Check

课堂快检

A station has arrival rate R(t) people/hour and serves 120 people/hour.

  1. Write the average arrival rate on [2,6].
  2. If Q(t)=∫₂ᵗ[R(x)−120]dx, when is Q increasing?

写平均到达率；再用净变化率判断队伍何时增加。

Answer:
  1. (1/4)∫₂⁶R(t)dt.
  2. Q′(t)=R(t)−120, so Q is increasing where R(t)>120.

平均值要除以区间长度；队伍增加看净变化率为正。

Exit ticket

Exit Ticket

离堂题

In one complete sentence, explain how to find the absolute maximum of C(t)=∫ₐᵗ[r(x)−k]dx on [a,d].

用一句完整的话说明如何寻找累积函数的绝对最大值。

Answer:

Use C′(t)=r(t)−k; find all interior critical times where r(t)=k (and any where C′ is undefined), then compare C at those times and the endpoints—or prove the maximum with a complete sign chart.

找临界点与端点并比较，或用完整符号表论证。
