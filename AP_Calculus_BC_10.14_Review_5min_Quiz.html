1. Explain the difference between radius of convergence and interval of convergence.3 points
Meaning / 含义
Answer: The radius R is the distance from the center to the boundary of guaranteed interior convergence. The interval records the actual x-values, including separate endpoint decisions.
2. Find the interval of convergence of Σ[(x−1)/4]n.3 points
Geometric series / 几何级数
Answer: |(x−1)/4|<1 gives −3<x<5. At both endpoints the terms fail to approach zero, so the interval is (−3,5).
3. Find the interval of convergence of Σn=1∞(x+2)n/(n·5n).4 points
Ratio and endpoints / 比值与端点
Answer: Interior: |x+2|<5. At x=3, Σ1/n diverges. At x=−7, Σ(−1)n/n converges. The interval is [−7,3).
