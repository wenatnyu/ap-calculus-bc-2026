Directions: State the hypotheses before using the theorem.
要求：使用定理前先写出成立条件。

Fast check: MVT needs continuity on the closed interval and differentiability on the open interval.

1. Complete the MVT: If f is ______ on [a,b] and ______ on (a,b), then some c∈(a,b) satisfies ______.3 points
Three blanks / 三个空：
Answer: continuous; differentiable; f′(c)=[f(b)−f(a)]/(b−a).
2. For f(x)=x²−4x on [0,4], find every c guaranteed by the MVT.3 points
Secant slope and equation / 割线斜率与方程：
Answer: A polynomial satisfies both hypotheses. The secant slope is 0; solve 2c−4=0 to get c=2.
3. A position function is continuous on [1,5], differentiable on (1,5), and satisfies s(1)=3, s(5)=11. What velocity must occur?4 points
Theorem conclusion / 定理结论：
Answer: The average velocity is [11−3]/[5−1]=2. The MVT guarantees at least one c∈(1,5) with s′(c)=2.
