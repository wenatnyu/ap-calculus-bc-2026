Differentiate Before Substituting

先求导，再代入变化量的数值

A=LW

Wrong:

Replace L and W by instant values before differentiating.

Correct:

A′=L′W+LW′

Then substitute.

A value known at one instant is not necessarily constant over time.

AP Calculus BC · 4.504

这是官方 packet 的 IMPORTANT 提示；区分 instant value 与 constant。

Police-Car Example

警车追车例题

x=0.8,   y=0.6,   z=1

x²+y²=z²

x x′+y y′=z z′

With y′=−60 mph and z′=20 mph:

0.8x′+0.6(−60)=20 ⇒ x′=70 mph

AP Calculus BC · 4.506

警车到路口的距离 y 在减小，所以 y'=-60；两车距离 z 在增大。

Constraint Question

约束条件选择题

The base of a triangle decreases at 0.2 cm/s, height increases at 0.1 cm/s, and b=3 cm.

A′=½(b′h+bh′)=½(−0.2h+0.3)

A′>0 ⇒ −0.2h+0.3>0 ⇒ h<1.5

Answer: D. h<1.5 cm.只有在高度小于1.5厘米时面积增加。

Exit Ticket

离堂检测

A sphere’s radius increases at 2 cm/s. How fast is its volume changing when r=3 cm?

Answer: V=(4/3)πr³, so V′=4πr²r′=4π(9)(2)=72π cm³/s.体积变化率为 72π 立方厘米每秒。
