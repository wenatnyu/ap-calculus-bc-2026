From the Product Rule

由乘积法则推导

(uv)′=u′v+uv′

∫u dv=uv−∫v du

The minus sign comes directly from rearranging the product rule.

One-Step Example

A Hidden Product

隐含的乘积

∫ln x dx = ∫(ln x)(1)dx

u=ln x, dv=dx

=xln x−x+C

When only one function is visible, the second factor may be 1.
