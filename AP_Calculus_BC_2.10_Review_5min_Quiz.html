Directions: Use (f/g)′=(f′g−fg′)/g2. Preserve the order in the numerator.
要求：商法则分子顺序不可颠倒，分母整体平方。

Fast check: Derivative of the top times the bottom, minus the top times the derivative of the bottom, over the bottom squared.

1. Differentiate h(x)=(x2+1)/(x−1).3 points
Quotient rule / 商法则：
Answer: h′(x)=[2x(x−1)−(x2+1)]/(x−1)2=(x2−2x−1)/(x−1)2.
2. At x=1, suppose f=2, f′=3, g=−1, g′=4. Find q′(1) for q=f/g.3 points
Substitute all four values / 代入四个值：
Answer: q′(1)=[(3)(−1)−(2)(4)]/(−1)2=−11.
3. Find the tangent line to y=(sin x)/x at x=π/2.4 points
Point and slope / 点与斜率：
Answer: The point is (π/2,2/π). Since y′=[x cos x−sin x]/x2, the slope is −4/π2. Thus y−2/π=−(4/π2)(x−π/2).
