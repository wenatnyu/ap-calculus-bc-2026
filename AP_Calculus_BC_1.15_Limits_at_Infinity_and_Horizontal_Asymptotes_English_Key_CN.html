Notation · Which quantity is unbounded?

Do Not Confuse Two Kinds of Infinity

看清无穷出现在输入还是输出

Infinite limit

limx→cf(x)=+∞

Input approaches a finite c; output is unbounded.

有限输入附近的竖直渐近行为。

Limit at infinity

limx→+∞f(x)=L

Input is unbounded; output may approach a finite L.

研究图像右端或左端的趋势。

Today: infinity appears under the limit operator with x.今天研究 x→±∞。

Definition · Check the two ends separately

Definition of a Horizontal Asymptote

正无穷端和负无穷端分别判断

The line y=L is a horizontal asymptote if lim x→+∞ f(x)=L or lim x→−∞ f(x)=L.

只要一个端的有限极限为 L，y=L 就是水平渐近线。

Same at both ends

One horizontal asymptote.

两端相同。

Different end limits

Two horizontal asymptotes are possible.

两端可有不同渐近线。

One finite end

One horizontal asymptote is still possible.

只需一个端有限。

Crossing

A graph may cross a horizontal asymptote.

图像可以穿过水平渐近线。
Horizontal asymptotes describe end behavior, not a barrier.04

纠正常见误解：水平渐近线不是图像不能穿过的线；定义只看端行为。

Growth hierarchy · Dominant terms

Which Functions Grow Faster?

无穷远处只保留增长最快的部分

logarithm < power < exponential

ln x grows slower than any positive power of x; a positive-base exponential with base greater than 1 eventually outgrows every power.

Worked example · Every term vanishes

Case 1: Denominator Degree Is Larger

分母次数更高：极限为 0

limx→∞x+43x²−5

=limx→∞1/x+4/x²3−5/x²=0

Horizontal asymptote: y=0.

The denominator grows faster than the numerator.分母增长更快，商趋近 0。

Case 2: Equal Degrees

次数相同：取首项系数之比

f(x)=(x+5)(x−2)(4x−3)²

Leading terms: x² and 16x².

lim x→±∞ f(x)=116

Horizontal asymptote: y=1/16.

Lower-degree terms do not affect the finite end limit.低次项不影响端行为的有限极限。

Critical identity · √(x²)=|x|

Radicals: The Absolute-Value Trap

根式题必须保留绝对值和方向符号

√(4x²+x−2)3x−1

=|x|√(4+1/x−2/x²)x(3−1/x)

=|x|x·√(4+1/x−2/x²)3−1/x

x→+∞: +2/3

x→−∞: −2/3

Two HAs: y=2/3 and y=−2/3.因为 |x|/x 在两端符号不同。

Never replace √(x²) by x without checking the direction.10

本节最重要易错点。让学生背：√(x²)=|x|；正无穷时 |x|/x=1，负无穷时等于 -1。

Radical growth · Both ends may agree

Even Powers Under a Radical

根号内最高次为四次时，两端可同号

h(x)=√(4x⁴+x−2)3x²−1

√(x⁴)=|x²|=x²

h(x)≈2x²3x²=2/3

lim x→±∞ h(x)=2/3

One HA: y=2/3.

Why no sign change? x²≥0 on both ends, so |x²|=x².x² 两端都非负，因此不出现正负号翻转。

Official packet Notes 911

与上一页对比：√(x²)=|x| 会翻方向；√(x⁴)=x² 不翻方向。

Beyond rational functions · Use known behavior

Exponential and Trigonometric End Behavior

指数函数与三角函数要看增长、衰减和振荡

Exponential decay

lim x→∞ 5e−x=0

e−x=1/eˣ.

指数衰减到 0。

Composition

lim x→∞ −4e1/x=−4

1/x→0, then continuity gives e⁰=1.

先求内层极限。

Oscillation

lim x→∞ sin x = DNE

Bounded does not mean convergent.

有界振荡不等于有极限。

Squeeze example: |sin x/x|≤1/|x|→0, so sin x/x→0 as x→±∞.有界分子除以无界分母可用夹逼定理。

Different ends · Exponential switch

Two Different Horizontal Asymptotes

左右两端可以趋近不同常数

f(x)=5+2ˣ1−2ˣ

x→+∞: 2ˣ−2ˣ→−1

x→−∞: 2ˣ→0, so f(x)→5

Right-end HA: y=−1.

Left-end HA: y=5.

One function may have two horizontal asymptotes.两个端分别判断。

End behavior can be asymmetric.14

这题能检验学生是否分别处理正负无穷。x→-∞ 时 2^x 不是无穷，而是 0。

Exit ticket · Rational, radical, exponential

Three End-Behavior Decisions

三种结构，分别写极限与水平渐近线

A. lim x→∞ 6x²−13x²+5
B. lim x→−∞ √(9x²+1)2x−7
C. 2+3ˣ1+3ˣ
Find both HAs.
A: 2; HA y=2. B: −3/2; HA y=−3/2 on the left. Use √(x²)=|x|. C: y=1 as x→+∞ and y=2 as x→−∞.A 取系数比；B 保留 |x|；C 两端分别判断。
