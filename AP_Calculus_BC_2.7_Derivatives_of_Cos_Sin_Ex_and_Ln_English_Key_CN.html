Trig derivatives · Radians only

Sine and Cosine

正弦与余弦的导数循环

sin x → cos x

cos x → −sin x

Why radians?

The clean formulas above are true when x is measured in radians.

In degree mode, an extra conversion factor appears.微积分三角函数默认使用弧度制。

Sign cue: sine differentiates to positive cosine; cosine differentiates to negative sine.口头读出正负号，减少漏号。

sin → cos → −sin → −cos → sin05

可带学生顺时针念一遍四步循环。本单元所有精确三角值按单位圆与弧度制处理。

The base matters

Exponential Derivatives

底数决定是否出现 ln a

Natural base

(ex)′=ex

Because ln e=1.函数形式不变。

General base

(ax)′=axln a

Example: (5x)′=5xln5.别漏乘 ln a。

e0=1, so evaluating an ex derivative at x=0 is often simple.

The exponent is x; this is not the power rule.06

对比 x^5 与 5^x：前者变量在底数，后者变量在指数。不要把 5^x 的导数写成 5x^4。

Natural log and other bases

Logarithmic Derivatives

自然对数与一般底数对数

(ln x)′=1x

(logax)′=1x ln a

Domain comes first

For real-valued logarithms, x>0.

The derivative formula does not erase the original domain restriction.求导后仍保留原函数定义域。

ln 0 is undefined. Never substitute zero into ln x or 1/x.

Natural log means base e.07

强调 log_a x 的导数是 1/(x ln a)，不是 1/(x ln x)。定义域 x>0 要保留。

Use Unit-Circle Values
