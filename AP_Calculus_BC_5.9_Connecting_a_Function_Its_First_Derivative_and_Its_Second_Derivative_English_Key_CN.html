Keep the questions separate

Two Independent Decisions

运动方向与速率变化是两件事

v>0

moving right/up

v<0

moving left/down

va>0

speeding up

va<0

slowing down

An object can move left while speeding up: v<0 and a<0.向左运动不等于减速。

When x-Spacing Is Unequal

Calculator-active reasoning

Maximize the First Derivative

增长最快等价于一阶导数最大

1

Compute or graph f′.

2

Find candidates where f′′=0 or DNE.

3

Compare values of f′, including endpoints.

f′′=0 only gives a candidate for an extremum of f′.仍需比较或检查符号。

AP-style motion

Choose the Speeding-Up Set

判断加速区间

v(t)=(t−1)(t−4),   a(t)=2t−5,   t>0

On which set of intervals is the particle speeding up?

  1. A(0,1)∪(5/2,4)
  2. B(1,5/2)∪(4,∞)
  3. C(0,5/2)
  4. D(5/2,∞)
Answer: B. Velocity and acceleration are both negative on (1,5/2) and both positive on (4,∞).同号区间为 (1,5/2) 与 (4,∞)。

Exit ticket · Two derivatives

Intersect the Intervals

求两个条件的交集

f(x)=x3−3x2

Where is f both decreasing and concave up?

Answer: f′=3x(x−2)<0 on (0,2); f′′=6(x−1)>0 on (1,∞). Therefore the answer is (1,2).分别求区间后取交集。
