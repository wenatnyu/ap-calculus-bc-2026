Finding Antiderivatives and Indefinite Integrals: Basic Rules and Notation

求原函数与不定积分：基本法则和符号

∫f(x)dx=F(x)+C
because F′(x)=f(x)

An indefinite integral names the entire family of antiderivatives.不定积分表示全部原函数，因此必须写积分常数 C。

对数情形

∫(1/x)dx=ln|x|+C

Rewrite Before Integrating

先改写后积分

∫(3x²+x−2)/x dx

=∫(3x+1−2/x)dx

=(3/2)x²+x−2ln|x|+C

Separate simple terms only after valid algebra.

Exit ticket · One clean solution

Exit Ticket

离堂检测

Find the particular solution if y′=2x+3 and y(0)=5.

Answer: y=x²+3x+C; y(0)=5 gives C=5, so y=x²+3x+5.先求通解，再代初值确定 C。
