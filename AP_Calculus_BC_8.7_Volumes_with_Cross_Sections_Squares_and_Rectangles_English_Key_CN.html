AP Calculus BC · Topic 8.7

Volumes with Cross Sections: Squares and Rectangles

正方形与矩形截面体积

V = ∫ A(x) dx or V = ∫ A(y) dy

The base region determines a cross-sectional length; the stated shape turns that length into area. / 底面区域给出截面长度，再由截面形状写出面积。

Read “Perpendicular To”

Core lesson

Rectangular Cross Sections

矩形截面

A(x)=base(x)·height(x)

If height=k·base, then A(x)=k[base(x)]².

Square Example

正方形示例

Base: y=x and y=x², 0≤x≤1

V=∫₀¹(x−x²)²dx=1/30

Core lesson

Rectangle Ratio Example

矩形比例示例

If the in-base segment has length w(x) and rectangle height is twice its base,

A(x)=w(x)[2w(x)]=2w(x)²

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 809

Do not confuse a 2:1 side ratio with an extra factor after integration.

Units and Reasonableness

单位与合理性

Core lesson

Common Modeling Errors

常见建模错误

  • Integrating length instead of area.
  • Forgetting to square a square’s side.
  • Using the wrong orientation.
  • Mixing x-bounds with a dy integral.

Cross-Section Checklist

截面体积检查表

  • Sketch the base.
  • Match slice orientation to dx or dy.
  • Find the base length.
  • Write the shape area A.
  • Integrate over the base interval.
  • Report cubic units.

Quick Check

课堂即时检测

A solid has square cross sections perpendicular to the x-axis with side length 2−x for 0≤x≤2. Find its volume.

Answer: V=∫₀²(2−x)²dx=8/3 cubic units.
