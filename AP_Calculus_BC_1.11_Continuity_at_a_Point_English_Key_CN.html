Official notes · Formal definition

Three Conditions for Continuity at x=c

三条必须同时满足

01

The point exists

f(c) is defined.

c 在定义域内。
02

The limit exists

limx→c f(x) exists.

左右极限相等，双侧极限存在。
03

They agree

limx→c f(x)=f(c)

附近趋势等于点值。

All three are required. Passing only one or two checks is not enough.缺一不可。

Defined · Limit exists · Equal03

让学生按 1-2-3 顺序背诵。第2条内部包含左右极限相等。

Use a Three-Row Checklist

CheckEvidence to writePass?

If every row says yes, the function is continuous at c.三行全部通过，才连续。

If a row says no, name that failed condition in the justification.理由要指出失败的是哪一条。

Official notes 1 · Check two boundaries

One Piecewise Function, Two Decisions

同一分段函数在不同交界点可能有不同结论

f(x)= x²−2x+1x<−1      x+2−1≤x<2      2ˣx≥2

At x=−1: left limit 4; right limit and point value 1. Not continuous - jump.

At x=2: left limit 4; right limit and point value 4. Continuous.

Official notes 105

在 -1 代左右表达式分别得4和1；在2两边都得4。提醒分段不等号决定点值来自哪一段。

Official notes 2 · Unequal one-sided limits

A Jump at the Boundary

左右极限不相等，第二条直接失败

f(x)=3x+1x<4      x/2−1x≥4

limx→4⁻f(x)=13

limx→4⁺f(x)=1=f(4)

Not continuous at x=4. The two-sided limit does not exist because 13≠1.左右极限不同，是跳跃间断。

Official notes 206

虽然 f(4) 有定义，但不能只凭第一条判连续。第2条失败。

Official notes 3 · Limit and point disagree

A Removable Discontinuity

极限存在，但点值放错位置

g(x)=x²+2x−1x<−1      x−1x>−1      5x=−1

left limit = −2

right limit = −2

g(−1)=5

Condition 3 fails: −2≠5. The discontinuity is a hole at x=−1.把点值改为 −2 即可修复。

Official notes 307

前两条成立：点值有定义且极限存在。只有极限不等于点值，因此是可去间断。

Official notes 4 · Match the pieces

Choose k to Make the Function Continuous

让交界处两段的值相等

h(x)=2−x²x≤−2      4x+kx>−2

2−(−2)²=4(−2)+k

−2=−8+k

k=6. Then the left limit, right limit, and h(−2) all equal −2.代回后左右极限与点值均为 −2。

For standard continuous pieces, match their boundary outputs.各分段内部已连续，只需在交界处衔接。

Official notes 408

强调右段虽不含 -2，但右极限仍通过把 -2 代入表达式得到。

Connect definition to discontinuity type

Which Condition Failed?

从失败条件反推间断类型

Facts at x=cFailed conditionTypical type
f(c) undefined; finite limit exists1hole
left limit ≠ right limit2jump
unbounded on a side2vertical asymptote
finite limit exists but ≠ f(c)3hole

A hole can occur because the point is missing or because it has the wrong value.空洞可能是点值缺失，也可能是点值错误。

The definition explains the classification.09

让学生注意 hole 有两种图像表现，但都可通过修改一个点修复。

Official practice 1 · Two boundaries again

Check x=−1 and x=2

分别计算左右极限与点值

f(x)=1/(x+4)x≤−1      3ˣ−1<x<2      x²−1x≥2

At x=−1: both sides and f(−1) equal 1/3. Continuous.

At x=2: left limit 9; right limit and f(2) equal 3. Not continuous - jump.

Official practice 110

官方答案：-1 连续，2 不连续。3^(-1)=1/3，3²=9，2²-1=3。

Official practice 2 · Matching limit, wrong point

The Sides Agree, but the Point Does Not

双侧极限存在仍不一定连续

g(x)=x−x²x<1      ln xx>1      xx=1

left limit = 0

right limit = 0

g(1)=1

Not continuous at x=1. The limit is 0, but the point value is 1.条件3失败，是可去间断。

Official practice 211

官方解答把左右极限都算为0，而 g(1)=1。强调 ln x 从右趋近1时趋近0。

Official practice 3 · Different outcomes

At One Boundary: Jump; at the Other: Continuous

同一道题要逐个交界点判断

f(x)=x²+2x−4x<−3      1ˣ−3≤x≤4      17−x²x>4

At x=−3: left limit −1; right limit and value 1. Jump.

At x=4: both sides and point value equal 1. Continuous.

Official practice 312

Exit ticket · Official CA #1 question 5

Find the Continuous Value of k

用三条件完成参数题

g(x)={ x²+6, x≤2
x/3+k, x>2 }

2²+6=23+k

k=28/3. Then both one-sided limits and g(2) equal 10.10=2/3+k，所以 k=28/3。
Point defined · Limit exists · They agree
