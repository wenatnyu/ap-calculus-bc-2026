Fast check: Velocity gives direction; matching signs of velocity and acceleration mean speeding up.

1. For s(t)=t³−3t², find v(t) and a(t).3 points
Derivatives / 导数：
Answer: v(t)=3t²−6t and a(t)=6t−6.
2. At an instant, v=−2 m/s and a=5 m/s². State the direction and whether the particle is speeding up.3 points
Signs and motion / 符号与运动：
Answer: The particle moves left and slows down because velocity and acceleration have opposite signs.
3. A position function s(t), measured in meters with t in seconds, has s(0)=2 and s(4)=10. Find displacement and average velocity on [0,4].4 points
Endpoint change and rate / 端点变化与变化率：
Answer: Displacement: 10−2=8 m. Average velocity: 8/(4−0)=2 m/s.
