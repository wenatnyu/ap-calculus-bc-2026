AP Calculus BC · Topic 5.4

Using the First Derivative Test to Determine Relative Extrema

用一阶导数检验判断局部极值

f′: + → − gives a local maximum

f′: − → + gives a local minimum

A local extremum comes from a change in the sign of the derivative.局部极值来自导数符号的改变。

Sign-change rules

The First Derivative Test

只看临界点两侧的 f′ 符号

Sign of f′ near cBehavior of fConclusion at c
+ → −increasing → decreasingrelative maximum
− → +decreasing → increasingrelative minimum
+ → + or − → −same directionneither

The test also works when f′(c) does not exist, provided f is continuous at c and the derivative has the stated signs on both sides.尖点处还要确认函数连续，并检查两侧导数符号。

Before → after03

强调读序从左到右。符号改变才有极值；若导数在 c 不存在，还必须确认 f 在 c 连续。

An Exponential Factor Is Always Positive

A Quadratic Derivative

正、负、正对应最大再最小

Graph of the derivative

Crossings of the x-axis Matter

看 f′ 穿过 x 轴的方向

Crosses downward

f′:+→−

f has a local maximum.

Crosses upward

f′:−→+

f has a local minimum.

Touches and turns

No sign change.

f has neither.

Do not classify the peaks of the graph of f′; classify its sign changes.不要把导数图自己的极值当成原函数极值。

Crossing direction → extremum type12

在导数图上，学生常盯着“峰谷”。引导他们只看与 x 轴的相对位置及穿越方向。

Extrema in context

A Maximum Means the Quantity Changes Direction

情境题也要引用变化率符号

If Q is continuous at t=6, with Q′(t)>0 just before 6 and Q′(t)<0 just after 6, then Q has a local maximum at t=6.

Interpret the result with units: “The amount is locally greatest at 6 minutes.”结论写回原情境并带单位。

The rate is zero or undefined at the turning time; the amount itself need not be zero.

Derivative evidence + contextual conclusion13

强调连续性不可省略，且 Q′=0 不代表 Q=0。情境结论的主语必须是原数量 Q。

Official test prep · Unknown negative factor

Use the Sign of an Unknown Function

已知 g<0，会翻转多项式的符号

For every x, g(x)<0. If f′(x)=(x²−x−12)g(x), what extrema does f have?

  • A.max at −3; min at 4
  • B.min at −3; max at 4
  • C.mins at −3 and 4
  • D.neither point
B. Since x²−x−12=(x−4)(x+3) and g<0, the signs of f′ are −,+,−. Thus f has a minimum at −3 and a maximum at 4.负因子使整个符号表翻转。
Factor signs before naming extrema.14

官方 test-prep 题，答案 B。先画二次式的 +,-,+，再因 g<0 全部反号为 -,+,-。
