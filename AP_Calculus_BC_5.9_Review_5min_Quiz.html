Directions: Translate signs and zeros of a derivative into the behavior of the original function.
要求：把导函数的符号与零点翻译成原函数的增减与极值。

Fast check: The height of f′ gives the slope of f; a sign change of f′ classifies a turning point.

1. If f′>0 on (−∞,−1), f′<0 on (−1,3), and f′>0 on (3,∞), identify the local extrema of f.3 points
Sign changes / 符号变化：
Answer: f has a local maximum at x=−1 and a local minimum at x=3.
2. For g(x)=x3−3x, state where the graph is increasing and decreasing.3 points
Use g′ / 使用导数：
Answer: g′=3(x−1)(x+1). Thus g increases on (−∞,−1)∪(1,∞) and decreases on (−1,1).
3. Given h′(x)=x²−4, describe the local extrema of h and give one possible formula for h.4 points
Shape and antiderivative / 形状与原函数：
Answer: The sign of h′ is +, −, +, so h has a local maximum at x=−2 and a local minimum at x=2. One possible function is h=x³/3−4x.
