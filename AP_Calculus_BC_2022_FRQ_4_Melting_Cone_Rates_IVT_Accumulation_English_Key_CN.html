Read the Data Before Calculating

先读表格

t (days): 0, 3, 7, 10, 12

r′(t) (cm/day): −6.1, −5.0, −4.4, −3.8, −3.5

  • r′ is negative: the radius is shrinking.
  • r′ becomes less negative: the shrinking is slowing.
  • At t=3, r′(3)=−5.

负号表示半径在减小；数值逐渐接近 0。

Official solution walkthrough

Part (a) · Derivative from a Table

用平均变化率估计二阶导

Approximate r″(8.5) with the average rate of change of r′ on [7,10].

r″(8.5) ≈ [r′(10)−r′(7)]/(10−7).

8.5 是区间中点；分子仍是“后值减前值”。

2022 BC Free-Response Practice04

Emphasize that the problem explicitly asks for the average rate of change of r′. Students should not try to differentiate isolated table values.

Part (a) · Work, Value, Units

过程、数值与单位

[−3.8−(−4.4)]/3 = 0.6/3 = 0.2 cm/day².

  • Show the difference and quotient.
  • Attach units to the value.
  • An unsimplified correct quotient is acceptable.

Part (b) · An IVT Checklist

介值定理三件套

  1. Continuity: r is twice differentiable, so r′ is continuous.
  2. Bracketing: r′(0)=−6.1<−6<−5=r′(3).
  3. Conclusion: some t∈(0,3) has r′(t)=−6.

连续性 + 夹住目标值 + 明确结论。

2022 BC Free-Response Practice06

Require all three ingredients. Merely naming IVT is not a justification, and continuity should be tied to the stated twice-differentiability.

Part (b) · Write the Argument

完整书写模板

Because r is twice differentiable, r′ is continuous on [0,3]. Since r′(0)=−6.1<−6<−5.0=r′(3), the IVT guarantees a t in (0,3) for which r′(t)=−6.

不要只写 “Yes, by IVT.”

Part (c) · Right Riemann Sum

右端点黎曼和

∫₀¹² r′(t)dt ≈ 3r′(3)+4r′(7)+3r′(10)+2r′(12)

=3(−5)+4(−4.4)+3(−3.8)+2(−3.5)

=−51.

结果是半径的净变化，负值合理。

Part (d) · Build the Rate Model

相关变化率建模

V=(1/3)πr²h, where both r and h depend on t.

dV/dt=(π/3)[2rh(dr/dt)+r²(dh/dt)].

r²h 是乘积；r² 求导还要链式法则。

2022 BC Free-Response Practice10

Differentiate symbolically first. Both r and h vary; the phrase “maintains a conical shape” does not give a fixed ratio between them.

Part (d) · Substitute with Signs

代入时保留符号

At t=3:

r=100, h=50, dr/dt=−5, dh/dt=−2.

dV/dt=(π/3)[2(100)(50)(−5)+(100)²(−2)]

=−70,000π/3 cm³/day.

Common Errors to Eliminate

常见失分点

  • Dropping a negative sign in −3.8−(−4.4).
  • Writing cm/day instead of cm/day².
  • Naming IVT without continuity and bracketing.
  • Using four equal widths in the Riemann sum.
  • Treating r or h as constant in V=(1/3)πr²h.
  • Inventing a relationship such as r=2h.

A table gives p′(2)=−4.8 and p′(6)=−3.2. Approximate p″(4) and state its units if p is measured in meters and t in seconds.

Exit Ticket

离堂题

For V=(1/3)πr²h, suppose r=6, h=10, dr/dt=−1, and dh/dt=−2. Find dV/dt.

先求导，再代入；两个变量都在变化。

dV/dt=(π/3)[2(6)(10)(−1)+6²(−2)]

= (π/3)(−120−72)=−64π.
