1. For dP/dt=0.2P(1−P/500), identify both equilibrium populations and the carrying capacity.3 points
Equilibria / 平衡值
Answer: The equilibria are P=0 and P=500; the carrying capacity is 500.
2. For P(t)=400/(1+3e−0.5t), find P(0) and limt→∞P(t).3 points
Initial and limiting values / 初值与极限
Answer: P(0)=100 and limt→∞P(t)=400.
3. For dP/dt=0.1P(1−P/800), at what population is growth fastest, and what is that maximum rate?4 points
Maximum growth / 最大增长
Answer: Growth is fastest at P=400, where dP/dt=0.1(400)(1/2)=20 population units per unit time.
