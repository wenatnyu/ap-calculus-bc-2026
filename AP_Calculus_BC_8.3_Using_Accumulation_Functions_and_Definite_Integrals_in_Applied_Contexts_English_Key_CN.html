Core lesson

Change Versus Total

变化量与总量

  • ∫ₐᵇR(t)dt: net change from a to b.
  • Q(a)+∫ₐᵇR(t)dt: amount at time b.
  • ∫ₐᵇ|R(t)|dt: total variation, only when that quantity is asked for.

A Direct Model

直接建模

Q(0)=10, R(t)=4+t ⇒ Q(3)=10+∫₀³(4+t)dt=53/2

The answer is 26.5 units of the accumulated quantity.

Derivative of an Accumulator

累积函数的导数

A(x)=A(a)+∫axR(t)dt ⇒ A′(x)=R(x)

Quick Check

课堂即时检测

A tank contains 20 liters at t=0. Water enters at 5 liters/min and leaves at t liters/min. How much water is present at t=3?

Answer: 20+∫₀³(5−t)dt=20+15−9/2=61/2 liters.

Exit Ticket

离堂检测

The rate R(t) of people entering a museum is in people/hour. Interpret ∫₂⁶R(t)dt.

Answer: It is the total number of people who enter from hour 2 to hour 6, measured in people (assuming R is an entry rate and hence nonnegative).
