Directions: Show the one-half factor, squared radius, and a justified tracing interval.
要求：写出二分之一、半径平方，并说明描迹区间。

Fast check: Check the polar-area formula, zero-to-zero petal bounds, and full-curve tracing.

1. Find the area swept by r=2 for 0≤θ≤π/2.3 points
Sector area / 扇形面积
Answer: A=(1/2)∫₀^{π/2}4dθ=π.
2. Find the area of the petal of r=2cos(2θ) centered on the positive x-axis.3 points
One petal / 一片花瓣
Answer: The petal is traced on [−π/4,π/4], so A=(1/2)∫−π/4π/44cos²(2θ)dθ=π/2.
3. Find the total area enclosed by r=1+cosθ.4 points
Full cardioid / 完整心形线
Answer: The curve is traced once on [0,2π]. A=(1/2)∫₀^{2π}(1+cosθ)²dθ=3π/2.

Scoring / 评分

  • Q1: one-half, radius squared, and correct interval.
  • Q2: correct one-petal bounds and exact evaluation.
  • Q3: one full trace and correct expansion/integration.
  • Do not accept a 0 to 2π interval that retraces a curve without adjustment.
