Directions: Build units from the function definition and use nearby data to estimate rates.
要求：根据函数定义写单位，并用邻近数据估计变化率。

Fast check: First decide whether the function is a quantity or already a rate.

1. F(t) is volume in gallons and t is minutes. State the units of F″(t).3 points
Units:
Answer: gallons/minute². 加仑/分钟²。
2. A table gives P(4)=20 and P(6)=28, where P is people and t is years. Estimate P′(5).3 points
Difference quotient and units:
Answer: P′(5)≈(28−20)/(6−4)=4 people/year.
3. R(t) is an arrival rate in customers/hour, and t is measured in hours. Interpret R′(2)=12.4 points
Complete sentence:
Answer: At hour 2, the customer-arrival rate is increasing at 12 customers/hour². 第2小时时，到店速率每小时增加12位顾客/小时。
