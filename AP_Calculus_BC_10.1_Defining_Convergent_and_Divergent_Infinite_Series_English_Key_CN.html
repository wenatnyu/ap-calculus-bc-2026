Defining Convergent and Divergent Infinite Series

定义收敛与发散的无穷级数

SN=Σn=1Nan; Σan converges when limN→∞SN is finite.

An infinite series is defined by the long-run behavior of its partial sums. / 无穷级数由部分和的极限来定义。

Core lesson

Sequence or Series?

数列还是级数

A sequence lists terms: a1, a2, ... . A series adds them: Σn=1∞an.

The series is a new object defined through its partial sums.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1003

Insist on the verb distinction: a sequence has terms; a series has partial sums.

Core lesson

Definition of Convergence

收敛的定义

If limN→∞SN=L, then Σan converges to L.

If that finite limit does not exist, the series diverges.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1005

The sum L is the limit of partial sums, not the limit of the individual terms.

Core lesson

Terms Are Not Partial Sums

通项不等于部分和

aN is one contribution; SN is the total of the first N contributions.

For N>1, aN=SN-SN-1.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1006

Use a numerical example to prevent students from using a term limit as the series sum.

Core lesson

A Telescoping Example

裂项相消示例

Σn=1∞1/[n(n+1)]

Since 1/[n(n+1)]=1/n-1/(n+1), SN=1-1/(N+1), so the sum is 1.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1007

Write out the first four terms and circle the cancellation; only the boundary terms survive.

Core lesson

Divergence by Unbounded Sums

部分和无界导致发散

For Σn=1∞1, the partial sums are SN=N.

Because SN→∞ rather than a finite number, the series diverges.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1008

Clarify that tending to infinity is divergence, not convergence to infinity.

Divergence by Oscillation

部分和振荡导致发散

For 1-1+1-1+..., the partial sums alternate between 1 and 0.

No single limit exists, so the series diverges.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1009

Separate oscillating terms from oscillating partial sums; here both fail to settle.

A Necessary Term Condition

通项趋零是必要条件

Σan convergent ⇒ limn→∞an=0.

The converse is false: an→0 alone does not prove convergence.

Core lesson

Finite Changes Do Not Matter

有限项不影响收敛性

Adding, removing, or changing finitely many terms can change the sum.

It cannot change whether the infinite series converges or diverges.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1011

Explain that convergence is controlled by the tail, while the numerical sum includes every term.

Core lesson

Monotone and Bounded Partial Sums

部分和单调且有界

A monotone increasing sequence that is bounded above converges; a monotone decreasing sequence bounded below also converges.

For a positive-term series, SN increases, so it converges exactly when the partial sums are bounded above.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1012

Connect the recalled sequence theorem to the sequence of partial sums; monotonicity alone does not guarantee a finite limit.

Convergence Workflow

收敛定义流程

  1. Write SN.
  2. Simplify the finite sum.
  3. Compute lim SN.
  4. Name the conclusion and, if convergent, the sum.

Quick Check

Exit Ticket

离堂检测

A series has partial sums SN=(-1)N+2. Does it converge?

Answer: No. The partial sums oscillate between 1 and 3, so no limit exists.
