5-Minute Review: Limits from Tables

教师答案与评分标准
TEACHER KEY
10 points

Timing: about 1 minute per question, with 2 minutes available for Question 2 or 4. The quiz checks whether students read the direction of approach before using nearby output values.

1. Estimate limx→2f(x).2 points
x1.91.991.9992.0012.012.1
f(x)5.725.9725.9976.0036.0286.28
Answer: 6. Values from the left and right both approach 6. 左右两侧都趋近 6。
2. Find both one-sided limits, then decide whether the two-sided limit exists.3 points
x3.93.993.9994.0014.014.1
g(x)1.801.9801.9983.0013.0103.10
Left-hand limit: 2. Right-hand limit: 3. Therefore limx→4g(x) is DNE. 两个单侧极限都存在，但不相等。
3. Estimate limx→−2+h(x).2 points
x−2.10−2.01−2.001−1.999−1.99−1.90
h(x)7.217.0207.0026.9986.9806.80
Answer: 7. Use only the columns where x>−2: 6.998, 6.980, 6.80. 右极限只看 −2 右侧。
4. Estimate limx→1cos(p(x)).3 points
x0.900.990.9991.0011.011.10
p(x)1.801.9801.9982.0022.0202.20
Answer: cos(2) ≈ −0.416. First read p(x)→2; then evaluate cosine in radians. 先从表格读出内层趋近 2，再计算 cos 2。
