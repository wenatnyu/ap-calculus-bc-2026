Topic 6.6 · Core lesson

Zero and Reverse

零区间与反向

∫ₐᵃ f(x)dx=0

∫ᵦᵃ f(x)dx=−∫ₐᵦ f(x)dx

Only the orientation changes; the integrand stays the same.

Split an Interval

拆分区间

∫ₐᶜ f + ∫cᵇ f = ∫ₐᵇ f

for c between a and b

Adjacent pieces combine when their orientations match.

Topic 6.6 · Core lesson

Linearity

线性性质

∫ₐᵇ [cf(x)+dg(x)]dx

=c∫ₐᵇf(x)dx+d∫ₐᵇg(x)dx

Constants factor out; sums and differences split.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 605

Known-Value Example

利用已知积分值

∫₀³f=5, ∫₃⁷f=−2

∫₀⁷f=5+(−2)=3

∫₇³f=2

Match interval direction before doing arithmetic.

Combine Functions

组合函数

∫₀³f=5, ∫₀³g=−1

∫₀³(4f−2g)=4(5)−2(−1)=22

Apply coefficients to integral values, not to the bounds.

Even Symmetry

偶函数对称性

f(−x)=f(x)

∫₋ₐᵃ f(x)dx=2∫₀ᵃ f(x)dx

Left and right signed areas match.

Odd Symmetry

奇函数对称性

f(−x)=−f(x)

∫₋ₐᵃ f(x)dx=0

Opposite signed areas cancel on a symmetric interval.

Topic 6.6 · Core lesson

Comparison

积分比较

if f(x)≤g(x) on [a,b]

then ∫ₐᵇf(x)dx≤∫ₐᵇg(x)dx

For a≤b, ordering of functions carries to their integrals.

Absolute-Value Bound

绝对值估计

|∫ₐᵇ f(x)dx| ≤ ∫ₐᵇ |f(x)|dx

Net change cannot exceed total accumulated magnitude.

Topic 6.6 · Core lesson

A Common Non-Property

常见错误

∫ f(x)g(x)dx ≠ (∫f)(∫g)

∫ f(x)/g(x)dx ≠ (∫f)/(∫g)

Linearity applies to sums and constant multiples only.

AP-style quick check

Quick Check

课堂即时检测

If ∫₀³f=5 and ∫₃⁷f=−2, find ∫₀⁷f.

  1. A−7
  2. B−3
  3. C3
  4. D7
Answer: C. Add adjacent intervals: 5+(−2)=3.相邻区间可直接相加。

Exit Ticket

离堂检测

Given ∫₀³f=5 and ∫₀³g=−1, evaluate ∫₀³(4f−2g)dx.

Answer: 4(5)−2(−1)=22.用线性性质分别处理常数倍。
