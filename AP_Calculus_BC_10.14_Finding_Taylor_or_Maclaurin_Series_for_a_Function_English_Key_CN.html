Core lesson

The Infinite Taylor Formula

无穷泰勒级数公式

Σn=0∞ f(n)(c)/n! · (x−c)n

When c=0, this is a Maclaurin series.

The finite polynomial PN stops after degree N; the series continues indefinitely.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1003

Having derivatives of all orders allows the formal Taylor series to be formed, but equality with the function requires convergence to that function.

Core lesson

Four Core Maclaurin Series

四个基本麦克劳林级数

ex=Σxn/n!, all real x

sin x=Σ(−1)nx2n+1/(2n+1)!, all real x

cos x=Σ(−1)nx2n/(2n)!, all real x

1/(1−x)=Σxn, |x|<1

Substitution into eˣ

在 eˣ 级数中代换

Replace x by 2x² and divide by 2:

e2x²/2=1/2+x²+x⁴+(2/3)x⁶+⋯

In general, e2x²/2=(1/2)Σ(2x²)n/n!.

Core lesson

Substitution into sin x

在 sin x 级数中代换

For 2sin(x³), replace every x in the sine series by x³:

2x³−2x⁹/3!+2x¹⁵/5!−2x²¹/7!+⋯

General term: Σ2(−1)nx6n+3/(2n+1)!.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1007

The exponent is 3(2n+1)=6n+3. This exact function appears in the official end review.

Core lesson

A Taylor Series about x=2

以 x=2 为中心的泰勒级数

Every derivative of ex at 2 equals e².

ex=e²Σn=0∞(x−2)n/n!.

This converges for all real x.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1010

Do not replace x by x-2 in the Maclaurin series without the factor e². Use e^x=e²e^(x-2).

Recognizing a Function from Its Series

由级数识别函数

The even positive terms

1+x²/2!+x⁴/4!+x⁶/6!+⋯

equal (ex+e−x)/2, the even part of ex.

Core lesson

Where the Series Equals the Function

级数何时等于函数

The standard exponential and trigonometric series converge to their functions for every real x.

A geometric transformation represents its function only where the resulting common ratio has magnitude below 1.

Derivatives of all orders alone do not guarantee that a Taylor series equals the function away from the center.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1012

Keep this caveat concise. AP problems normally provide or use standard analytic functions, but the logical distinction matters.

Common Errors

常见错误

  • Changing the input but not every exponent
  • Dropping alternating signs
  • Forgetting an outside constant factor
  • Giving terms without a general term or convergence interval when requested

Quick Check

课堂检测

Find the coefficient of x⁶ in the Maclaurin series for e2x²/2.

Answer: The n=3 term is (1/2)(2x²)³/3!=(1/2)(8x⁶/6)=(2/3)x⁶. The coefficient is 2/3.
