Fast check: Checks rate-to-amount units, average value, and accumulation extrema before the 2022 problem.

1. A crowd enters at R(t)=80+10t people/hour. Write and evaluate the number entering from t=1 to t=3. State units.3 points
Integrate a rate over the time interval. / 对速率在时间区间上积分。
Answer: ∫₁³(80+10t)dt=[80t+5t²]₁³=200 people.
2. Find the average value of f(t)=t² on [0,3].3 points
Divide the definite integral by interval length. / 定积分除以区间长度。
Answer: (1/3)∫₀³t²dt=(1/3)(9)=3.
3. C(t)=∫₁ᵗ[R(x)−50]dx, with R continuous. State where C increases and describe a complete method for finding its absolute maximum on [1,6].4 points
Differentiate C using FTC, then use a candidates test. / 用微积分基本定理求 C′，再做候选点检验。
Answer:

C′(t)=R(t)−50, so C increases where R(t)>50. Solve R(t)=50 for all interior critical times and compare C at those times and t=1,6 (or give a complete sign analysis).
