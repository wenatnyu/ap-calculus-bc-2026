Fast check: The height of f′ determines whether f increases or decreases.

1. A sign chart shows f′>0 on (−∞,−2), f′<0 on (−2,3), and f′>0 on (3,∞). State where f increases and decreases.3 points
Intervals / 区间：
Answer: Increasing on (−∞,−2)∪(3,∞); decreasing on (−2,3).
2. Find the intervals on which g(x)=x³−3x is increasing and decreasing.3 points
Derivative sign chart / 导数符号表：
Answer: g′=3(x−1)(x+1). Thus g increases on (−∞,−1)∪(1,∞) and decreases on (−1,1).
3. A population changes at rate R(t)=2t cos(t²). Is the population increasing or decreasing at t=3? Use radians.4 points
Sign and conclusion / 符号与结论：
Answer: R(3)=6cos(9)≈−5.467<0. The population is decreasing because its rate of change is negative.
