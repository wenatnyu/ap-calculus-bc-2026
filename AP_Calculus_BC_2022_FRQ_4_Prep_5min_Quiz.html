1. Values of v are v(1)=−3.6 and v(5)=−2.4, where v is in meters per second. Approximate v′(3) using the average rate of change on [1,5].3 points
Difference quotient + units / 后值减前值；v′ 的单位是什么？
Answer:

v′(3)≈[−2.4−(−3.6)]/(5−1)=1.2/4=0.3 m/s².

2. A function g is differentiable on [2,7], with g(2)=−1 and g(7)=4. Explain why g(c)=0 for some c in (2,7).3 points
IVT statement / 可导推出连续；0 被两端函数值夹住。
Answer:

Because g is differentiable, it is continuous on [2,7]. Since −1=g(2)<0<g(7)=4, the IVT guarantees c∈(2,7) with g(c)=0.

3. A cone has V=(1/3)πr²h. At an instant, r=4, h=9, dr/dt=2, and dh/dt=−1. Find dV/dt.4 points
Related-rate derivative / r 和 h 都是 t 的函数。
Answer:

dV/dt=(π/3)[2rh r′+r²h′]=(π/3)[2(4)(9)(2)+16(−1)]=128π/3.
