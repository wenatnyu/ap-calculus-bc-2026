Geometric data / 等比级数要素
Answer: a=6 and r=1/4, so the series converges to 6/(1-1/4)=8.
2. Find Σn=1∞5(-1/2)n-1.3 points
Negative ratio / 负公比
Answer: a=5 and r=-1/2, so the sum is 5/[1-(-1/2)]=10/3.
3. Write 0.272727... as a fraction using a geometric series.4 points
Repeating decimal / 循环小数
Answer: 0.27+0.0027+... has a=27/100 and r=1/100, so its sum is (27/100)/(99/100)=3/11.
