Area, Cross Sections & Improper Volume

面积、截面体积与反常积分

A no-calculator BC question built around three accumulation models:

  1. Area under a curve
  2. Volume from known cross-sectional area
  3. Improper volume of revolution

先判断“累积的是什么”，再写积分。

Part (a) · Area Setup

面积积分

Area under y=1/x on [1,5]:

∫₁⁵ 1/x dx

函数为正，所以不需要绝对值或分段。

Part (a) · Evaluate

计算面积

∫₁⁵1/x dx=[ln x]₁⁵

=ln 5−ln 1

=ln 5.

Part (b) · Use the Given Area

截面积已经给出

The problem gives the rectangular cross-sectional area:

A(x)=xex/5.

Therefore V=∫₁⁵A(x)dx=∫₁⁵xex/5dx.

不要再乘 1/x；体积就是截面积的累积。

2022 BC Free-Response Practice06

This is the main reading trap. The base region explains where the solid exists, but A(x) is already the full area of each slice.

Part (b) · Integration by Parts

分部积分

Choose u=x and dv=ex/5dx.

Then du=dx and v=5ex/5.

∫xex/5dx=5xex/5−∫5ex/5dx.

对 ex/5 积分会乘 5。

2022 BC Free-Response Practice07

Have students verify v by differentiating 5e^(x/5). This catches the most common chain-rule factor error.

Part (b) · Finish the Definite Integral

代上下限

Antiderivative:

5xex/5−25ex/5=5ex/5(x−5).

V=[5ex/5(x−5)]₁⁵

=0−(−20e1/5)=20e1/5.

Part (c) · From Radius to Disk Area

半径平方才是圆盘面积

When W revolves about the x-axis:

radius=1/x²

disk area=π(radius)²=π/x⁴.

旋转体的积分函数不是 1/x²，而是 π(1/x²)²。

2022 BC Free-Response Practice09

Ask students to point to the radius on the sketch. This prevents the common area-instead-of-volume setup.

Part (c) · Proper Limit Notation

反常积分必须写极限

V=π∫₃∞x−4dx

=limb→∞π∫₃ᵇx−4dx

=limb→∞π[−1/(3x³)]₃ᵇ

=π/81.

2022 BC Free-Response Practice10

Keep b finite until the final limit. Infinity is not a number and must not be substituted into an algebraic fraction.

Common Errors to Eliminate

常见失分点

  • Giving ln 5 with no definite integral.
  • Multiplying the provided A(x) by 1/x.
  • Forgetting the factor 5 when integrating ex/5.
  • Using π/x² instead of π/x⁴ for disks.
  • Skipping the b→∞ limit.
  • Writing arithmetic with ∞ as if it were a number.

Quick check · Reveal

Quick Check

快速检查

A solid has cross-sectional area A(x)=x²+1 for 0≤x≤2. What integral gives its volume, and what is the volume?

截面积已经给出，直接累积。

V=∫₀²(x²+1)dx=[x³/3+x]₀²=14/3.

Do not multiply by another base-region function.

离堂题

Find the volume generated by revolving y=1/x³, x≥2, about the x-axis.

写圆盘面积、极限和最终值。

V=limb→∞π∫₂ᵇx−6dx

=limb→∞π[−1/(5x⁵)]₂ᵇ=π/160.
