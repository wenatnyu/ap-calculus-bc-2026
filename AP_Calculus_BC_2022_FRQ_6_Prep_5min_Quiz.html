Fast check: Checks Ratio Test mechanics and endpoint habits, alternating error bounds, and termwise differentiation followed by a geometric sum.

1. Use the Ratio Test to find the interior interval of convergence of Σn=1∞xn/n.3 points
Ratio Test interior / 先只求内部；端点本题暂不要求。
Answer:

lim |[xn+1/(n+1)]/[xn/n]|=|x|. Thus |x|<1, so the interior is (−1,1).

2. An alternating series has decreasing term magnitudes 1/2, 1/8, 1/32, …. If only the first term is used, give an error bound.3 points
Alternating error / 误差小于第一遗漏项。
Answer:

By the alternating-series error bound, |error|<1/8.

3. If F(x)=x−x³/3+x⁵/5−…, write F′(x) and find F′(1/2).4 points
Differentiate and sum / 逐项求导后是等比级数。
Answer:

F′(x)=1−x²+x⁴−… . At x=1/2, a=1 and r=−1/4, so F′(1/2)=1/(1+1/4)=4/5.
