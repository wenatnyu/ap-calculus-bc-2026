General Solutions Using Separation of Variables

用变量分离法求通解

dy/dx=g(x)h(y)
→
dy/h(y)=g(x)dx

Separate the variables, integrate both sides, and preserve every valid solution.把 y 项和 dy 放一边，x 项和 dx 放另一边。

Core lesson

When Is an Equation Separable?

什么方程可以分离

dy/dx=g(x)h(y)

If the right side factors into an x-only part times a y-only part, move h(y) beside dy.

1/h(y) dy=g(x) dx

An expression such as x+y does not factor this way, so the method does not apply directly.先判断可分离，再动手。

The Four-Step Method

变量分离四步法

1

Factor the right side.

2

Separate x and y.

3

Integrate both sides.

4

Simplify, check equilibria, and verify.

只需一个积分常数，因为两边常数可合并。

Power Example

幂函数例题

dy/dx=3x²/y

y dy=3x² dx

½y²=x³+C   ⇒   y²=2x³+C

An implicit general solution is often a complete answer.通解不一定要解出 y。

Logarithm Becomes an Exponential Family

对数式化成指数解族

y′=8x²y

dy/y=8x²dx

ln|y|=(8/3)x³+C

y=Ce(8/3)x³

指数化后，非零乘法常数可吸收符号；还要单独检查 y=0。

A Reciprocal-Power Example

负幂积分例题

y′=eˣy²

y−2dy=eˣdx

−1/y=eˣ+C

y=−1/(eˣ+C)

Also check the equilibrium y=0, lost when dividing by y².除以含 y 的因子前先记录零解。

Core lesson

Shift Before Separating

先识别平移因子

y′=−2x(y−3)

dy/(y−3)=−2x dx

ln|y−3|=−x²+C

y=3+Ce−x²

y=3 is included when C=0 in the final family.平衡值是 3，不是 0。

Core lesson

Implicit or Explicit?

隐式解与显式解

Implicit

y²=2x³+C

Keeps both branches together.

Explicit

y=±√(2x³+C)

Requires a branch and domain.

Do not choose + or − without an initial condition.通解阶段不要擅自选分支。

Core lesson

Use One Constant, Not Two

积分常数只保留一个

∫A(y)dy=∫B(x)dx

Writing F(y)+C₁=G(x)+C₂ is correct, but subtracting gives one arbitrary constant C=C₂−C₁.

After exponentiating, eC becomes a nonzero multiplicative constant; its sign is absorbed separately.常数可改名，但不能把加法常数留在指数外。

Core lesson

Division Can Lose Equilibria

除法可能丢失平衡解

y′=(y−2)x

Dividing by y−2 assumes y≠2. Record y=2 first and test it directly.

Never claim “no solution” merely because a separation step divided by zero.被除掉的零因子可能对应常数解。

Core lesson

State a Valid Solution Interval

说明有效解区间

y=−1/(eˣ+C)

The denominator must not be zero. If C<0, a vertical singularity occurs where eˣ=−C.

A solution interval cannot cross a point where the formula or differential equation is undefined.解区间不能跨过奇点。

Quick check · Reveal

Find the General Family

求通解

Solve y′=y cos x.

Answer: dy/y=cos x dx, so ln|y|=sin x+C and y=Cesin x. The value C=0 includes the equilibrium.先保留 ln|y|。

Include the Equilibrium

别漏掉常数解

Solve y′=(y+5)(x+2).

Answer: Non-equilibrium solutions satisfy y+5=Cex²/2+2x, so y=−5+Cex²/2+2x. Taking C=0 includes y=−5.验证常数解。
