AP Calculus BC · Topic 8.6

Finding the Area Between Curves That Intersect at More Than Two Points

多交点曲线间面积

Area = Σ ∫(upper − lower) dx = ∫|f(x)−g(x)|dx

Every crossover can change which curve is on top, so total area is a sum of nonnegative pieces. / 每个交点都可能改变上下关系，总面积要分段相加。

Find Every Intersection

找出所有交点

Solve f(x)=g(x) across the full requested interval.

Missing one interior intersection can make positive and negative pieces cancel.

Core lesson

Build a Sign Chart

建立差值符号表

D(x)=f(x)−g(x)

  • D>0: f is above g.
  • D<0: g is above f.

Split at Crossovers

Absolute-Difference Form

绝对值形式

A=∫ab|f(x)−g(x)|dx

For hand evaluation, splitting at zeros of f−g is usually clearer.

Sine Example

正弦函数示例

Area between y=sin x and y=0 on [0,2π]

=∫₀^πsin x dx+∫π2π(−sin x)dx=4

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 807

Polynomial Example

多项式示例

Graphing Calculator Workflow

图形计算器流程

  • Graph both curves or f−g.
  • Find and store every intersection.
  • Determine order on each interval.
  • Integrate each positive difference.
  • Add and round once.

Total Area Is Not Net Area

总面积不等于净面积

  • Signed integral allows cancellation.
  • Geometric area adds magnitudes.
  • Area must be nonnegative.

Check with Symmetry

利用对称性核对

Even or symmetric regions can shorten work, but symmetry must be applied to nonnegative area pieces.

A=2∫₀ᵇ|f(x)−g(x)|dx when appropriate

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 812

Multiple-Intersection Checklist
