5-Minute Review: Types of Discontinuities

五分钟复习：间断点的类型

5MINUTES · 10 POINTS

Directions: Name each discontinuity and give its location. Show factoring or one-sided limits when needed.
要求：写出间断类型和位置；必要时展示因式分解或左右极限。

Fast check: a canceled factor gives a hole; an uncanceled denominator zero gives a vertical asymptote; unequal finite one-sided limits give a jump.

1. Match each description to hole, vertical asymptote, or jump: (a) unequal finite one-sided limits; (b) finite limit but a missing/wrong point value; (c) unbounded behavior near x=c.3 points
a. ________    b. ________    c. ________
Answer: (a) jump; (b) hole (removable); (c) vertical asymptote. 左右有限极限不同是跳跃；可补点的是空洞；趋向无穷对应竖直渐近线。
2. Identify every discontinuity of f(x)=(x−2)(x+1)(x−2)(x−4).4 points
Type and location / 类型与位置：
Answer: a hole at x=2 and a vertical asymptote at x=4. The factor x−2 cancels only for nearby x; x−4 remains in the denominator. 约掉的分母因子产生空洞；未约掉的分母零点产生竖直渐近线。
3. Let g(x)=2x+1 for x<1 and g(x)=x²+4 for x≥1. Classify the discontinuity at x=1.3 points
Left limit: ______   Right limit: ______   Type: ______
Answer: jump at x=1. The left-hand limit is 3; the right-hand limit and g(1) are 5. Since 3≠5, the two-sided limit does not exist. 左右极限分别为 3 和 5，因此是跳跃间断。

Scoring / 评分

  • Q1: 1 point for each correct match.
  • Q2: 1 for factoring/cancellation; 1 each for hole and VA; 1 for locations.
  • Q3: 1 for each one-sided limit; 1 for jump at 1.
  • Use “removable discontinuity” as equivalent to “hole.”
