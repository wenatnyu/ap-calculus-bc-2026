1. The rate of change of Y with respect to t is proportional to t². Write the model.3 points
Differential equation / 微分方程：
Answer: dY/dt=kt².
2. The rate of change of S with respect to y is proportional to √y and inversely proportional to a fixed positive parameter v.3 points
Differential equation / 微分方程：
Answer: dS/dy=k√y/v.
3. Suppose h″(t)=k∛t and h″(8)=6. Find k and the calibrated equation.4 points
Substitution and model / 代入与模型：
Answer: 6=k∛8=2k, so k=3 and h″(t)=3∛t.
