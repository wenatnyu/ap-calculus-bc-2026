Part (a) · Set Up the Ratio

比值判别法起步

For an=(−1)nx2n+1/(2n+1):

|an+1/an|=|x²(2n+1)/(2n+3)|.

2(n+1)+1=2n+3。

Part (a) · Find the Interior

先求内部区间

limn→∞|an+1/an|=x².

Convergence requires x²<1, so

−1<x<1.

这里还没有完成：端点要单独检查。

Official solution walkthrough

Part (a) · Test Both Endpoints

两个端点分别代入

x=1: 1−1/3+1/5−… converges by AST.

x=−1: −1+1/3−1/5+… converges by AST.

In each case, magnitudes decrease to 0.

Interval: [−1,1].

Part (b) · First Omitted Term

第一遗漏项控制误差

At x=1/2:

f(1/2)=1/2−(1/2)³/3+(1/2)⁵/5−…

Using 1/2 as the approximation, the first omitted term has magnitude 1/24.

Official solution walkthrough

Part (b) · Write the Full Justification

误差界完整表达

The series alternates, and its term magnitudes decrease to 0. Therefore,

|f(1/2)−1/2|<1/24<1/10.

1/24 是误差上界，不是实际误差。

Part (c) · Differentiate Term by Term

逐项求导

d/dx[(−1)nx2n+1/(2n+1)]

=(−1)nx2n.

The factor 2n+1 cancels exactly.

Part (d) · See the Geometric Series

识别等比级数

At x=1/6:

1−1/36+1/36²−1/36³+…

First term a=1; ratio r=−1/36.

Sum=a/(1−r)=36/37.

Common Errors to Eliminate

常见失分点

  • Simplifying 2(n+1)+1 as 2n+2.
  • Stopping at (−1,1) without testing endpoints.
  • Using the Ratio Test to decide a limit-1 endpoint.
  • Writing Error=1/24 instead of Error<1/24.
  • Losing the n=0 term when differentiating.
  • Using ratio +1/36 in part (d).
2022 BC Free-Response Practice13

Quick check · Reveal

Quick Check

快速检查

For Σ from n=0 to ∞ of (−1)^n x^n/(n+1), the Ratio Test gives |x|<1. Test x=1 and x=−1.

端点必须代入原级数，结果可能不同。

x=1 gives 1−1/2+1/3−…: convergent by AST.

x=−1 gives 1+1/2+1/3+…: divergent.

Interval: (−1,1].
