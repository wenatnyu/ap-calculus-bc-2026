Choose R and r by Distance

按距离确定外内半径

  • R: boundary farther from the axis.
  • r: boundary nearer the axis.
  • Both radii are nonnegative distances.

Washer Errors

垫圈法常见错误

  • Using (R−r)².
  • Reversing inner and outer radii.
  • Using slices parallel to the axis.
  • Mixing x and y in one setup.

Washer Checklist

垫圈法检查表

  • Sketch region and axis.
  • Choose perpendicular slices.
  • Measure R and r from the axis.
  • Write π(R²−r²).
  • Use matching bounds and variable.
  • Report cubic units.

Quick Check

Answer: R=x+1 and r=1, so V=π∫₀²[(x+1)²−1]dx=20π/3.
