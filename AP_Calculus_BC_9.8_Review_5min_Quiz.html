1. Convert (r,θ)=(2,π/3) to Cartesian coordinates.3 points
Coordinate conversion / 坐标转换
Answer: (x,y)=(1,√3).
2. For r=θ, find dy/dx at θ=π/2.3 points
Polar slope / 极坐标斜率
Answer: With r′=1, dy/dθ=1 and dx/dθ=−π/2, so dy/dx=−2/π.
3. For r=2cosθ, classify the tangent at θ=0 and give its equation. Verify both θ-derivatives.4 points
Tangent classification / 切线判定
Answer: At θ=0, the point is (2,0), dx/dθ=0, and dy/dθ=2≠0. The tangent is vertical: x=2.

  • Q3: point, both derivative conditions, and vertical-line equation.
  • A zero denominator without the nonzero numerator check is incomplete.
