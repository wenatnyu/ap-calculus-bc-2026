AP Calculus BC · Topic 8.9

Volume with Disc Method: Revolving Around the x- or y-Axis

绕坐标轴旋转的圆盘法

V = π∫ R²

A slice perpendicular to the axis of rotation becomes a circular disc whose radius is a distance to the axis. / 垂直于旋转轴的切片生成圆盘，半径是到旋转轴的距离。

From Region to Solid

由平面区域到旋转体

Rotating a region around a boundary axis creates circular cross sections with no hole.

A=πR²

Around the x-Axis

Core lesson

Set Bounds from the Region

由区域确定上下限

Bounds describe the interval swept by the perpendicular slices.

  • dx: left to right x-values.
  • dy: bottom to top y-values.
FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 810

Do not use the radius values as integration bounds unless they coincide with the coordinate interval.

Core lesson

Why the Radius Is Squared

为什么半径要平方

disc area = πR²; volume ≈ ΣπR²Δx

The integral accumulates circular area, not circumference.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 811

This prevents the common error 2πR.

Disc Checklist

圆盘法检查表

  • Sketch region and axis.
  • Use slices perpendicular to the axis.
  • Confirm the region touches the axis.
  • Write radius as a distance.
  • Integrate πR².
  • Report cubic units.
