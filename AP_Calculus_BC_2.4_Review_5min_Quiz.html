Directions: Compare one-sided limits or one-sided derivatives when needed. Justify each conclusion.
要求：需要时比较左右极限或左右导数，并写出理由。

Fast check: Differentiable implies continuous. Corners, cusps, vertical tangents, and discontinuities prevent a finite two-sided derivative.

1. Complete and evaluate the implication: “If f is differentiable at x=c, then ______ at x=c.” Is the converse always true?2 points
Statement + converse / 命题与逆命题：
Answer: f is continuous at c. The converse is not always true; a continuous graph can have a corner or cusp.
2. For h(x)=|x−3|, determine whether h is continuous and whether it is differentiable at x=3. Justify.4 points
Continuity + one-sided slopes / 连续性与左右斜率：
Answer: It is continuous at 3 because limx→3|x−3|=0=h(3). It is not differentiable there: the left-hand slope is −1 and the right-hand slope is 1, so a corner occurs.
3. Let f(x)=x+1 for x<1 and f(x)=ax+b for x≥1. Find a and b so that f is differentiable at x=1.4 points
Match values and slopes / 先连续，再匹配斜率：
Answer: Continuity gives 2=a+b. Matching derivatives gives 1=a. Therefore a=1 and b=1.

Scoring

  • Q1: 1 point for continuous; 1 for rejecting the converse.
  • Q2: 2 points for continuity; 2 for unequal one-sided slopes.
  • Q3: 2 for value equation, 1 for slope equation, 1 for values.
  • Require justification, not only yes/no.
