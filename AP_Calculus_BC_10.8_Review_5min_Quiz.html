1. State the two conditions on bn in the alternating series test.3 points
Conditions / 条件
Answer: bn must eventually decrease and satisfy lim bn=0, with bn≥0.
2. Test Σn=1∞(-1)n-1/√n for convergence.3 points
AST / 交错级数判别
Answer: bn=1/√n is positive, decreasing, and tends to zero, so the series converges by AST.
3. Classify Σ(-1)nn/(n+1), and explain why AST cannot rescue it.4 points
Term test / 通项判别
Answer: The magnitudes approach 1, so the terms do not approach zero. The series diverges by the nth-term test.
