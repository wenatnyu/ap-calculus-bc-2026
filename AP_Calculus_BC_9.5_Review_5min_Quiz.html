1. Differentiate r(t)=⟨t³, e2t⟩.3 points
Componentwise derivative
Answer: r′(t)=⟨3t²,2e2t⟩.
2. For r(t)=⟨cos t,sin t⟩, find the velocity vector at t=π/2.3 points
Differentiate and evaluate
Answer: v(t)=⟨−sin t,cos t⟩, so v(π/2)=⟨−1,0⟩.
3. For r(t)=⟨t²,t³−3t⟩, find the acceleration vector and the slope of the path at t=1.4 points
Vector and scalar
Answer: v(t)=⟨2t,3t²−3⟩, a(t)=⟨2,6t⟩, so a(1)=⟨2,6⟩. The path slope is vy/vx=0/2=0.
