Rate Becomes Change

从变化率得到变化量

accumulated change ≈ rate × elapsed time

units: (output/time)(time) = output

The horizontal width supplies the time factor. 横向宽度提供时间因子。

Constant-Rate Model

恒定变化率模型

v(t)=60 miles/hour for 4 hours

change = 60·4 = 240 miles

The rectangle has height 60 and width 4. 矩形面积就是路程增量。

Topic 6.1 · Core lesson

Piecewise-Constant Rate

分段恒定变化率

r(t)=5 for 0≤t≤3

r(t)=−2 for 3<t≤7

net change = 5(3)−2(4)=7

Positive and negative pieces must be combined with signs. 横轴下方的面积记为负。

Initial Value + Accumulation

初始值加累积变化

quantity at time b

= initial quantity + change from a to b

Q(b)=Q(a)+signed accumulation

Accumulation is a change, not automatically the final amount. 先区分“变化量”和“总量”。

Net Change vs. Total Change

净变化与总变化

Net change

Add signed pieces.

(+A)+(−B)=A−B

Total change

Add magnitudes.

|A|+|−B|=A+B

Motion Connection

运动中的累积

signed area under v(t) = displacement

sum of absolute areas = distance traveled

A return to the starting point can have zero displacement but positive distance.

AP-style quick check

Quick Check

课堂即时检测

A tank fills at 8 liters per minute for 5 minutes. What is the accumulated change?

  1. A13 L
  2. B40 L
  3. C40 L/min
  4. D1.6 L
Answer: B. The rectangle has area 8·5=40 liters.变化率乘时间，单位化为升。

Exit ticket · One clean solution

Exit Ticket

离堂检测

A velocity is 3 m/s for 4 s, then −2 m/s for 3 s. Find displacement and distance traveled.

Answer: Displacement: 3(4)−2(3)=6 m. Distance: 3(4)+2(3)=18 m.净位移保留符号；总路程取各段面积的绝对值。
