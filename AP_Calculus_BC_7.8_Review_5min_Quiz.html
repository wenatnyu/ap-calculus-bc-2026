Fast check: The final function must satisfy the DE, the initial point, and a valid interval.

1. Solve y′=2xy with y(0)=3.3 points
General family and condition / 通解与初值：
Answer: The family is y=Cex². Since y(0)=C=3, y=3ex².
2. Solve y′=6x²/y with y(0)=−2.3 points
Implicit form and branch / 隐式式与分支：
Answer: y²=4x³+4; the initial value selects y=−2√(x³+1) on the interval x>−1.
3. Solve y′=6−2y with y(0)=4.4 points
Separation and solution / 分离与特解：
Answer: y=3+Ce−2x; the condition gives C=1. Thus y=3+e−2x.
