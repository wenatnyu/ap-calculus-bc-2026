Δs≈√((Δx)²+(Δy)²)

Core lesson

Arc-Length Formula

弧长公式

L=∫ab√(1+[f′(x)]²)dx

A standard sufficient condition is that f′ is continuous on [a,b].

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 804

State the smoothness condition before applying the formula; piecewise-smooth curves can be handled by splitting.

Core lesson

Why 1+[f′]² Appears

公式中的 1+[f′]²

ds=√(dx²+dy²)=√(1+(dy/dx)²)dx

The square root is essential.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 805

Do not confuse arc length with area or integrate 1+[f′]² without the square root.

Differentiate First

先求导

  • Find f′(x).
  • Square the derivative.
  • Add 1.
  • Take the square root.
  • Integrate from a to b.

Core lesson

Distance Traveled Along a Graph

沿曲线行进的路程

If a particle follows the graph while x moves monotonically from a to b, distance traveled equals the arc length.

Retraced pieces must be counted each time they are traveled.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 810

Quick Check

Find the arc length of y=(2/3)x3/2 from x=0 to x=3.
