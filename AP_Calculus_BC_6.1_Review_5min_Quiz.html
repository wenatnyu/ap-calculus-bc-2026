1. For x²+4y²=20, find dy/dx.3 points
Derivative / 导数：
Answer: 2x+8yy′=0, so y′=−x/(4y).
2. For x²+xy+y²=7, find the slope at (1,2).3 points
Work / 过程：
Answer: 2x+y+xy′+2yy′=0, so y′=−(2x+y)/(x+2y)=−4/5.
3. Find all horizontal- and vertical-tangent points on x²+4y²=20.4 points
Points and conditions / 坐标与条件：
Answer: Since y′=−x/(4y), horizontal tangents occur at x=0: (0,±√5). Vertical tangents occur at y=0: (±2√5,0).
