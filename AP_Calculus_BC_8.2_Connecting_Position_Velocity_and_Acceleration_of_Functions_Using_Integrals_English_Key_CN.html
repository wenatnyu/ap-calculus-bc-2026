AP Calculus BC · Topic 8.2

Connecting Position, Velocity, and Acceleration of Functions Using Integrals

用积分联系位置、速度与加速度

s(b)=s(a)+∫abv(t)dt; v(b)=v(a)+∫aba(t)dt

Integrating a rate recovers net change; initial values recover the quantity itself. / 对变化率积分得到净变化，再结合初值恢复原量。

Displacement

Total Distance

总路程

total distance = ∫ab|v(t)|dt

Split at every time where velocity changes sign.

Locate Direction Changes

确定变向时刻

  • Solve v(t)=0 inside the interval.
  • Make a sign chart for v.
  • Split only where the sign actually changes.

Core lesson

Direction of Motion

运动方向

  • v(t)>0: position is increasing.
  • v(t)<0: position is decreasing.
  • v(t)=0: instantaneously at rest.
FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 809

Core lesson

Speeding Up or Slowing Down

加速还是减速

  • Same signs for v and a: speed increases.
  • Opposite signs for v and a: speed decreases.
  • Speed is |v|.

Quick Check

课堂即时检测

A particle has v(t)=t−1 for 0≤t≤3. Find its displacement and total distance.

Answer: Displacement = ∫₀³(t−1)dt=3/2. Total distance = ∫₀¹(1−t)dt+∫₁³(t−1)dt=5/2.

Exit Ticket

Answer: v(2)=2+∫₀²6t dt=2+12=14.
