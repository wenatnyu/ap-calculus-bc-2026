Long-Division Example

长除法例题

(x²+1)/(x−1)=x+1+2/(x−1)

∫(x²+1)/(x−1)dx

=x²/2+x+2ln|x−1|+C

Multiply back to verify the division.

Inverse-Tangent Form

反正切结构

∫dx/[(x+3)²+2²]

=(1/2)tan⁻¹((x+3)/2)+C

Template: ∫du/(u²+a²)=(1/a)tan⁻¹(u/a)+C.

Inverse-Sine Form

反正弦结构

∫dx/√(9−(x−2)²)=sin⁻¹((x−2)/3)+C

Separate a Derivative Term

拆出分母导数

∫(2x+7)/(x²+6x+13)dx

2x+7=(2x+6)+1

=ln(x²+6x+13)+(1/2)tan⁻¹((x+3)/2)+C

One integral can use two different patterns.

AP-style quick check

Quick Check

课堂即时检测

Evaluate ∫dx/(x²+6x+13).

  1. Aln|x²+6x+13|+C
  2. B(1/2)tan⁻¹((x+3)/2)+C
  3. Ctan⁻¹(x+3)+C
  4. Dsin⁻¹((x+3)/2)+C
Answer: B. Complete the square: x²+6x+13=(x+3)²+4.配方后使用反正切积分公式。

Exit Ticket

离堂检测

Integrate (x²+1)/(x−1).

Answer: Long division gives x+1+2/(x−1), so the integral is x²/2+x+2ln|x−1|+C.先长除，再逐项积分。
