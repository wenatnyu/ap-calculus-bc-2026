五分钟复习：相关变化率方程

Directions: Differentiate each relation with respect to time. Include a rate factor for every changing variable.
要求：两边对时间求导；每个变化变量都要带上自己的变化率。

Fast check: Treat x, y, r, and other changing quantities as functions of time.

1. A cube has V=s³. Write a relation between dV/dt and ds/dt.3 points
Differentiate with respect to t:
Answer: dV/dt=3s²(ds/dt).
2. A rectangle has A=LW, and both dimensions change. Find dA/dt.3 points
Product rule in time:
Answer: dA/dt=L(dW/dt)+W(dL/dt).
3. A 10-foot ladder satisfies x²+y²=100. At one instant x=6, y=8, and dx/dt=2 ft/s. Find dy/dt.4 points
Rate equation, substitution, answer:
Answer: 2x(dx/dt)+2y(dy/dt)=0, so 6(2)+8(dy/dt)=0 and dy/dt=−3/2 ft/s.

  • Q3: 1 for relation; 1 for derivative; 1 for substitution; 1 for answer/units.
