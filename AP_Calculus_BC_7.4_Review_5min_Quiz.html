Directions: Use the differential equation to determine local segment directions.
要求：代入坐标求局部斜率，并说明线段方向。

Fast check: Segment direction comes from the slope value; segment length should stay uniform.

1. For y′=x+y, find the slope at (1,−2) and describe the segment.3 points
Slope and direction / 斜率与方向：
Answer: m=1+(−2)=−1; draw a short falling segment of slope −1.
2. For y′=2y, explain the repeated pattern and locate horizontal segments.3 points
Pattern / 图案：
Answer: Slopes repeat across each horizontal row because the derivative depends only on y. Segments are horizontal on y=0.
3. For y′=x+y, find the slopes at (−1,1), (0,1), and (1,1).4 points
Three slopes / 三个斜率：
Answer: The slopes are 0, 1, 2, respectively: horizontal, rising at 45°, then more steeply rising.
