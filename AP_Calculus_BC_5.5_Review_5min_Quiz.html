Directions: Classify each critical input from the sign change of the derivative.
要求：根据临界点两侧导数的符号变化分类。

Fast check: +→− gives a local maximum; −→+ gives a local minimum; no sign change gives neither.

1. The sign of f′ is + on (−∞,−2), − on (−2,0) and (0,3), and + on (3,∞). Classify x=−2,0,3.3 points
Three classifications / 三个分类：
Answer: Local maximum at x=−2; neither at x=0; local minimum at x=3.
2. If g′(x)=(x−3)(x+1), find and classify every relative extremum by its x-value.3 points
Sign pattern / 符号变化：
Answer: The sign pattern is +,−,+. Thus g has a local maximum at x=−1 and a local minimum at x=3.
3. Suppose f is continuous at a critical number c, and f′ is positive on both sides of c. Does f have a relative extremum there? Explain.4 points
Decision and reason / 判断与理由：
Answer: No. The function is increasing on both sides, so f′ has no sign change at c.

Scoring / 评分

  • Q1: 1 point per correct classification.
  • Q2: 1 sign pattern, 2 classifications.
  • Q3: 2 decision, 2 explanation.
  • Do not award an extremum from f′(c)=0 alone.
