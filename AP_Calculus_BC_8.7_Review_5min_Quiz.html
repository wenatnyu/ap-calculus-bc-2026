1. Where must the area between y=sin x and y=0 on [0,2π] be split?3 points
Crossover / 交叉点
Answer: At x=π, the interior sign-change point.
2. Find the total area between y=sin x and the x-axis on [0,2π].3 points
Positive pieces / 非负分段
Answer: ∫₀^πsin x dx+∫π2π(−sin x)dx=4.
3. Find the total area between y=x³−x and the x-axis on [−1,1].4 points
Roots and symmetry / 零点与对称性
Answer: The roots are −1,0,1; A=2∫₀¹(x−x³)dx=1/2.
