Fast check: L(x)=f(a)+f′(a)(x−a); concave up gives an underestimate and concave down an overestimate.

1. Write the linearization of f at x=a.3 points
Formula:
Answer: L(x)=f(a)+f′(a)(x−a).
2. Use linearization at a=4 to approximate √4.1.3 points
f(4), f′(4), estimate:
Answer: √4.1≈2+(1/4)(0.1)=2.025.
3. f is concave down, f(2)=5, and f′(2)=−3. Estimate f(1.8) and classify the estimate.4 points
Linearization and error direction:
Answer: f(1.8)≈5+(−3)(−0.2)=5.6. Because f is concave down, the tangent-line estimate is an overestimate. 近似值5.6，为高估。
