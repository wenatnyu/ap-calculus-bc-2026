Fast check: Differentiate repeatedly, and distinguish position, velocity, and acceleration.

1. For f(x)=x³−2x, find f′(x) and f″(x).3 points
First and second derivatives:
Answer: f′(x)=3x²−2 and f″(x)=6x. 逐次求导。
2. If y=e2x, find y″.3 points
Differentiate twice:
Answer: y′=2e2x, so y″=4e2x.
3. A particle has position s(t)=t⁴−2t² meters, where t is measured in seconds. Find its velocity and acceleration at t=1.4 points
v(1) and a(1), with units:
Answer: v(t)=4t³−4t, so v(1)=0 m/s. a(t)=12t²−4, so a(1)=8 m/s².
