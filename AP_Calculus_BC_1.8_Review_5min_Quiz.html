5-Minute Review: Selecting Limit Procedures

五分钟复习：选择合适的极限计算方法

5MINUTES - 10 POINTS

Directions: Name the best procedure before calculating. Show the key algebraic step. Write DNE when the two-sided limit does not exist.
要求：先写出最合适的方法，再计算并写关键步骤；若双侧极限不存在，写 DNE。

Timing: Q1 about 1 minute; Q2-Q4 about 1 minute each. Accept equivalent algebra, but require Q4 to compare the two sides.

1. Match each limit with its best first procedure: direct substitution, factoring, rationalizing, or combining fractions. 2 points
A. limx→2x²−4x−2 B. limx→0√(x+9)−3x C. limx→01/(x+1)−1x D. limx→1x²+42x+1
A: __________ B: __________ C: __________ D: __________
A factoring; B rationalizing; C combining fractions; D direct substitution. A-C give 0/0 and their structures select the rewrite; D gives the finite value 5/3. 先代入诊断，再根据表达式结构选方法。
2. Evaluate limx→3x²−9x²−5x+6. 3 points
Procedure and key step / 方法与关键步骤：
Answer: 6. Direct substitution gives 0/0. Factor: (x−3)(x+3)(x−3)(x−2)=x+3x−2 for x≠3, then substitute. 约去的是公因子，不是加减中的项。
3. Evaluate limx→0√(x+16)−4x. 3 points
Procedure and key step / 方法与关键步骤：
Answer: 1/8. Multiply by the conjugate: √(x+16)−4x·√(x+16)+4√(x+16)+4=1√(x+16)+4. 乘以共轭式化简，再代入。
4. Decide whether limx→2x+1x−2 exists. Give the one-sided behavior. 2 points
Left: __________ Right: __________ Two-sided limit: __________
Answer: DNE. The numerator approaches 3. As x→2−, the quotient approaches −∞; as x→2+, it approaches +∞. 左右单侧趋势不同，所以双侧极限不存在。

Scoring / 评分

  • Q1: 0.5 point for each correct procedure.
  • Q2: 1 factoring, 1 cancellation, 1 final value.
  • Q3: 1 conjugate, 1 simplification, 1 final value.
  • Q4: 1 one-sided behavior, 1 DNE conclusion.
