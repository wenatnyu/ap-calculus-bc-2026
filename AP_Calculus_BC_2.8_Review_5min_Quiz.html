Directions: Differentiate. Keep exact values and use radians for trigonometric functions.
要求：求导并保留精确值；三角函数按弧度制。

Fast check: (sin x)′=cos x, (cos x)′=−sin x, (ax)′=axln a, and (ln x)′=1/x.

1. Differentiate f(x)=4sin x−3cos x+2ex.3 points
Work / 过程：
Answer: f′(x)=4cos x+3sin x+2ex.
2. If g(x)=5x+3ln x, find g′(1).3 points
Derivative, then substitute / 先求导，再代入：
Answer: g′(x)=5xln5+3/x, so g′(1)=5ln5+3.
3. Find the equation of the tangent line to y=2cos x+x at x=0.4 points
Point and slope / 点与斜率：
Answer: The point is (0,2). Since y′=−2sin x+1, the slope is 1. Thus y−2=x, or y=x+2.
