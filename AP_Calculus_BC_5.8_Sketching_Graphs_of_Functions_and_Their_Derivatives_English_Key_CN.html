Sketching Graphs of Functions and Their Derivatives

函数图像与导函数图像互译

f′ > 0 ⇒ f increasing
f′ increasing ⇒ f concave up
smooth extremum of f ⇒ f′=0

Read slope as height: the slope of f becomes the value of f′.把原函数的斜率读成导函数的纵坐标。

Core translation

Slope Becomes Height

把 f 的斜率翻译成 f′ 的高度

+

Positive slope

f′>0, so the curve rises locally.

0

Level

A smooth horizontal tangent gives f′=0.

−

Negative slope

f′<0, so the curve falls locally.

Magnitude matters: a steeper graph of f produces a larger |f′|.越陡，导数绝对值越大。

Key landmarks

Turning Points Become Zeros

转折点对应导函数的零点

On the graph of f

At a smooth local maximum or minimum, the tangent is horizontal.

slope = 0

On the graph of f′

The matching x-value is an x-intercept.

f′(c)=0

A zero of f′ is only a critical point. Check the sign change before naming an extremum.导数为零不自动保证极值。

Derivative may fail to exist

When the Derivative Is Undefined

尖点与竖直切线处导数可能不存在

Corner

Left and right slopes disagree.

f′(c) DNE

Cusp

Slopes diverge with opposite signs.

f′(c) DNE

Vertical tangent

The slope is infinite, not a finite derivative.

f′(c) DNE

Draw an open point or a break in the graph of f′ when the derivative does not exist.导数不存在处不要画实心点。

Reverse reading

Sketch f from f′

由导函数反推原函数

1

Mark zeros and undefined points of f′.

2

Use the sign of f′ for increase/decrease.

3

Use whether f′ rises or falls for concavity.

4

Choose any vertical shift; the shape is what matters.

Many functions can share the same derivative: they differ by a constant.同一导函数对应一族竖直平移的原函数。

Algebra confirms the sketch

One Derivative, Many Functions

f(x)=x33−4x+C

f′>0 on (−∞,−2)∪(2,∞).

f′<0 on (−2,2).

Changing C moves the graph vertically but does not change its slopes.

Error check

Four Common Errors

画导图常见错误

Incorrect moveCorrection
Copy the height of fUse the slope of f
Put every f-intercept on f′Only horizontal tangents create zeros of f′
Connect across a cornerLeave f′ undefined there
Assume f′ fixes the vertical position of fAny vertical shift is possible

Before finishing, test one point in every interval.每个区间选一点检查正负。

AP-style reasoning

Choose the Correct Description

根据导函数描述原函数

f′(x)=(x+2)(x−1)

Which statement must be true?

  1. Af has a local maximum at −2 and a local minimum at 1.
  2. Bf has local minima at both points.
  3. Cf is increasing on (−2,1).
  4. Df is concave down everywhere.
Answer: A. The sign of f′ is +, −, +, so f has a local maximum at −2 and a local minimum at 1.导数符号依次为正、负、正。

Exit ticket · Sketch logic

Describe the Shape Before You Draw

先描述，再画图

g′(x)=4−x2

State where g increases/decreases and identify its local extrema.

Answer: g increases on (−2,2) and decreases on (−∞,−2)∪(2,∞). It has a local minimum at x=−2 and a local maximum at x=2.注意 −2 处符号由负变正，是局部最小。
