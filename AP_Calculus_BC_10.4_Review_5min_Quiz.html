1. Use the nth-term test on Σ(2n+1)/(n+4).3 points
Term limit / 通项极限
Answer: The term limit is 2, not zero, so the series diverges.
2. Use the nth-term test on Σsin(nπ/2).3 points
No limit / 极限不存在
Answer: The terms cycle through 1,0,-1,0 and have no limit, so the series diverges.
3. What does the nth-term test conclude for Σ1/(n+3)? Explain the logical limitation.4 points
Inconclusive case / 无法判定情形
Answer: The term limit is zero, so the nth-term test is inconclusive. A zero term limit is necessary but not sufficient for series convergence.
