Core lesson

From the Differential Equation to the Family

由微分方程得到解族

dY/dt=kY

dY/Y=k dt

ln|Y|=kt+C

Y=Cekt

The general nonzero family comes from ln|Y|; Y=0 is also an equilibrium solution.积分时保留绝对值，并检查零解。

Core lesson

What the Constant C Means

常数 C 的含义

Y(t)=Cekt

If the initial time is t=0, then Y(0)=C.

If data are given at t=t₀, a safer form is

Y(t)=Y(t₀)ek(t−t₀)

C equals the initial amount only when the chosen time origin is zero.先确认初始时刻是不是 0。

Core lesson

Find k from Two Measurements

用两个数据点求 k

Y(t)=Y₀ekt,   Y(T)=Y₁

Y₁/Y₀=ekT

k=(1/T)ln(Y₁/Y₀)

Use a ratio before taking logarithms; the units of the quantity cancel.先除以初值，再取自然对数。

Core lesson

A Two-Point Growth Model

两点确定增长模型

An animal weighs 3 lb at month 0 and 4 lb at month 3.

4=3e3k ⇒ k=(1/3)ln(4/3)

W(t)=3(4/3)t/3

W(5)=3(4/3)5/3≈4.85 lb

同一模型可写成 e 底或增长因子形式。

Core lesson

Doubling Time

倍增时间

2Y₀=Y₀ekTd

Td=ln 2/k   (k>0)

If a population doubles every 7 years, then k=ln 2/7 year⁻¹.

Doubling time does not depend on the starting amount.固定相对增长率下，任意初值倍增所需时间相同。

Core lesson

Half-Life

半衰期

½Y₀=Y₀ekTh

Th=ln(1/2)/k=ln 2/|k|   (k<0)

A half-life is positive even though the decay constant is negative.半衰期为正；衰减参数 k 为负。

Core lesson

Relative Rate Has a Precise Meaning

相对变化率的准确含义

Y′/Y=k

At every instant, the amount changes by the same fraction of its current size per unit time.

k=0.04 day⁻¹: instantaneous relative growth rate 4% per day.

This does not mean the same number of units is added each day.

绝对增长量会随 Y 改变。

Continuous and Discrete Growth

连续与离散增长

ModelFormulaOne-unit factor
continuous relative rate kY=Y₀ekteᵏ
discrete percent r per periodY=Y₀(1+r)ᵗ1+r

A continuous rate k corresponds to an effective one-period rate eᵏ−1.连续参数 k 不等于离散增长因子。

Quick check · Reveal

Use a Repeated Growth Factor

利用重复增长因子

An exponentially growing population is 500 at day 0 and 800 at day 5. What is the population at day 10?

Answer: Each 5-day interval multiplies by 800/500=1.6. Thus P(10)=800(1.6)=1280.等长时间段具有相同倍数。

Exit ticket

Model a Drug Leaving the Bloodstream

药物衰减离堂题

A drug satisfies A′=−0.5A and A(0)=100 mg. Find A(t) and its half-life.

Answer: A(t)=100e−0.5t mg and Th=ln2/0.5=2ln2≈1.386 hours.单位与精确值都保留。
