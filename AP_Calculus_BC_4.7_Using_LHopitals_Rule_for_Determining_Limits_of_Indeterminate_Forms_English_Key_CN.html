The Correct Rule

洛必达法则的正确形式

If f,g are differentiable near a, g′≠0 there, substitution gives 0/0 or ∞/∞, and the derivative quotient has a limit, then

limx→af(x)g(x)=limx→af′(x)g′(x).

It is a limit of a derivative quotient—not automatically f′(a)/g′(a).

AP Calculus BC · 4.704

官方 notes 把右侧印成 f'(a)/g'(a)，这在导数于 a 不存在或仍需取极限时会误导；本页给出正确表述。

L’Hôpital Is Not the Quotient Rule

洛必达法则不是商法则

Limit

limx→af/g

Under the two indeterminate forms, compare f′/g′.

Derivative

ddx(f/g)=f′g−fg′g²

Use the quotient rule.

AP Calculus BC · 4.705

对应官方大写提示；一边是极限工具，一边是求导公式。

何时不能使用

nonzero/0

finite/finite

Direct substitution gives the quotient.

The forms 0·∞, ∞−∞, 1∞, 0⁰, and ∞⁰ require rewriting first.

h(x)=x²−91−(f(x))³,   limx→3h(x)=5

Exit Ticket

离堂检测

Evaluate limx→0 2x²/(ex−1−x). State why each use of L’Hôpital’s Rule is valid.

Answer: First substitution gives 0/0. After one application, 4x/(ex−1) is still 0/0. Apply again: 4/ex→4.每次使用前都重新确认0/0。
