2. Find the least N for which Σ(−1)n+1/n³ is guaranteed to have error below 10−4.3 points
Solve the inequality / 解不等式
Answer: 1/(N+1)³<10−4 gives N+1>∛10000≈21.54, so N=21.
3. A convergent alternating series starts positive. If S8=0.688 and b9=0.012, decide whether S8 is high or low and give an interval for S.4 points

Answer: Term 9 is positive, so S8 is low. Since S9=0.700, 0.688≤S≤0.700.
