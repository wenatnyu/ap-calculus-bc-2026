First move · Use the result as a diagnosis

Substitution Tells You What to Do Next

直接代入既可能给答案，也可能给下一步线索

Substitution givesMeaningNext move
a finite numberThe expression is ready.Report that value. 直接得到极限。
0/0The form is indeterminate.Rewrite: factor, cancel, or use a trig identity. 变形后再代入。
nonzero/0The quotient law cannot finish the problem.Compare the two sides; the limit may be DNE. 检查左右趋势。

Always substitute first. It is the fastest way to choose a reasonable next step.

Substitution is both a method and a diagnostic.03

逐行追问：finite number 时是否还需要分解？0/0 是否等于 0？nonzero/0 是否能立刻写 “infinity”？最后一问答案是否定的，要看方向。

Case 1 · Direct substitution

A Finite Substituted Value Finishes the Problem

代入得到有限值，就可以结束

limx→-1(x2+2x-4)
Substitute(-1)2+2(-1)-4
Simplify1-2-4=-5

No extra algebra is needed. Polynomials are continuous everywhere.

Language: “By direct substitution, the limit is negative five.”用直接代入法，极限为 -5。

Start simple; manipulate only when the substitution result requires it.04

例题来自官方 notes 第 1 题。让学生完整说一句英文答案，然后再写算式。

Case 2 · Indeterminate form

Zero Over Zero Is a Signal to Rewrite

看到 0/0，不停下来；先寻找公因式

limx→04x2-5xx
Diagnose00 is not an answer
Factorx(4x-5)x
Cancel4x-5  →  -5

The original expression is undefined at x=0, but the limit only asks what happens near 0.原式在点上无定义，不妨碍附近趋势有极限。

0/0 asks for a new expression with the same nearby behavior.05

例题来自官方 notes 第 3 题。问学生约分后是否可以写 “x=0”？不可以；应写 x≠0 时两式相等，再取极限。

Algebra checkpoint · Protect the structure

Cancel Factors, Never Separate Terms

只能约因式，不能约加减项

ValidInvalid
(x-4)(5x-1)x-4=5x-1

✓ A common factor multiplies the entire numerator.

x2-4x-4 ≠ x+1

✕ The numerator is not factored, and x-4 is not a factor of x2-4.

Safe habit: factor completely, circle the matching factors, then cancel.先完全因式分解，再圈出相同因式并约去。

Cancellation is multiplication in reverse.06

让学生口头区分 factor 与 term。右侧错误式故意选用不能整除的分子，避免给出“看起来只差一点”的错误捷径。

Worked example · Factor and cancel

Factoring Removes a Hole, Not the Nearby Trend

约去造成空点的因式，保留附近趋势

limx→-72x2+13x-7x+7
Factor(2x-1)(x+7)x+7
Cancel2x-1   (x≠-7)
Evaluate2(-7)-1=-15

The factor x+7 creates a hole at x=-7; it does not change the trend on either side.空点改变点值，不改变两侧附近趋势。

Why it works · Nearby equivalence

Cancellation Works Because Limits Ignore One Point

极限比较附近；单个点可以缺失

Original expression

(x-a)q(x)x-a

Undefined at x=a

→

Simplified expression

q(x)

Defined wherever q is defined

For every nearby input with x≠a, the two expressions have the same output. Therefore they have the same limit at a.

Limit language: “equal near the point” is enough.对极限而言，“在点附近相等”就足够。

A limit can exist even when the original formula is undefined at the target.08

不要引入正式 epsilon-delta 证明；用“去掉目标点后的邻域”解释即可。要求学生说出 x≠a 这个条件。

Case 3 · Nonzero over zero

Some Zero Denominators Produce DNE

分子趋近非零而分母趋近零，要比较左右方向

limx→-6x2+4x+3x+6  →  150
ApproachNumeratorDenominatorQuotient
x→-6-positive, near 15small negative→ -∞
x→-6+positive, near 15small positive→ +∞

The one-sided trends disagree. The two-sided limit is DNE.左侧趋于负无穷，右侧趋于正无穷，双侧极限不存在。

Do not write “15/0 = infinity.” Division by zero is undefined, and the sign depends on the side.

Reference · Angles must be in radians

Special Trig Limits Anchor the Unit

特殊三角极限必须使用弧度制

limx→0sin xx=1
limx→0xsin x=1
limx→01-cos xx=0
limx→0cos x-1x=0

Pattern check: the sine's input and the denominator must approach 0 together.要把式子配成 sin u/u 或 u/sin u 的标准形式。

Use radians; the degree-mode versions do not equal these constants.10

让学生把四个极限抄到词汇页背面。calculator 必须使用 radians。今天重点是识别与配形，不讲 squeeze theorem 的证明。

Trig example 1 · Build sin u over u

Match the Special Form Before Substituting

先配成标准形式，再使用特殊极限

limx→0sin(3x)x
Matchsin(3x)x=3·sin(3x)3x
Use u=3xlimx→0sin(3x)3x=1
Evaluate3·1=3

The coefficient inside sine reappears outside the limit.sin 内部的系数通过配形变成外部系数。

Trig example 2 · Two familiar factors

Ratios of Sines Reduce to Their Coefficients

两个正弦分别配成特殊极限

limx→0sin(7x)sin(9x)
Rewrite79·sin(7x)7x·9xsin(9x)
Evaluate79·1·1=79

Shortcut after understanding: for nonzero constants a,b, limx→0 sin(ax)/sin(bx)=a/b.理解配形过程后，可用系数之比。

Trig example 3 · Difference of squares

Factor a Trig Identity Before Using a Special Limit

先用平方差分解，再识别特殊极限

limx→0cos2x-1x(cos x+1)
Factor(cos x-1)(cos x+1)x(cos x+1)
Cancelcos x-1x
Recognizelimx→0cos x-1x=0

Near x=0, cos x+1 is nonzero, so the cancellation is valid.在 0 附近 cos x+1 非零，可以约分。

Mixed trig · Split the structure

A Product Can Hide More Than One Familiar Limit

复杂式子可以拆成多个熟悉的极限

limx→0(1-cos2x)sin xx2
Factor1-cos xx·(1+cos x)·sin xx
Read limits0 · 2 · 1
Result0

Do not substitute into the original expression first and stop at 0/0. Rewrite until each factor has a known limit.把复杂未定式拆成已知极限的乘积。

Your turn · Two algebraic forms

Guided Practice: Diagnose, Rewrite, Evaluate

先判断代入结果，再选择变形

A · Factor and cancel

limx→45x2-21x+4x-4

Write the factorization before canceling.

B · Watch the reversed factor

limx→52x2-17x+355-x

Use 5-x=-(x-5).

A: 19. 5x²-21x+4=(5x-1)(x-4).   B: -3. 2x²-17x+35=(2x-7)(x-5) and 5-x=-(x-5).

Common error: forgetting the negative sign when the denominator reverses a factor.分母因式次序相反时，别漏掉负号。

Exit ticket · Explain one cancellation

Exit Ticket: Make 0/0 Useful

把未定式变成可计算的极限

limx→ax2-2ax+a2x-a
  1. Factor the numerator.
  2. State the restriction used when canceling.
  3. Evaluate the limit.
Answer: 0. x²-2ax+a²=(x-a)². For x≠a, cancel one factor to get x-a; then x-a→0.

You can now turn an undefined expression into an equivalent nearby expression: substitute, diagnose, rewrite, evaluate.代入、判断、变形、求值。

Official test-prep problem 26.16

官方 test prep 第 26 题，正确选项 C，答案 0。收尾要求一名学生用完整英文说出 cancellation restriction: “for x not equal to a.”
