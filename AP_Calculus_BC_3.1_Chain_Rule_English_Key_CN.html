The Chain Rule

核心公式

ddx f(g(x)) = f′(g(x)) · g′(x)

dydx = dydu · dudx

The inner expression stays in place. Only after the outer derivative is written do we multiply by the inner derivative.内层表达式先原样保留。

AP Calculus BC · 3.103

两种记号并列：函数记号适合结构识别，Leibniz 记号适合分层。

Name the Layers

先分层，再求导

y=(3x²−1)⁵

u=3x²−1,   y=u⁵

outer: u⁵

inner: 3x²−1

If replacing an expression with u makes a familiar function, a chain is present.能用 u 替换出熟悉外层，说明需要链式法则。

A Power Outside

幂函数在外层

y=(x²−5)⁴

y′=4(x²−5)³ · 2x

y′=8x(x²−5)³

Common miss: writing only 4(x²−5)³ and forgetting 2x.最常见错误是漏乘内层导数。

Logarithms: Simplify with Care

对数复合可化简

y=ln(x³)

y′=1x³·3x²=3x

The result is valid where the original function is defined: x>0 for ln(x³).化简后仍要保留原函数定义域。

ln(x³)=3ln x only on the original real domain x>0.

AP Calculus BC · 3.108

结果 3/x 不能让学生误以为 x<0 也属于原函数定义域。

Rules Can Be Combined

多个规则可以叠加

F(x)=x·g(h(x))

F′(x)=g(h(x))+x·g′(h(x))h′(x)

Outside structure: product rule.

Inside the second term: chain rule.先看最外层结构。

Tangent Line after Differentiating

求导后写切线

f(x)=√(x²−9),   x=5

f(5)=4,   f′(x)=x√(x²−9)

f′(5)=54

y−4=54(x−5)

A tangent line needs both a point and a slope.切线方程必须同时有点和斜率。

Three Errors to Catch

三种高频错误

Missing factor

(u⁵)′ ≠ 5u⁴

Multiply by u′.

Changed inside

sin(x²)′ ≠ cos(2x)

Keep x²: 2x cos(x²).

Stopped early

Nested layers remain

Continue until every layer is differentiated.

Exam Check

结构判断选择题

If y=ln(2x³), then y′ = ?

  • A.1/(2x³)
  • B.6x²·ln(2x³)
  • C.3/x
  • D.6x²
C. (1/(2x³))·6x²=3/x, on the original domain x>0.链式法则后再化简。
AP Calculus BC · 3.114
