Ratio Test for Convergence

比值判别法

L=lim|an+1/an|: L<1 gives absolute convergence; L>1 gives divergence.

The ratio test is especially efficient for factorials and exponential powers. / 比值判别法特别适合阶乘与指数项。

Core lesson

Ratio Test Statement

比值判别法陈述

For an nonzero eventually, let

L=limn→∞|an+1/an|.

  • L<1: absolute convergence.
  • L>1 or L=∞: divergence.
  • L=1: inconclusive.

Core lesson

Why Ratios Help

为什么比较相邻项

If successive magnitudes eventually shrink by a factor below 1, the tail behaves like a convergent geometric series.

Factorials and exponential powers simplify especially well in a ratio.

Exponential over Factorial

指数除以阶乘

an=3n/n!, |an+1/an|=3/(n+1)→0.

The series converges absolutely.

Factorial over Exponential

阶乘除以指数

an=n!/3n, |an+1/an|=(n+1)/3→∞.

The series diverges.

多项式除以指数

Core lesson

The Case L=1

L 等于 1 时无法判定

For Σ1/n and Σ1/n2, the ratio limit is 1.

One diverges and one converges, so L=1 gives no conclusion.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1009

Core lesson

Absolute Values Matter

必须取绝对值

Alternating signs disappear inside |an+1/an|.

If L<1, the absolute-value series converges and hence the original series converges.

Powers Depending on n

含 n 次幂的项

an=n!/nn

|an+1/an|=(n/(n+1))n→e-1<1, so the series converges absolutely.

Core lesson

When to Choose the Ratio Test

何时优先使用比值判别

Look for factorials, constants raised to n, or products with n-dependent powers.

For rational functions of n alone, comparison tests are usually simpler.

Core lesson

Ratio-Test Workflow

比值判别流程

  1. Write an+1.
  2. Form the absolute ratio.
  3. Cancel before taking the limit.
  4. Compare L with 1.
  5. State the exact conclusion.
