Lagrange Error Bound

拉格朗日误差界

A Taylor polynomial gives an approximation. The Lagrange bound controls how far the approximation can be from the function.

误差界给出最坏情况下的可靠上限。

Core lesson

The Bound

误差界公式

If Pn is centered at a and |f(n+1)(z)|≤M between a and x, then

|f(x)−Pn(x)|≤M|x−a|n+1/(n+1)!.

Core lesson

The Relevant Interval

选择正确区间

The derivative bound must hold on every point between the center and the evaluation point.

If a=2 and x=1.4, use the interval [1.4,2].

区间取 a 与 x 之间，不要只看端点。

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1004

For a bound over a whole target interval, M must work over the union of all center-to-target intervals.

One Derivative Beyond the Degree

使用高一阶导数

Core lesson

Choosing M

确定导数上界 M

Any valid M with |f(n+1)(z)|≤M works on the relevant interval.

A smaller valid M gives a tighter bound, but a convenient larger bound may be easier to justify.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1006

Do not use f^(n+1)(x) automatically. It is valid only if its absolute value bounds the derivative everywhere on the interval.

Core lesson

Example: eˣ at 0.5

示例：近似 e⁰·⁵

Use P3 for ex centered at 0.

On [0,0.5], |f(4)(z)|=ez≤e0.5.

|R3(0.5)|≤e0.5(0.5)⁴/4!=e0.5/384≈0.004294.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1007

The maximum occurs at the right endpoint because e^z is increasing.

Core lesson

Example: sin(0.3)

示例：近似 sin(0.3)

For P3(x)=x−x³/3!, use |f(4)(z)|=|sin z|≤1.

|R3(0.3)|≤(0.3)⁴/4!=3.375×10−4.

A sharper M is possible, but M=1 gives a simple valid guarantee.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1008

The official end review uses this standard M=1 bound. Stress that a valid bound need not be the least possible bound.

Core lesson

A Nonzero Center

非零展开中心

Approximate ex−1 at x=1.4 with P2 centered at 1.

On [1,1.4], take M=e0.4.

|R2(1.4)|≤e0.4(0.4)³/3!≈0.015913.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1009

The distance is |1.4-1|=0.4. Do not substitute x=1.4 in the power factor.

Core lesson

Choosing the Degree

确定所需次数

For e0.8 centered at 0, take M=e0.8 and test degrees until

e0.8(0.8)n+1/(n+1)!<0.005.

Degree 4 gives about 0.00608; degree 5 gives about 0.000810. The least degree is 5.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1011

Students should test the previous degree to prove minimality.

Core lesson

Two Different Error Theorems

区分两种误差定理

Alternating series: error is bounded by the first omitted term when its magnitudes decrease to zero.

Taylor polynomial: error is bounded using an (n+1)st derivative bound over an interval.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1012

Some Taylor series are alternating, so both bounds may apply. They are separate theorems and may give different numerical bounds.

Core lesson

Common Errors

常见错误

  • Using the nth derivative instead of the (n+1)st
  • Choosing M at one point without checking the interval
  • Using x instead of |x−a|
  • Forgetting the factorial
