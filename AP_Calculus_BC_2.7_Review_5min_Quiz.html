Directions: Apply constant, constant-multiple, and sum/difference rules. For a join, check value and slope.
要求：逐项求导；分段连接点需同时检查函数值与斜率。

Fast check: A horizontal tangent has derivative 0. Differentiability at a join needs continuity and matching one-sided derivatives.

1. Find f′(x) for f(x)=4x3−3x2+6x−π.3 points
Work / 过程：
Answer: f′(x)=12x2−6x+6. π 是常数。
2. Find every x-value where g(x)=x3−3x has a horizontal tangent.3 points
Set the derivative equal to 0 / 令导数为 0：
Answer: g′(x)=3x2−3=3(x−1)(x+1); therefore x=−1,1.
3. Find a and b so that p(x)={x2+a, x<1; bx+1, x≥1} is differentiable at x=1.4 points
Continuity and equal derivatives / 连续且左右导数相等：
Answer: Continuity gives 1+a=b+1, so a=b. Matching slopes gives 2=b. Thus a=2, b=2. 两个条件都要满足。

  • Q3: 2 for continuity; 2 for matching derivatives.
