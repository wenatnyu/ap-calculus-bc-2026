Displacement Is Not Distance

位移不等于路程

Displacement: ∫v(t)dt, a vector.

Distance: ∫||v(t)||dt, a nonnegative scalar.

Acceleration to Velocity

If acceleration and an initial velocity are given, integrate once to find velocity. Integrate again, with an initial position, to find position.

Quick check · Reveal

Reveal: Initial Position

揭晓：利用初始位置

Try it. Given v(t)=⟨2t,3⟩ and r(1)=⟨4,−2⟩, find r(t).

Answer. An antiderivative is ⟨t²,3t⟩+⟨C₁,C₂⟩. Using t=1 gives C=⟨3,−5⟩, so r(t)=⟨t²+3,3t−5⟩.

Exit ticket

Reveal: Displacement vs Distance

揭晓：位移与路程

Try it. A particle has v(t)=⟨t,1⟩ for 0≤t≤2. Give its displacement vector and a distance integral.

Answer. Displacement is ∫₀²⟨t,1⟩dt=⟨2,2⟩. Distance is ∫₀²√(t²+1)dt.
