Directions: Evaluate each limit. Show the algebraic step that makes the limit clear. Write DNE when the two-sided limit does not exist.
要求：求每个极限，并写出关键代数步骤；若双侧极限不存在，写 DNE。

Timing: about 1 minute per item. Question 4 checks whether students distinguish the indeterminate form 0/0 from a nonzero number divided by a quantity approaching zero.

1. Evaluate by direct substitution: limx→−2(3x²−x+1). 2 points
Work / 计算：
Answer: 15. Polynomials are continuous, so substitute x=−2: 3(4)+2+1=15. 多项式可直接代入。
2. Evaluate limx→4x²−16x−4. 3 points
Key algebraic step / 关键步骤：
Answer: 8. Factor x²−16=(x−4)(x+4), cancel x−4 for x≠4, then take limx→4(x+4)=8. 先因式分解，再约去公共因子。
3. Evaluate limx→0sin(5x)2x. 3 points
Rewrite using limu→0 sin u/u=1：
Answer: 5/2. Write sin(5x)2x=52sin(5x)5x; the second factor approaches 1. 把分母配成角度 5x。
4. Decide whether limx→−6x²+4x+3x+6 exists. 2 points
Reason / 理由：
Answer: DNE. The numerator approaches 15, not 0, while the denominator changes sign through 0. The one-sided limits are opposite infinities. 分子趋近非零数，分母过零变号，左右趋势相反。

Scoring / 评分

  • Q1: 1 point for substitution; 1 for 15.
  • Q2: 1 for factoring; 1 for cancellation; 1 for 8.
  • Q3: 2 for a correct special-limit rewrite; 1 for 5/2.
  • Q4: 1 for nonzero/0 with sign change; 1 for DNE.
