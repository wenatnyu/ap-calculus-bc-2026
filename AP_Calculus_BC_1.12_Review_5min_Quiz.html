1. State the three conditions required for f to be continuous at x=c.3 points
1. ____________________   2. ____________________   3. ____________________
Answer: (1) f(c) is defined; (2) limx→cf(x) exists; (3) limx→cf(x)=f(c). 点值有定义、双侧极限存在、极限等于点值。

Decision and reason / 结论与理由：
Answer: yes. The one-sided limits agree, so the two-sided limit is 1, and that value equals f(3)=1. 左右极限与点值都为 1，三条件全部成立。
3. Let g(x)=x²+1 for x≤2 and g(x)=3x+k for x>2. Find k so that g is continuous at x=2.5 points
Boundary equation / 交界处方程：
Solve and verify / 求解并验证：
Answer: k=−1. The left piece gives g(2)=2²+1=5. The right-hand limit is 3(2)+k=6+k. Set 6+k=5, giving k=−1; then both sides and the point value equal 5. 令交界处两段趋近值相等，并核对点值。
