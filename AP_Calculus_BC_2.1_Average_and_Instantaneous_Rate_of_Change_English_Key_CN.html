Recall: Rate of Change Is Slope

变化率就是“输出变化量 ÷ 输入变化量”

slope = y₂−y₁x₂−x₁ = ΔyΔx

units = dependent-variable unitsindependent-variable units

Positive

The second endpoint output is greater than the first.

Negative

The second endpoint output is less than the first.

Zero

The two endpoint outputs are equal.

Always attach meaning and units to a contextual rate.03

要求学生说 dependent per independent。例如 feet per minute、dollars per year。负的平均变化率只说明终点输出低于起点，不保证区间内一直下降；在位移语境中，负号表示平均速度的方向。

Two inputs · One secant slope

Average Rate of Change

在区间 [a,b] 上连接两个端点

AROC on [a,b]

= f(b)−f(a)b−a

= slope of the secant line

Equivalent interval form

f(a+h)−f(a)h,   h≠0

Average rate uses two distinct inputs. The order in the numerator must match the order in the denominator.分子分母的端点顺序必须一致。

An interval may be written [a,b], a≤x≤b, or from a to b.04

比较两种写法：(f(b)−f(a))/(b−a) 与 (f(a+h)−f(a))/h。第二种中右端点是 a+h，区间长度是 h。

Official notes · Function example

Average Rate from a Formula

把区间端点代入函数，再做“后减前”

f(x)=ln(3x),   1≤x≤4

f(4)−f(1)4−1 = ln12−ln33

= ln43 ≈ 0.462

Why ln 4?

ln12−ln3=ln(12/3)=ln4.

This is an average rate across the entire interval, not the rate at a single point.这是整个区间上的平均变化率。

Use radians for trigonometric calculus unless told otherwise.05

先保持精确值 ln4/3，再给近似值。明确这条割线斜率不能叫 x=1 或 x=4 的瞬时变化率。

Graphical meaning

A Secant Uses the Endpoints

割线连接区间两端的图像点

Read rise over run

slope = f(b)−f(a)b−a

The curve may bend between the endpoints. The average rate still uses only the two endpoint points.

The secant slope summarizes the net output change across the interval.割线斜率总结区间内的净变化。

Average behavior need not match the rate at every interior point.07

用直尺比对割线与曲线。曲线内部可能先快后慢、甚至改变方向，但端点割线仍给整个区间的平均变化率。

One input · A limiting tangent slope

Instantaneous Rate of Change

让割线区间缩小到一个点

h-formx-form
limh→0f(a+h)−f(a)hlimx→af(x)−f(a)x−a
Second input: a+hSecond input: x

Both limits give the slope of the tangent line at x=a.两种差商形式表示同一个瞬时变化率。

The quotient at h=0 is undefined. The limit of nearby quotients may still exist.不能把 h 直接代成 0。

Instantaneous rate = limit of average rates09

把两个式子并排逐项对应：x=a+h，所以 x−a=h。再次回扣 1.1：瞬时变化率不能用零宽度区间直接相除，而是取极限。

Official notes · Difference quotient

Use the h-Form

在 a=−1 处求 f(x)=x−x² 的瞬时变化率

limh→0f(−1+h)−f(−1)h

f(−1+h)=−2+3h−h²,   f(−1)=−2

= limh→03h−h²h = limh→0(3−h)=3

Instantaneous rate: 3. Factor and cancel only for h≠0, then take the limit.先在 h≠0 时化简，再取极限。

The cancellation removes the 0/0 form, not the limit process.10

逐行展开 (-1+h)^2。常见错误是漏掉负号：f=x−x²，所以展开后为 −2+3h−h²。最终答案 3。

Official practice 18 · Same tangent slope

Use the x-Form

在 a=−3 处使用 x→a 的差商形式

f(x)=2x²+3x−4,   f(−3)=5

limx→−32x²+3x−9x+3

= limx→−3(x+3)(2x−3)x+3 = −9

Match the base point

The denominator x+3=x−(−3) confirms that a=−3.

Instantaneous rate: −9.负号表示切线斜率为负。

The numerator must be f(x)−f(a), not f(x)−a.11

先算 f(−3)=5，所以分子是 f(x)−5。因式分解后约去 x+3，最后代入 x=−3 得 −9。

Identify the original function and base point

Read a Difference Quotient Backward

从差商识别原函数与瞬时变化率所在位置

h-form

limh→05ln(4+h)−5ln4h

f(x)=5ln x · a=4

对照 f(a+h)−f(a)。

x-form

limx→π/2sin x−1x−π/2

f(x)=sin x · a=π/2

因为 sin(π/2)=1。

Use precise contextual language

Position Is Not Distance Traveled

位置、位移与路程的措辞要分清

QuantityCan be negative?Correct rate language
position s(t)Yesaverage velocity / change in position per time
displacement s(b)−s(a)Yesnet change in position
distance traveledNototal path length; needs motion information

If AROC of position is −4 m/s: say “average velocity is −4 m/s” or “position decreases by 4 meters per second on average.” Do not say “negative distance traveled.”负号表示位移方向，不表示路程为负。

Context words carry mathematical meaning.13

位置是带方向的坐标，因此变化量和平均速度可以为负；路程是路径总长度，不能为负，且通常不能只用两个位置端点求出。

Official test prep 29 · Multiple choice

Choose the Correct Average-Rate Equation

把“区间 [2,a] 上平均变化率为 41”翻译成方程

f(x)=ln(7x),   a>2

  • A.f(a)=41
  • B.f(a)−f(2)=41
  • C.f(a)−f(2)a−2=41
  • D.f(a)+f(2)2=41
C. Average rate of change is output change divided by input change: [f(a)−f(2)]/(a−2)=41.分子是函数值之差，分母是输入之差。
Do not confuse average rate with an average of two outputs.14

官方答案 C。A 只给函数值；B 只给输出变化量；D 是输出的算术平均，不是变化率。

Interpret an Average Velocity

公式、单位与情境解释缺一不可

s(t)=t²−4t meters

Find AROC on [1,5].

Then state the displacement and interpret the rate.

AROC = 2 m/s. Since s(1)=−3 and s(5)=5, displacement is 8 m and 8/(5−1)=2 m/s. The particle's position increases by 2 meters per second on average. The average-rate calculation alone does not report total distance traveled.位移为 8 米，平均速度为 2 米/秒；平均变化率本身不等于路程。
