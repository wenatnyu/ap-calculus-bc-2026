Directions: Name the outer rule, then calculate. Preserve any original domain restriction.
要求：先写最外层法则，再计算；保留原函数定义域限制。

Fast check: Structure determines the first rule: product, quotient, chain, or implicit differentiation.

1. Differentiate y=x²e3x. Name the two rules used.3 points
Rules and derivative / 法则与导数：
Answer: y′=e3x(2x+3x²). Use product rule, with chain rule inside the exponential derivative. 外层乘积，内部链式。
2. Differentiate f(x)=ln x/x.3 points
Derivative / 导数：
Answer: f′(x)=(1−ln x)/x², for x>0. 可用商法则，并保留定义域。
3. Find the tangent line to 9x²+16y²=52 at (2,−1).4 points
Implicit derivative and line / 隐函数导数与切线：
Answer: y+1=(9/8)(x−2). From 18x+32y y′=0, the slope at (2,−1) is 9/8. 负坐标代入时注意符号。
