Directions: Compare original-function values at every required candidate.
要求：在所有候选输入处比较原函数值。

Fast check: On a closed interval, include both endpoints and every interior critical number.

1. For the Candidates Test on [a,b], which inputs must be checked?3 points
Candidate list / 候选列表：
Answer: Check every critical number in (a,b) and both endpoints a,b.
2. Find every absolute maximum and minimum of f(x)=x³−3x on [−2,2].3 points
Candidates and values / 候选点与函数值：
Answer: Check x=−2,−1,1,2; the values are −2,2,−2,2. Maximum 2 at x=−1,2; minimum −2 at x=−2,1.
3. Why must endpoints be included even though their two-sided derivatives may not exist?4 points
Reason / 理由：
Answer: An absolute maximum or minimum on a closed interval can occur at an endpoint, so both endpoint function values must be compared.
