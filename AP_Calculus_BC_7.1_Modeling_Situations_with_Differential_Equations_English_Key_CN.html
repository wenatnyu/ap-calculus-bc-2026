What the Equation Records

微分方程记录什么

Current state

Y tells how much is present.

Change

dY/dx tells how fast that amount changes.

The equation may contain the independent variable, the dependent variable, or both.右侧可以含自变量、因变量，或两者同时出现。

Core lesson

Direct Proportion

成正比

A is proportional to B ⇒ A=kB

“The rate of change of y with respect to t is proportional to t².”

dy/dt=kt²

Keep the constant k unless data determine it.没有数值条件时保留比例常数 k。

Inverse and Joint Proportion

反比与联合比例

Inverse

A=k/B

Square root

A=k√B

Joint

A=kBC

“Proportional to √u and inversely proportional to v” becomes A=k√u/v.and 表示相乘；inversely 表示放到分母。

Core lesson

Use Direction to Set the Sign

用增减方向判断符号

Increasing

dY/dt>0

Often k>0.

Decreasing

dY/dt<0

Write −k with k>0, or allow k<0.

State the sign convention. Do not write both a negative sign and k<0.负号只能计算一次。

Acceleration Means a Second Derivative

加速度对应二阶导数

The acceleration of h(t) is proportional to ∛t.

h″(t)=k∛t

If h″(8)=6, then

6=2k ⇒ k=3

Units of h″ are height units per time squared.加速度单位是长度/时间²。

Units Are a Built-in Check

Build and Calibrate a Decay Model

A population P decreases at a rate proportional to its size. When P=80, it decreases by 12 units per day. Write the differential equation.

Answer: P′=kP and −12=80k, so P′=−(3/20)P.decreases 决定导数为负。
