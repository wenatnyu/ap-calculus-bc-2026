What Makes Rates Related?

为什么叫相关变化率

V=s³

Horizontal and vertical distances are linked.

Every Changing Variable Depends on Time

每个变化量都依赖时间

x=x(t),   y=y(t),   z=z(t)

ddt(x²+y²)=2xdxdt+2ydydt

Do not write 2x+2y; the chain rule supplies a time-rate for each changing variable.

AP Calculus BC · 4.404

把 x(t)、y(t) 显式写一次，帮助学生理解链式因子。

Pythagorean Relationship

勾股关系

x²+y²=z²

2xdxdt+2ydydt=2zdzdt

Each term pairs a quantity with its own rate.每一项都由“量 × 该量的变化率”组成。

Cylinder with Constant Radius

半径不变的圆柱

V=πr²h

If r is constant, then dr/dt=0.

Ladder with Constant Length

固定长度的梯子

Guided Setup: Boat and Pulley

课堂练习：船与滑轮

A pulley is a fixed height h above a boat. Horizontal distance is x; rope length is r. Write the rate equation.

Answer: x²+h²=r², so 2x(dx/dt)=2r(dr/dt) because dh/dt=0.滑轮高度固定。
AP Calculus BC · 4.413

只要求关系式和求导式，不代数字。

Exit Ticket

离堂检测

Given L=k/M, where k is constant, write dL/dt.

Answer: L=kM−1, so dL/dt=−(k/M²)(dM/dt).把 M 的倒数看作 M−1 并使用链式法则。
AP Calculus BC · 4.415

官方 test prep 关系题，检查负号和 dM/dt。
