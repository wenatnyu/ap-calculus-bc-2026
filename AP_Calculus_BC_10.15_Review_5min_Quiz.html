1. Write the first four nonzero terms and general term of the Maclaurin series for cos x.3 points
Known series / 基本级数
Answer: 1−x²/2!+x⁴/4!−x⁶/6!+⋯=Σn=0∞(−1)nx2n/(2n)!.
2. Write the first four terms of 1/(1+3x) and state its interval of convergence.3 points
Geometric substitution / 几何代换
Answer: 1−3x+9x²−27x³+⋯, valid when |−3x|<1, so −1/3<x<1/3.
3. Find the coefficient of x⁶ in the Maclaurin series for e2x²/2.4 points
Coefficient / 系数
Answer: The n=3 term is (1/2)(2x²)³/3!=(2/3)x⁶, so the coefficient is 2/3.
