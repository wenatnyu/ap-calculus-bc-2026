Directions: Rewrite roots and reciprocals as powers when useful. Differentiate with respect to x.
要求：必要时先把根式、分式改写成幂，再对 x 求导。

Fast check: Multiply by the exponent, then subtract 1 from the exponent. Constants have derivative 0.

1. Find f′(x) if f(x)=3x5−4√x+7.3 points
Work / 过程：
Answer: f′(x)=15x4−2x−1/2=15x4−2/√x. 常数 7 的导数为 0。
2. Differentiate g(x)=2/x3−5∛x.3 points
Rewrite first / 先改写：
Answer: g(x)=2x−3−5x1/3, so g′(x)=−6x−4−(5/3)x−2/3.
3. Let h(x)=x3−4/x. Find h′(2).4 points
Derivative, then substitute / 先求导，再代入：
Answer: h′(x)=3x2+4x−2, so h′(2)=12+1=13. 先求导后代入。

  • Q3: 2 for the derivative; 2 for the value 13.
