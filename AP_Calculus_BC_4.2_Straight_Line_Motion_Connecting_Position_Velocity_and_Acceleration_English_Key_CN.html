Velocity Gives Direction

速度符号给出方向

v>0

Moving right

Positive direction.向正方向运动。

v<0

Moving left

Negative direction.向负方向运动。

v=0

At rest

Check signs around the time to decide whether direction changes.

Velocity Is Not Speed

速度不等于速率

velocity = v(t)

Includes direction.带方向与正负号。

speed = |v(t)|

Always nonnegative.速率始终非负。

If v=−7 m/s, the speed is 7 m/s.

AP Calculus BC · 4.205

让学生把 negative velocity 翻译为向负方向运动，而不是负的速率。

Displacement and Average Velocity

位移与平均速度

displacement on [a,b] = s(b)−s(a)

average velocity = s(b)−s(a)b−a

Displacement depends only on the endpoints; total distance must account for every turn.位移只看端点；路程要分段处理转向。

AP Calculus BC · 4.206

本节 packet 重点是位移与平均速度；顺带预告总路程需按转向分段。

VelocityAccelerationResult

Same signs → speeding up. Opposite signs → slowing down.同号加速，异号减速。

Rest and Direction Change

静止与改变方向

v(t)=3t²−8t=t(3t−8)

v(t)=0 at t=0 and t=8/3

For interior time t=8/3, check a sign chart: velocity changes from negative to positive, so direction changes.速度由负变正，粒子从向左改为向右。

Guided Practice

课堂练习

At one instant, v=−2 m/s and a=5 m/s². State the direction and whether the particle is speeding up.

Answer: It is moving left and slowing down. Velocity and acceleration have opposite signs.向左运动且减速；速度与加速度异号。
AP Calculus BC · 4.213

先问方向，再问 speed 的变化；避免把两问混成一句。

AP Check

AP 选择题检查

A particle has s(t)=t³−6t²+9t. What is happening at t=1?

  • A.moving right
  • B.moving left
  • C.at rest, velocity decreasing
  • D.at rest, velocity increasing
C. v=3(t−1)(t−3), so v(1)=0; a=6t−12, so a(1)=−6.静止且速度值正在减小。
AP Calculus BC · 4.214

注意 velocity decreasing 不等于 speed decreasing；在静止瞬间只描述 v 的变化趋势。

Exit Ticket

离堂检测

For s(t)=2t³−9t²+12t, find v(2), a(2), and decide speeding up or slowing down.

Answer: v=6t²−18t+12, so v(2)=0; a=12t−18, so a(2)=6. At that exact instant it is at rest; inspect nearby signs before claiming “speeding up.”该瞬间静止，不能只凭 a>0 说“正在加速”。
AP Calculus BC · 4.215

本题用于纠正常见口号滥用：v=0 时同号/异号规则不直接适用。
