1. State the Lagrange error bound for a degree n Taylor polynomial centered at a.3 points
Formula and condition / 公式与条件
Answer: If |f(n+1)(z)|≤M between a and x, then |f(x)−Pn(x)|≤M|x−a|n+1/(n+1)!.
2. A second-degree Taylor polynomial has M=4 for the third derivative, and |x−a|=0.3. Find the bound.3 points
Substitute / 代入
Answer: 4(0.3)³/3!=0.018.
3. With M=1 and |x−a|=0.4, find the least degree n that guarantees an error below 0.001.4 points
Test degrees / 检验次数
Answer: n=4. Degree 3 gives 0.4⁴/4!≈0.0010667; degree 4 gives 0.4⁵/5!≈0.00008533.
