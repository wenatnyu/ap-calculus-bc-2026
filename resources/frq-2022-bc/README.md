# 2022 AP Calculus BC Free-Response Practice

The full question booklet is the official 2022 AP Calculus BC free-response booklet distributed by College Board and linked by the FlippedMath 2022 BC practice page.

The scoring-guidelines file is the official College Board 2022 AP Calculus BC scoring-guidelines PDF. College Board retired the old direct PDF URL from its current past-exam page, so this copy was retrieved from the Internet Archive capture of that original College Board URL dated 2022-12-05.

## Source URLs

- FlippedMath practice page: <https://calculus.flippedmath.com/2022-bc.html>
- FlippedMath-hosted official question booklet: <https://calculus.flippedmath.com/uploads/1/1/3/0/11305589/frq_bc_2022.pdf>
- Original College Board scoring URL: <https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap22-sg-calculus-bc.pdf>
- Archived capture of the College Board scoring PDF: <https://web.archive.org/web/20221205152125id_/https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap22-sg-calculus-bc.pdf>

## Local files

- `questions.pdf`: complete 11-page question booklet
- `scoring-guidelines.pdf`: complete 22-page scoring guide
- `question-1.pdf` through `question-6.pdf`: one-question classroom copies
- `scoring-question-1.pdf` through `scoring-question-6.pdf`: matching scoring-guide excerpts

Question pages in the full booklet are 3, 4, 7, 8, 9, and 10. Scoring-guide page ranges are 2–5, 6–9, 10–12, 13–16, 17–19, and 20–22.
