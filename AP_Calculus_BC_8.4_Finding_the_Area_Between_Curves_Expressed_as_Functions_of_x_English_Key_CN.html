AP Calculus BC · Topic 8.4

Finding the Area Between Curves Expressed as Functions of x

关于 x 的曲线间面积

Area = ∫ab[top − bottom] dx

With vertical slices, area is the integral of nonnegative vertical width. / 使用竖直切片时，面积是“上减下”的积分。

Top Minus Bottom

上减下

A=∫ab[f(x)−g(x)]dx, where f(x)≥g(x)

Area must be nonnegative.

Quick Check

课堂即时检测

Find the area enclosed by y=4−x² and y=x².

Answer: The intersections are x=±√2. Area = ∫−√2√2(4−2x²)dx = 16√2/3.
