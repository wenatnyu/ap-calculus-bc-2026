AP Calculus BC · Topic 8.5

Finding Area Between Curves Expressed as Functions of y

关于 y 的曲线间面积

Area = ∫cd[right − left] dy

With horizontal slices, area is the integral of nonnegative horizontal width. / 使用水平切片时，面积是“右减左”的积分。

Core lesson

Right Minus Left

右减左

A=∫cd[r(y)−ℓ(y)]dy, where r(y)≥ℓ(y)

The horizontal width must be nonnegative.

Core lesson

Choose the Easier Orientation

选择更简单的切片方向

  • Use dx if one top-minus-bottom integral works.
  • Use dy if horizontal slices avoid multiple pieces.
  • Either correct orientation gives the same area.

Branches Matter

注意函数分支

x²=y ⇒ x=−√y or x=√y

For the full horizontal span, width may be √y−(−√y)=2√y.

Quick Check

课堂即时检测

Find the area bounded by x=y² and x=2y.

Answer: The intersections are y=0 and y=2. Area = ∫₀²(2y−y²)dy=4/3.
