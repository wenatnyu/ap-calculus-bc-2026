Simplify Before You Differentiate

能化简时先化简

Original

f(x)=x³+xx

Simplified on x≠0

f(x)=x²+1

f′(x)=2x

Keep the original domain restriction x≠0.约分不能补回原来缺失的点。

AP Calculus BC · 3.504

化简是为了减少出错，但定义域必须继承原式。

Product Rule plus a Basic Derivative

乘积法则

f(x)=x²ln x

f′(x)=2x ln x+x²·1x

f′(x)=2x ln x+x

The outer operation is multiplication, not composition.两个因子并列相乘，使用乘积法则。

AP Calculus BC · 3.505

x² 与 ln x 都依赖 x，所以两个乘积项都要保留。

Quotient Rule and Simplification

Implicit Tangent Line

隐函数切线

9x²+16y²=52 at (2,−1)

18x+32y y′=0

y′=98

y+1=98(x−2)

Implicit relation → implicit differentiation → point-slope form.

AP Calculus BC · 3.508

Horizontal Tangents Need an Equation

水平切线还要解方程

f(x)=x e−x

f′(x)=e−x−x e−x=e−x(1−x)

f′(x)=0 → x=1

Because e−x>0, only 1−x=0 can make the derivative zero.先求导，再解 f′(x)=0。

Common Procedure Errors

方法选择常见错误

Product vs chain

x²sin x is a product; sin(x²) is a composite.

Lost domain

Simplification does not restore excluded inputs.

Stopped too soon

Differentiate every inner piece.

Guided Practice

课堂练习

Differentiate y=ln xx.

y′=1−ln xx². Quotient rule: [(1/x)x−ln x]/x².也可先写 y=(ln x)x−1 用乘积法则。

Exam Check

先选法则再计算

For y=x²e3x, which rules are required?

  • A.Power only
  • B.Product only
  • C.Product and chain
  • D.Quotient and chain
C. Product rule joins x² and e3x; the exponential factor needs a chain derivative.最外层乘积，内部指数复合。
AP Calculus BC · 3.514

延伸结果：y′=e^{3x}(2x+3x²)。
