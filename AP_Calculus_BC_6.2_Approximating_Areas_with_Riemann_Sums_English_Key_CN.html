Rectangle Approximation

矩形近似

area ≈ Σ(height)(width)

≈ Σ f(sample point)·Δx

Every term must include its interval width. 每一项都要乘对应区间宽度。

Four Standard Sums

四种常见近似

Left

Use each left endpoint.

Right

Use each right endpoint.

Midpoint

Use each interval midpoint.

Trapezoidal: average the two endpoint heights on each subinterval.

Topic 6.2 · Core lesson

Worked Example: f(x)=x²

例题：左、右端点和

[0,4], n=4 ⇒ Δx=1

L₄=(0²+1²+2²+3²)(1)=14

R₄=(1²+2²+3²+4²)(1)=30

Because f is increasing, L₄ underestimates and R₄ overestimates.

Midpoint and Trapezoid

中点和与梯形和

M₄=(0.5²+1.5²+2.5²+3.5²)(1)=21

T₄=(L₄+R₄)/2=22

For equal subintervals, the trapezoidal sum is the average of matching left and right sums.

Unequal Subintervals

不等宽子区间

partition: 0, 1, 3, 6

widths: 1, 2, 3

left sum = f(0)(1)+f(1)(2)+f(3)(3)

Never use one common Δx when widths differ. 逐段写宽度。

Concavity and Error

凹凸性与误差方向

Concave up

Trapezoids tend to overestimate; midpoints tend to underestimate.

Concave down

Trapezoids tend to underestimate; midpoints tend to overestimate.

From a Table

T = 2(4+7)/2 + 3(7+3)/2 = 26

Unequal widths are handled one trapezoid at a time.

Quick Check

课堂即时检测

For f(x)=x² on [0,4] with four equal subintervals, what is L₄?

  1. A14
  2. B21
  3. C22
  4. D30
Answer: A. Use heights f(0),f(1),f(2),f(3) and Δx=1.左端点依次为 0、1、2、3。

Exit Ticket

Use a trapezoidal sum for x=0,2,5 with f(x)=4,7,3.

Answer: T=2(4+7)/2+3(7+3)/2=11+15=26.两个子区间宽度分别为 2 和 3。
