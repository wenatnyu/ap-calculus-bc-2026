Determining Concavity of Functions over Their Domains

判断函数在定义域上的凹向性

f″(x)>0 ⟹ concave up

f″(x)<0 ⟹ concave down

Concavity tells whether tangent slopes are increasing or decreasing.凹向性描述切线斜率如何变化。

Two requirements

What Is an Inflection Point?

函数有定义，并且凹向改变

A point (c,f(c)) is an inflection point when f is defined at c and the graph changes concavity there.

Required: concave up ↔ concave down.

Not sufficient: merely f″(c)=0 or DNE.

Report an ordered pair when the question asks for an inflection point.

Defined point + change in concavity05

把“candidate x-value”和“inflection point”分开说。函数未定义的竖直渐近线不能是拐点。

f(x)=¼x⁴−6x²+x−3

f″(x)=3x²−12=3(x−2)(x+2)

An Inflection Can Occur Where f″ Does Not Exist

二阶导数不存在也可能发生凹向改变

Since f(0)=0 and concavity changes, (0,0) is an inflection point.定义成立的关键是凹向改变，不是 f″ 必须存在。

Official practice · Local geometry

Is the Tangent Line Above or Below?

凹向下时切线位于曲线上方

f(x)=xe−x

f″(x)=e−x(x−2)

f″(1)=−e−1<0

The graph is concave down near x=1, so the tangent line lies locally above the graph.凹向下像“帽子”，切线局部在图像上方。

Official practice · Local conclusion11

官方题型。此结论是 x=1 附近的局部比较，不应表述为整条切线在全定义域上都高于函数。
