Integrating Using Substitution

换元积分法

u=g(x)
du=g′(x)dx
reverse the chain rule

Substitution recognizes a composite function together with its inner derivative.换元法本质上是反向使用链式法则。

Topic 6.9 · Core lesson

Reverse the Chain Rule

反向链式法则

∫f(g(x))g′(x)dx

let u=g(x), du=g′(x)dx

=∫f(u)du

Choose u so the remaining integral becomes simpler.

Power Composite

幂函数复合

∫6x(x²+4)⁵dx

u=x²+4, du=2x dx

=3∫u⁵du=(1/2)u⁶+C

=(1/2)(x²+4)⁶+C

Account for the factor 3 after matching du.

Exponential Composite

指数复合

∫sin x·e^(cos x)dx

u=cos x, du=−sin x dx

=−eᵘ+C=−e^(cos x)+C

A missing negative sign is the main risk.

Logarithm Pattern

对数型换元

∫ cos x/(1+sin x) dx

u=1+sin x, du=cos x dx

=ln|1+sin x|+C

A derivative divided by its original expression produces a logarithm.

A Radical Pattern

根式换元

∫ x/√(x²+9) dx

u=x²+9, du=2x dx

=√(x²+9)+C

The factor one-half cancels when integrating u to the power −1/2.

Topic 6.9 · Core lesson

Definite Integral: Change Bounds

定积分换上下限

∫₀¹2x e^(x²)dx

u=x²: x=0→u=0, x=1→u=1

=∫₀¹eᵘdu=e−1

After changing bounds, stay in u; do not back-substitute.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 609

This is usually the cleanest definite-integral method.

Definite Integral: Back-Substitute

另一种定积分做法

find an x-antiderivative first

then evaluate at the original x-bounds

Use either changed u-bounds or original x-bounds, never a mixture.

Topic 6.9 · Core lesson

When u-Sub Does Not Fit

何时不适合换元

the derivative of the proposed u must be present up to a constant factor

if x remains after substitution, reconsider u or use another technique

Do not force a substitution that makes the integrand more complicated.

AP-style quick check

Quick Check

课堂即时检测

Evaluate ∫₀¹2x e^(x²)dx.

  1. Ae
  2. Be−1
  3. Ce²−1
  4. D1/e
Answer: B. Let u=x²; the bounds remain 0 and 1, giving ∫₀¹eᵘdu=e−1.定积分换元后同步更换上下限。

Exit Ticket

离堂检测

Find ∫6x(x²+4)⁵dx.

Answer: Let u=x²+4. Then 6x dx=3du, so the integral is (1/2)(x²+4)⁶+C.系数匹配后别忘了换回 x。
