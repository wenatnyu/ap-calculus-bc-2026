Core lesson

The Standard Logistic Equation

标准逻辑斯蒂方程

P′=kP(1−P/K),   k>0, K>0

P factor

More individuals can produce more growth.

1−P/K factor

Crowding reduces growth near capacity.

k 是早期相对增长参数，K 是容纳量。

PopulationSign of P′Direction

For positive initial data, K acts as a stable equilibrium.低于 K 向上靠近，高于 K 向下靠近。

Core lesson

Carrying Capacity Is a Limit

容纳量通常是极限

limt→∞P(t)=K

For a typical solution starting with 0<P₀<K, the population approaches K from below.

Over a finite time interval, K is usually an asymptotic supremum, not an attained maximum value.“趋近 K”不等于有限时刻“达到 K”。

Where Growth Rate Is Largest

增长率关于种群数量何时最大

R(P)=kP(1−P/K)

R′(P)=k(1−2P/K)=0 ⇒ P=K/2

Rmax=kK/4

This maximizes the rate as a function of P. It occurs at a future time only if the trajectory actually reaches K/2.若初值已不小于 K/2，t≥0 上最快时刻可能在起点。

Concavity and the Inflection Level

凹凸性与拐点高度

For a solution with 0<P₀<K:

Population levelGrowth-rate trendShape
0<P<K/2P′ increasingconcave up
K/2<P<KP′ decreasingconcave down

A trajectory has an inflection only if it passes through P=K/2 in the time interval considered.K/2 是拐点高度，不保证在 t≥0 出现。

Recognize the Alternative Form

识别另一种参数形式

P′=aP−bP²=P(a−bP),   a,b>0

K=a/b

P at maximum rate = a/(2b)=K/2

Rmax=a²/(4b)

In aP(K−P), the coefficient a is not the same parameter as k in kP(1−P/K).先因式分解，再确认参数约定。

Calibrate k from One Observed Rate

用一次观测求 k

Capacity K=2500. When P=1200, the rate is 100 people/hour.

100=k(1200)(1−1200/2500)

k=25/156≈0.1603 hour⁻¹

Substitute the population level and its simultaneous rate into the differential equation.数量与变化率必须来自同一时刻。

Core lesson

The Explicit Logistic Solution

显式逻辑斯蒂解

P(t)=K/(1+Ae−kt)

When t=0 and P(0)=P₀:

A=(K−P₀)/P₀

For 0<P₀<K, A>0 and the denominator decreases toward 1.初值决定水平位置，不改变容纳量。

A Symmetric Starting Point

从 K/2 开始的模型

P′=0.5P(1−P/200),   P(0)=100

A=(200−100)/100=1

P(t)=200/(1+e−0.5t)

The solution begins at its inflection level, so the forward-time growth rate is largest at t=0.此例初始点正好是 K/2。

Read Capacity and Maximum Rate

读出容纳量与最大增长率

P′=(1/1000)P(600−P). Find the carrying capacity and maximum rate as a function of P.

Answer: K=600. The rate is largest at P=300, where P′=(1/1000)(300)(300)=90 units/time.此形式的前系数不是标准式中的 k。

Factor Before Interpreting

先因式分解再解释

P′=(1/5)P−(1/2000)P². Find K, the inflection level, and the maximum rate as a function of P.

Answer: P′=0.2P(1−P/400), so K=400, inflection level P=200, and Rmax=0.2(400)/4=20 units/time.展开式先还原成标准式。
