Directions: Combine simultaneous sign conditions and motion vocabulary.
要求：结合一阶、二阶导数符号判断函数行为与运动状态。

Fast check: “And” means intersect intervals; speeding up means velocity and acceleration have the same sign.

1. For f(x)=x³−3x², where is f both decreasing and concave up?3 points
Intersect intervals / 区间取交集：
Answer: f′=3x(x−2)<0 on (0,2), while f″=6(x−1)>0 on (1,∞). Therefore the answer is (1,2).
2. A particle has v(t)=(t−2)(t−5) and a(t)=2t−7 for t>0. Where is it speeding up?3 points
Compare signs / 比较符号：
Answer: Velocity and acceleration have the same sign on (2,7/2)∪(5,∞).
3. At equally spaced inputs 0,1,2,3, a function has values 0,1,3,6. What monotonicity and concavity do the data support?4 points
First differences / 一阶差分：
Answer: The outputs rise, and the successive differences 1,2,3 rise. The table supports an increasing, concave-up function, assuming the relevant derivative signs do not change between entries.

  • Q3: 1 monotonicity, 1 differences, 2 qualified concavity.
