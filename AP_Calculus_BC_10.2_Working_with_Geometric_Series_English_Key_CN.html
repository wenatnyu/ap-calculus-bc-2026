Working with Geometric Series

等比级数

Σn=0∞arn=a/(1-r), provided |r|<1.

A geometric series is controlled by its first included term and common ratio. / 等比级数由首项和公比决定。

Recognize the Pattern

识别等比结构

a+ar+ar2+...=Σn=0∞arn

Each term is obtained by multiplying the previous term by the common ratio r.

Core lesson

Finite Geometric Sum

有限等比级数求和

SN=a(1-rN)/(1-r), r≠1

Here a is the first included term and N is the number of included terms.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1004

Core lesson

Infinite Geometric Sum

无穷等比级数求和

Σn=0∞arn=a/(1-r) when |r|<1.

If |r|≥1 and a≠0, the series diverges.

Core lesson

Why the Formula Works

公式由来

When |r|<1, rN→0 in the finite-sum formula.

lim SN=lim a(1-rN)/(1-r)=a/(1-r).

The Starting Index Matters

起始指标会改变首项

Σn=1∞3(1/2)n

The first term is 3/2, not 3; the sum is (3/2)/(1-1/2)=3.

Negative Common Ratio

负公比

Σn=0∞4(-1/2)n=4/(1+1/2)=8/3.

The terms alternate, but |r|<1 makes their magnitudes shrink.

Repeating Decimals

循环小数转分数

0.272727...=0.27+0.0027+...

a=27/100, r=1/100, sum=(27/100)/(99/100)=3/11.

Core lesson

A Bouncing Model

反弹模型

A ball dropped h meters and rebounding at ratio r travels h+2hr+2hr2+... .

Total distance=h+2hr/(1-r), 0<r<1.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1010

The initial drop occurs once; every rebound height after that is traveled up and down.

Solve for a Parameter

由级数和求参数

If Σn=0∞krn=M and |r|<1, then k=M(1-r).

Check that the resulting first term and ratio match the original indexing.

Core lesson

When Geometric Series Diverge

等比级数何时发散

  • |r|>1: term magnitudes grow.
  • r=1: nonzero terms stay constant.
  • r=-1: partial sums oscillate.
FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1012

For a nonzero first term, all three cases fail the necessary term condition.

Core lesson

Geometric Checklist

等比级数检查表

  1. Write the first included term a.
  2. Find r.
  3. Check |r|<1.
  4. Use a/(1-r), or state divergence.

Quick Check
