Directions: Use the sign of the second derivative and verify every concavity change.
要求：根据二阶导数符号判断，并验证凹向是否改变。

Fast check: f″(c)=0 identifies only a candidate; an inflection point requires a concavity change and a defined point.

1. Find the concavity intervals and inflection point of f(x)=x³−3x².3 points
Second derivative sign / 二阶导数符号：
Answer: f″=6x−6. Concave down on (−∞,1), concave up on (1,∞); inflection point (1,−2).
2. For g(x)=x⁴, g″(0)=0. Is (0,0) an inflection point? Explain.3 points
Sign on both sides / 两侧符号：
Answer: No. g″(x)=12x²>0 on both sides of 0, so the graph remains concave up.
3. Near x=a, suppose f″(x)>0. Does the tangent line at a lie locally above or below the graph?4 points
Conclusion and reason / 结论与理由：
Answer: Below the graph. Positive second derivative means the graph is concave up, so nearby graph values lie above the tangent line.
