Sigma Notation

求和符号

Σᵢ₌₁ⁿ aᵢ = a₁+a₂+⋯+aₙ

Σ(caᵢ+dbᵢ)=cΣaᵢ+dΣbᵢ

The index changes term by term; n tells how many terms appear.

General Riemann Sum

一般黎曼和

partition a=x₀<x₁<⋯<xₙ=b

Δxᵢ=xᵢ−xᵢ₋₁

Σᵢ₌₁ⁿ f(xᵢ*)Δxᵢ

The sample point xᵢ* lies inside the ith subinterval.

Equal-Width Right Sum

Topic 6.3 · Core lesson

The Limit Definition

定积分的极限定义

∫ₐᵇ f(x)dx = limmax Δxᵢ→0 Σᵢ₌₁ⁿ f(xᵢ*)Δxᵢ

provided the limit exists

For continuous f, the limit exists and does not depend on the sample-point rule.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 606

For arbitrary partitions, the mesh max Δx_i must approach zero. With equal subintervals, this is equivalent to n approaching infinity.

Geometry and Sign

Quick Check

Rewrite limₙ→∞(3/n)Σᵢ₌₁ⁿ[1+(3i/n)²] as an integral.

  1. A∫₀³(1+x²)dx
  2. B∫₁³x²dx

Explain the meaning and units of ∫₀⁵ r(t)dt when r is measured in people per hour and t in hours.
