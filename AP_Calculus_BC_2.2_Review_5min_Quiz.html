Fast check: A derivative is a limit of average rates. In context, include “at that instant” and output-units per input-unit.

1. A runner's distance is d(t) meters, where t is seconds. Interpret d′(4)=6 in one sentence.2 points
Sentence / 完整句：
Answer: At t=4 seconds, the runner's distance from the reference point is increasing at 6 m/s. 第 4 秒这一瞬间，距离以 6 米/秒增加。
2. Use the definition f′(x)=limh→0[f(x+h)−f(x)]/h to find the derivative of f(x)=x²−3x.4 points
Limit setup and algebra / 极限式与化简：
Answer: f′(x)=limh→0{[(x+h)²−3(x+h)]−(x²−3x)}/h = limh→0(2x+h−3)=2x−3.
3. Given f(2)=5 and f′(2)=−3, write an equation of the line tangent to y=f(x) at x=2.4 points
Point + slope → tangent line / 点与斜率：
Answer: The point is (2,5) and the slope is −3, so y−5=−3(x−2) (equivalently, y=−3x+11).
