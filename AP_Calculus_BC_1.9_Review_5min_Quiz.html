Directions: Use the Squeeze Theorem. State the two outside limits before giving the middle limit.
要求：使用夹逼定理；先写出上下界的极限，再写中间函数的极限。

Fast check: students must verify that both outside functions approach the same number. An inequality by itself is not enough.

1. Suppose g(x) ≤ f(x) ≤ h(x) near x=a, and limx→ag(x)=2=limx→ah(x). Find limx→af(x). 2 points
Conclusion / 结论：
Answer: 2. The middle function is squeezed between two functions whose limits are both 2. 上下界极限相同，所以中间函数的极限也是 2。
2. Evaluate limx→0x² cos(1/x²). Write a useful inequality. 4 points
Bounds and limit / 上下界与极限：
Answer: 0. Since −1≤cos(1/x²)≤1 and x²≥0, −x²≤x²cos(1/x²)≤x². Both outside limits are 0, so the middle limit is 0. 注意乘以非负的 x²，不等号方向不变。
3. Near x=2, a function satisfies −2−(x−2)² ≤ f(x) ≤ −2+(x−2)². Find limx→2f(x) and justify. 4 points
Outside limits / 上下界极限：
Answer: −2. Since (x−2)²→0, both outside functions −2−(x−2)² and −2+(x−2)² approach −2. Therefore the Squeeze Theorem gives limx→2f(x)=−2. 上下界都趋近 −2，所以中间函数也趋近 −2。

Scoring / 评分

  • Q1: 1 point for the theorem condition; 1 for 2.
  • Q2: 2 for correct bounds; 1 for outside limits; 1 for 0.
  • Q3: 2 for evaluating both bounds; 1 for citing the theorem; 1 for −2.
