Secant slope · 割线斜率

时间窗一换，平均变化率也会换

Change the time interval, and the average rate changes.

D(6) − D(2)6 − 2 ≈ −65 m/min

负号：这段时间总体在靠近家。Negative means he moved closer to home overall.

D(3) − D(2)3 − 2 ≈ +60 m/min

正号：这段时间总体在远离家。Positive means he moved farther from home overall.

割线连接曲线上的两个点；它的斜率就是这段区间的平均变化率。A secant line joins two points on a curve. Its slope is the average rate of change over that interval.

来源 / Source: FlippedMath 1.1 Video06

讲法：先只比较正负，再估大小。原视频列出 [2,6]、[2,3] 两个商，不强求精确读数；这里的 -65 与 +60 来自对原图的近似重绘。

The key move · 关键一步

我们要的是“趋近”，
不是“直接等于”。

We want “closer and closer,” not “exactly equal.”

D(2) − D(2)2 − 2 = 00

两个时刻相同，分母为 0，所以差商无定义。The two times are the same, so the denominator is 0 and the difference quotient is undefined.

D(2) − D(1.999)2 − 1.999

仍然是两个不同的时刻，但间隔已经非常短。These are still two different times, but the interval is tiny.
今天先建立直觉；下一课再写极限记号 · Build the idea today; use limit notation next class.08

关键停顿：让学生说清楚“1.999 不是 2”。差商仍是区间平均，只是区间很短，因此可以估计瞬时变化率。

Practice 1 · 先画切线 / Draw the tangent first

频道增长越来越快：w = 10 时有多快？

The channel is growing faster and faster. How fast at w = 10?

频道浏览量 / Channel views，v(w)

  1. 在 w=10 处画一条切线。Draw a tangent line at w=10.
  2. 在切线上选两个好读的点。Choose two easy-to-read points on the tangent.
  3. 计算斜率，并带上单位。Calculate the slope and include units.
粗略估计：10 views/week。曲线凹向上，表示每周新增浏览量还在变大。Rough estimate: 10 views/week. The graph is concave up, so the weekly gain in views is still increasing.
