Fast check: A critical number is only a candidate; verify the required maximum or minimum.

1. Two positive numbers have sum 20. Find the pair with greatest product.3 points
Objective and verification / 目标函数与验证：
Answer: P=x(20−x); P′=20−2x=0 at x=10. Since P″=−2<0, this gives the greatest product. The numbers are 10 and 10.
2. A pen beside a wall uses 120 m of fence for the other three sides. Find the dimensions of greatest area.3 points
Constraint, area, verification / 约束、面积与验证：
Answer: Let x be each perpendicular side. Then A=x(120−2x); A′=120−4x=0 gives x=30. Since A″=−4<0, the maximum dimensions are 30 m by 60 m.
3. A closed cylinder has volume 54π. Find the radius and height minimizing surface area.4 points
One-variable surface area / 一元表面积：
Answer: h=54/r² and A=2πr²+108π/r. Solving A′=4πr−108π/r²=0 gives r=3, h=6. Since A″=4π+216π/r³>0 for r>0, this is the minimum.
