Intermediate ValueTheorem (IVT)

介值定理

continuous on [a,b]
k between f(a) and f(b)
⇒ there exists c with f(c)=k
A continuous graph cannot skip a height between its endpoint heights

The theorem · Conditions before conclusion

The Intermediate Value Theorem

先核对条件，再写存在性结论

If f is continuous on [a,b] and k lies between f(a) and f(b),

then there exists at least one c∈[a,b] such that f(c)=k.

When k is strictly between the two endpoint values, the guaranteed point satisfies c∈(a,b).若 k 严格位于两端值之间，则 c 在开区间内。

IVT guarantees existence, not uniqueness.IVT 只保证存在，不保证唯一。

Continuity · Between · Exists03

完整朗读定理。若 k 恰等于端点值，则端点本身可以作为 c；课堂常见题中 k 严格介于两端值，所以结论写 c∈(a,b)。

Official notes · A four-line proof frame

A Reliable IVT Justification

一套可直接用于 AP 书面表达的论证模板

01

Continuity

f is continuous on [a,b].

写清闭区间。
02

Endpoints

Compute f(a) and f(b).

计算端点值。
03

Between

k lies between those values.

说明目标值在中间。
04

Conclusion

Therefore ∃ c with f(c)=k.

结论写“至少存在”。

Do not stop after “IVT applies.” State the value produced by the theorem.必须把 c 与 f(c)=k 写出来。

A named theorem still needs its hypotheses and conclusion.04

要求学生每次都写四行，尤其不能省略 continuous on [a,b]。用 ∃ 可简写 there exists，但考试中完整英文更稳妥。

Official practice 13 · Polynomial

Example: A Guaranteed Zero

多项式连续，端点值跨过 0

f(x)=3−x² on [−2,1],   k=0

f(−2)=−1,   f(1)=2

−1<0<2

Write the full conclusion

f is a polynomial, so it is continuous on [−2,1].

There exists at least one c∈(−2,1) such that f(c)=0.区间内至少存在一个零点。

Notice: solving the equation is not required to make the guarantee.05

先用多项式说明连续，再计算端点值。IVT 的任务是保证解存在，不要求本题直接解出 c；事实上根为 ±√3，区间内只有 −√3，但“唯一”来自额外代数信息，不来自 IVT。

Official practice 14 · No guarantee

When k Is Not Between the Endpoints

函数连续还不够：目标值必须位于两端值之间

g(x)=1/x on [2,5],   k=−1

g(2)=1/2,   g(5)=1/5

−1 is not between 1/5 and 1/2.

What may we say?

IVT does not apply to k=−1

No guarantee is the theorem-level conclusion. Here we can also see directly that 1/x>0 on [2,5], so g(x)=−1 has no solution there.目标值不在端点值之间。

Continuous + target outside the endpoint range ⇒ IVT gives no conclusion.06

区分 “IVT does not apply” 与 “equation has no solution”。前者只说定理不能保证；本题还可由 1/x 为正进一步证明无解。

A sign change is not enough

The Discontinuity Trap

端点异号不等于一定有零点

g(x)=1/x on [−1,1]

g(−1)=−1,   g(1)=1

g is undefined at x=0.

Continuity fails

The graph jumps through a vertical asymptote instead of crossing the x-axis.

IVT cannot be used. In fact, 1/x≠0 for every point in its domain.区间内部在 x=0 处断开。

Always check continuity before reading a sign change as a guaranteed zero.07

这是最常见的错误：只看端点一正一负。让学生先圈出闭区间，再检查区间内是否有洞、跳跃或竖直渐近线。

Confirm continuity

Scan neighbors

04

Interpret the word guarantee

Existence Does Not Mean Uniqueness

“至少一次”不等于“恰好一次”

What IVT says

The endpoint heights lie on opposite sides of k, so at least one crossing must occur.

What IVT does not say

The curve may cross the same level several times.

Never conclude “exactly one” from IVT alone.唯一性需要单调性等额外信息。

Existence is a lower-bound statement.11

图中同一水平线穿过三次，说明 IVT 的存在结论和唯一性无关。若以后知道函数严格单调，才可能加上唯一性。

Official test prep 17 · Fixed point

Transform the Goal into a Zero

把 f(c)=c 改写成辅助函数的零点

f is continuous, f(1)=7, f(7)=1

Define g(x)=f(x)−x.

g(1)=6,   g(7)=−6

Apply IVT to g

g is continuous on [1,7], and 0 lies between 6 and −6.

There exists c∈(1,7) with g(c)=0; therefore f(c)=c.辅助函数有零点，所以原函数有不动点。

Turn an equation into “continuous function = 0.”12

这是 AP 常用策略：目标是 f(c)=c，就定义 g=f−x；目标是 f(c)=q(c)，就定义 g=f−q。然后用端点异号保证 g 的零点。

Official test prep 18 · Multiple choice

Force Two Intersections

怎样保证 f 与 y=1 至少相交两次？

The function f is continuous on [1,3].

x123
f(x)2k3
  • A.k=0
  • B.k=1
  • C.k=2
  • D.k=3
A · k=0. Then f(1)=2>1, f(2)=0<1, and f(3)=3>1. IVT forces one crossing in (1,2) and another in (2,3).两个互不重叠的区间各强制一个交点。
Two disjoint crossing intervals ⇒ at least two distinct solutions.13

官方答案 A。k=1 只给出 x=2 一个明确交点，不能保证两个；k=0 则在两侧都跨过水平线 y=1。

Official test prep 19 · Always true

Choose the Strongest Guaranteed Claim

区分“至少”与“恰好”

The function f is continuous on [0,4].

x01234
f(x)120−33
  • A.exactly one zero
  • B.more than one zero
  • C.more than two zeros
  • D.exactly two zeros
B · more than one zero. One zero is already listed at x=2; a different zero is forced in (3,4). Extra zeros remain possible, so “exactly two” is too strong.至少两个，但不能断言恰好两个。
“Always true” means choose only what the data force.14

官方答案 B。x=2 本身是零点；f(3) 与 f(4) 异号，再保证一个不同零点。由于其他区间可能额外交叉，不能选 exactly two。
