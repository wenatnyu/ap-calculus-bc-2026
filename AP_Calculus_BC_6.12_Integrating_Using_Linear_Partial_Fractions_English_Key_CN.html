factor Q into distinct linear factors

AP-style quick check

Quick Check

课堂即时检测

Decompose 1/[(x−1)(x+2)].

  1. A1/(x−1)+1/(x+2)
  2. B(1/3)/(x−1)−(1/3)/(x+2)
  3. C−1/(x−1)+1/(x+2)
  4. D(1/2)/(x−1)+(1/2)/(x+2)
Answer: B. Substituting x=1 gives A=1/3; x=−2 gives B=−1/3.令 x 等于两个根可快速求系数。

Exit ticket · One clean solution

Exit Ticket

离堂检测

Integrate (2x+3)/(x²+x−2).

Answer: Factor (x+2)(x−1). The decomposition is (1/3)/(x+2)+(5/3)/(x−1), so the integral is (1/3)ln|x+2|+(5/3)ln|x−1|+C.先完全因式分解，再求待定系数。
