Fast check: Check outer-versus-inner distance, subtraction of areas, and slice orientation.

1. State the cross-sectional area of a washer with outer radius R and inner radius r.3 points
Washer area / 垫圈面积
Answer: A=π(R²−r²).
2. Rotate 1≤y≤x+1, 0≤x≤2 about the x-axis. Find the volume.3 points
x-axis washers / 绕 x 轴垫圈
Answer: V=π∫₀²[(x+1)²−1]dx=20π/3.
3. Rotate 1≤x≤y+2, 0≤y≤1 about the y-axis. Find the volume.4 points
y-axis washers / 绕 y 轴垫圈
Answer: V=π∫₀¹[(y+2)²−1]dy=16π/3.

Scoring / 评分

  • Q3: horizontal slices, dy bounds, exact volume.
  • Volume answers use cubic units.
