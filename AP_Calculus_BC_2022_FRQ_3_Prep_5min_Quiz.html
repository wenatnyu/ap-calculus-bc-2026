要求：5 分钟。禁止计算器。写精确值与理由。满分 10 分。

Fast check: Checks signed-area net change, concavity from f′, and a transformed derivative.

1. Suppose f(2)=5 and the signed area under f′ from x=2 to x=6 is −3. Find f(6).3 points
Use net change: final = initial + integral. / 净变化：终值 = 初值 + 积分。
Answer: f(6)=5+∫₂⁶f′(x)dx=5−3=2.
2. The graph of f′ changes from decreasing to increasing at x=1, then from increasing to decreasing at x=4. Give the inflection-point x-coordinates of f with a reason.3 points
Concavity of f follows the trend of f′. / f 的凹凸性由 f′ 的增减决定。
Answer:

x=1,4, because f′ changes monotonicity at both values, so f changes concavity.

3. Let g(x)=f(x)−2x. On interval I the graph shows f′(x)<2, and on interval J it shows f′(x)>2. State where g decreases and justify.4 points
Differentiate g and compare f′ with 2. / 求 g′，把 f′ 与 2 比较。
Answer:

g′(x)=f′(x)−2. Therefore g decreases on I, where g′<0, and increases on J.
