Distance-squared method

Closest Point to (2, 1/2)

抛物线上到定点最近的点

point: (x,x²)

D²=(x−2)²+(x²−1/2)²

(D²)′=4x³−4

x=1

closest point: (1,1)

Minimizing D² avoids a square-root derivative and gives the same minimizing point.最终答案要写坐标点。

Discard infeasible roots

Maximize Box Volume

无盖盒体积最大

V=x(16−2x)(30−2x)=4x³−92x²+480x

V′=4(3x²−46x+120)=0

x=10/3 or 12; feasible domain: 0<x<8

cut size = 10/3 inches

x=12 is algebraically valid but geometrically impossible.短边 16 决定 x<8。

Quadratic optimization

Maximum Area beside a Wall

靠墙围栏面积最大

2x+y=120

A=x(120−2x)

A′=120−4x=0

x=30, y=60

dimensions: 30 by 60

A′ changes from positive to negative, so the candidate is a maximum.

Justification choices

Three Valid Verification Methods

三种常用最优性验证

Candidate test

Compare the objective at all critical points and endpoints.

First derivative

Show the objective increases then decreases—or the reverse—across the feasible domain.

Second derivative + global check

Classify the local extremum, then use the domain, uniqueness/end behavior, or all candidates to prove it is absolute.

“My calculator shows it” is not a complete justification.需说明候选值为何是全局或局部最优。

AP-style optimization

Which Candidate Is Feasible?

筛选可行临界点

V(x)=x(16−2x)2,   0<x<8

The equation V′(x)=0 gives x=8/3 and x=8. Which value can maximize the box volume?

  1. A8/3 only
  2. B8 only
  3. Cboth
  4. Dneither
Answer: A. Only 8/3 lies in the feasible domain (0,8); x=8 collapses the base.x=8 会使底面边长 16−2x 等于零。

Exit ticket · Full solution

Maximize a Product

完成一个最优化论证

Two positive numbers have sum 20. Find the numbers whose product is greatest, and justify.

Answer: Let P=x(20−x) for 0<x<20. Then P′=20−2x=0 at x=10, and P′′=−2<0. The numbers are 10 and 10.写出模型、定义域、导数、验证和最终两个数。
