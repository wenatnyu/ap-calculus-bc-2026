Directions: Use signs, equilibria, and local directions to describe solution behavior.
要求：利用斜率正负、平衡解与局部方向推理。

Fast check: Follow one solution trajectory; do not compare unrelated segments.

1. For y′=y−2x, is the solution through (0,1) increasing or decreasing there?3 points
Slope and behavior / 斜率与变化：
Answer: y′=1−0=1>0, so the solution is increasing at that point.
2. Find the equilibrium solution of y′=0.5y and explain its field pattern.3 points
Equilibrium and pattern / 平衡解与图案：
Answer: y=0. Along the x-axis every segment is horizontal because y′=0.
3. For y′=y²(x−1), describe all points where slopes are nonnegative.4 points
Inequality and boundary / 不等式与边界：
Answer: Slopes are nonnegative for x≥1, together with every point where y=0.
