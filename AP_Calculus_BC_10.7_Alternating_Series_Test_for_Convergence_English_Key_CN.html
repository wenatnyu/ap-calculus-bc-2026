Alternating Series Test for Convergence

交错级数判别法

bn↓0 ⇒ Σ(-1)nbn converges.

Alternating cancellation works when magnitudes eventually decrease to zero. / 项的绝对值最终递减到零时，交错抵消可保证收敛。

Core lesson

Alternating Series Form

交错级数形式

Σ(-1)nbn or Σ(-1)n-1bn, with bn≥0.

The factor (-1)n controls the sign; bn is the magnitude.

Core lesson

Alternating Series Test

交错级数判别法

If bn is eventually decreasing and lim bn=0, then Σ(-1)nbn converges.

Both conditions are required.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1004

Use 'eventually' precisely: finitely many early increases do not affect convergence.

Core lesson

Condition 1: Tends to Zero

条件一：趋于零

limn→∞bn=0.

If this fails, the original terms do not approach zero and the series diverges by the nth-term test.

Condition 2: Decreasing

条件二：递减

bn+1≤bn eventually.

You may prove this by algebra, a ratio, or a decreasing continuous extension.

Alternating Harmonic Series

交错调和级数

Σn=1∞(-1)n-1/n

Here bn=1/n decreases to zero, so the series converges.

Fractional-Power Example

分数次幂示例

Σ(-1)n-1/n2/3

bn=n-2/3 decreases to zero, so the alternating series converges.

Core lesson

Proving Decrease with a Function

用函数证明递减

If bn=f(n) and f'(x)<0 for x≥N, then bn decreases for n≥N.

Also verify f(n)≥0.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1009

Differentiability is a tool for monotonicity, not a separate AST requirement.

When the Term Limit Fails

通项极限失败

Σ(-1)nn/(n+1)

The magnitude tends to 1, not 0; therefore the series diverges.

Core lesson

AST Proves Convergence Only

AST 只证明级数收敛

The alternating series test proves that the signed series converges.

It does not by itself decide whether convergence is absolute or conditional.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1011

Core lesson

Not Every Sign Change Is Alternating

变号不一定是交错

An alternating series has a regular plus-minus pattern.

Irregularly changing signs require another argument; do not force them into (-1)nbn.

FlippedMath Version #1 · Unit 1012

Focus on the actual sign pattern, not just the presence of some negative terms.

Core lesson

AST Workflow

交错级数判别流程

  1. Write the series as Σ(±)bn.
  2. Check bn≥0.
  3. Show bn→0.
  4. Show bn eventually decreases.
  5. Conclude convergence.

Exit Ticket
