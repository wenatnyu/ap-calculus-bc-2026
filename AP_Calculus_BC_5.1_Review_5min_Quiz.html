Fast check: L’Hôpital’s Rule applies directly only to quotient limits with a verified 0/0 or ∞/∞ form.

1. Evaluate limx→0 sin(6x)/x. State the indeterminate form.3 points
Form and work / 形式与过程：
Answer: Substitution gives 0/0. L’Hôpital gives limx→06cos(6x)=6.
2. Evaluate limx→0(1−cos x)/x². Explain why the rule is used twice.3 points
Two form checks / 两次形式检查：
Answer: The first form is 0/0. One application gives sin x/(2x), still 0/0. A second application gives cos x/2→1/2.
3. May L’Hôpital’s Rule be used directly on limx→3(2x−5)/x? Evaluate the limit.4 points
Decision and value / 判断与结果：
Answer: No. Substitution gives the determinate value 1/3, not 0/0 or ∞/∞. The limit is 1/3.
