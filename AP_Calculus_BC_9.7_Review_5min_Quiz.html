Fast check: Check displacement versus distance and the same-time condition for collision.

1. For r(t)=⟨t²,3t⟩, find the displacement from t=1 to t=2.3 points
Displacement / 位移
Answer: r(2)−r(1)=⟨4,6⟩−⟨1,3⟩=⟨3,3⟩.
2. A particle has constant velocity v=⟨3,4⟩ on [0,5]. Find its total distance traveled.3 points
Distance / 路程
Answer: Speed=‖v‖=5, so distance=∫₀⁵5dt=25.
3. Do r₁(t)=⟨t,t²⟩ and r₂(t)=⟨2−t,2t−1⟩ collide for t≥0? Give the time and point or justify why not.4 points
Collision / 碰撞
Answer: Yes. Both coordinates agree at t=1, so the collision point is (1,1).
