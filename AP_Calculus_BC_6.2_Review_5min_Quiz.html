1. A pump adds water at 7 liters per minute for 6 minutes. Find the accumulated change and its units.3 points
Calculation / 计算：
Answer: 7·6=42 liters.
2. A rate is 4 units/hour for 3 hours and −2 units/hour for the next 2 hours. Find the net change.3 points
Signed areas / 有向面积：
Answer: 4(3)−2(2)=8 units.
3. A particle has velocity 3 m/s for 4 s, then −2 m/s for 3 s. Find displacement and distance traveled.4 points
Compare / 比较：
Answer: Displacement=12−6=6 m; distance=12+6=18 m.
