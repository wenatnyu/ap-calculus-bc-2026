1. State the three tail conditions on f required by the integral test.3 points
Hypotheses / 前提
Answer: f must be continuous, positive, and decreasing on [N,∞) for some N, with f(n)=an.
2. Use the integral test to classify Σn=1∞1/(n2+4).3 points
Convergence / 收敛
Answer: f(x)=1/(x^2+4) is continuous, positive, and decreasing; its improper integral is finite, so the series converges.
3. Use the integral test to classify Σn=1∞n/(n2+1).4 points
Divergence / 发散
Answer: The hypotheses hold on the tail, and ∫x/(x^2+1)dx=(1/2)ln(x^2+1), whose improper limit is infinite. The series diverges.
