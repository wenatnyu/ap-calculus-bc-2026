Interpreting the Meaning of the Derivative in Context

在情境中解释导数的含义

A derivative is a number with a time, a direction, and units.

f′(a)=b

At input a, the quantity f is changing at b output-units per input-unit.

AP Calculus BC · 4.101

开场先要求学生说完整句子：输入时刻、变化对象、快慢、单位缺一不可。

The Interpretation Sentence

导数解释句型

f′(a)=b

At x=a, the quantity f is changing at b output-units per input-unit.

Use “at,” not “over.” A derivative describes one instant.导数描述某个瞬间，不是整个区间。

AP Calculus BC · 4.103

让学生把 at、quantity、changing、units 四部分圈出来。

Units Are a Quotient

导数单位是两个单位的商

units of f′ = units of funits of x

If H(t) is gallons and t is minutes:

H′(t): gallons/minute

H″(t): gallons/minute²

AP Calculus BC · 4.104

单位先从函数和自变量读出，再相除；不要只写数字。

The Sign Tells Direction

导数正负决定变化方向

f′>0

Increasing

Output is rising.函数值在增加。

f′<0

Decreasing

Output is falling.函数值在减少。

f′=0

Instantaneously flat

No first-order change at that instant.该瞬间一阶变化率为零。

A negative derivative does not mean the quantity itself is negative.

AP Calculus BC · 4.105

区分 quantity is negative 和 quantity is decreasing。

Function Value vs. Derivative Value

函数值与导数值

StatementMeaning
p(10)=1After 10 weeks, 1 packet has been completed.
p′(39)=4At week 39, packets are being completed at 4 packets per week.

The function gives an amount; its derivative gives a rate.函数给“量”，导数给“变化率”。

AP Calculus BC · 4.106

使用官方 packet 的 packet-completion 情境，强调 p 与 p′ 主语不同。

When the Function Is Already a Rate

当原函数本身就是变化率

b(t) = buying rate, measured in cards/year

b(3)=150 cards/year

b′(4)=10 cards/year²

b′(4)=10 means the buying rate is increasing by 10 cards/year each year.这是“速率的变化率”。

AP Calculus BC · 4.107

官方买卡例子适合练习复合单位 cards/year/year。

Context Example: Water Tank

水箱情境

H(t) = water in a tank, in gallons

H′(5)=−3

At 5 minutes, the amount of water is decreasing at 3 gallons per minute.

第 5 分钟时，水量正以每分钟 3 加仑的速度减少。

Do not say “the tank contains −3 gallons.”

AP Calculus BC · 4.108

让学生说明负号进入动词 decreasing，而不是数量本身。

A Derivative of a Derivative

二阶导数的情境含义

R′(2)=−12 items/hour²

At hour 2, the production rate is decreasing by 12 items per hour each hour.

第 2 小时时，生产速率每小时下降 12 件/小时。

Spot the Incomplete Interpretation

找出不完整的解释

A student writes: “P′(8)=15 means the population changes by 15.” What is missing?

Missing: the instant, direction, and units. Better: At year 8, the population is increasing at 15 people per year.缺少时刻、方向和单位。
AP Calculus BC · 4.112

要求学生指出具体缺失内容，而不只说“写得不完整”。

Multiple-Choice Check

选择题检查

C(t) is measured in customers/hour. Which units belong to C′(t)?

  • A.customers
  • B.customers/hour
  • C.customers/hour²
  • D.hours/customer
C. Differentiating a rate with respect to hours introduces another “per hour.”对“每小时顾客数”再对小时求导，单位为 customers/hour²。
