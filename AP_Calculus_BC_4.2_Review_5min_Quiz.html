Directions: Write complete contextual sentences. Each interpretation needs an instant, direction, and units.
要求：用完整情境句作答；写明时刻、方向和单位。

Fast check: A derivative is a rate at one instant; its units are output-units per input-unit.

1. H(t) is water in a tank, in gallons, t minutes after noon. Interpret H′(5)=−3.3 points
Complete sentence:
Answer: At 5 minutes after noon, the water amount is decreasing at 3 gallons per minute. 正午后第5分钟，水量每分钟减少3加仑。
2. R(t) is a buying rate in cards/year, and t is measured in years. Interpret R′(2)=12.3 points
Rate-of-a-rate sentence:
Answer: At year 2, the buying rate is increasing by 12 cards per year each year, or 12 cards/year². 第2年时，购买速率每年增加12张/年。
3. Temperature T(h) satisfies T′(6)>0 and T″(6)<0. Describe what is happening at hour 6.4 points
Two-part description:
Answer: The temperature is increasing, but its rate of increase is decreasing. 温度正在上升，但上升速度在减慢。

Scoring

  • Q1: 1 for instant; 1 for direction; 1 for units.
  • Q2: 1 for rate as subject; 1 for increasing; 1 for cards/year².
  • Q3: 2 for increasing temperature; 2 for decreasing rate of increase.
  • Equivalent correct work earns full credit.
